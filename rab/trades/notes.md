## 1. What a note must do

- **Official test** (Competition Guide p.3): a note captures "the reasoning behind the decision, including its alignment with your strategy, the supporting research or analysis, and its expected role in growth, liquidity, risk management, or future funding." The TN Analysis later quotes three notes "exactly as it appears in WInS" and asks how each decision "aligned with, tested, or refined" the strategy (Trading Note Instruction).
- **Content, in the writer's own order:** the role word (growth, liquidity, risk management or future funding) + what is bought and which of Laura's needs it serves + the research or check behind it + the honest limit. About 45 words. The exemplars open in several ways on purpose: vary yours too (s8 checks it).
- **The box:** 300 characters maximum (maxlength, SEEN 29 Sep). The kit limit is **285**, which keeps 15 characters for a WInS name string longer than ours; a note with no security name left to swap in (IBTR and IBTR_F name none; IBTM and VT were SEEN in WInS) may run to **295**. Plain ASCII (straight quotes; no dashes, `~` or `<=`), one paragraph. Whether a saved note can be edited is UNVERIFIED: treat the first save as final.
- **The Guide's own example note** (Trading Note Instruction p.2) is 413 characters, so it would not fit the WInS box, and it would be true of any client. Use it for tone only; `note_check.py` warns if a draft echoes it.
- **Laura-specific:** name which of her ten payments, the floor, the residency or co-sponsors. A date alone could fit any client, so the checker no longer accepts a year as the Laura anchor. A note that may be featured must also name the residency, co-sponsors or which of her ten payments (`note_check.py --pick`).
- **One Friday fact, with room for it:** each note carries one thing only WInS or the day showed: the exact WInS name string, the dated check ('Its 1 Oct WInS price passed our curve check': on Friday morning WInS shows Thursday's close), the curve or premium date ('at 1 Oct yields', 'At the 1 Oct close'), or the Preview price as the note's one number. Each brief names its slot. A dated share price is the one WInS dollar figure a note may carry, and it must be the price WInS shows that day: the checker fails the kit's 28 Sep reference prices ($21.76 and the rest).
- **Numbers:** at most one analytic number, from `numbers.yaml` or a named, dated source. **A number written in words counts too**, and the checker now catches it: a quarter, fifth or third of, half of, percentage point, about the same, almost exactly, most of, twice. Dollar figures only as Laura's case facts ($50,000 payments, $300,000 first deposit) or "in Laura's plan"; WInS amounts as percentages. Never a WInS gain, loss or ranking.
- **Coupons, in words that do not reassure falsely:** for an iBonds fund, "its end value is not fixed" or "what it pays out is not fixed" (the whole payout, not only its income); for a bond, "part of each payment comes from reinvested coupons" or "in her plan its set coupons and principal cover most of that payment; the rest depends on reinvested coupons". Never "a set amount" alone (next to "Laura's $50,000 payment" it reads as the whole $50,000) and never "covers the rest" (false if coupons earn 2%). Vary the tail (premortem PM-23). Say what a low coupon does for her: less of her payment rests on reinvested coupons, not "less income"; for a bond, "more of what it pays comes at maturity", not "more of its value".
- **"Facility floor" is glossed** the first time it appears in a note or reflection: "the least she plans to give the facility". The TN Analysis reaches judges before the IPS. Never a dollar figure next to it.
- **No strategy name** in WInS notes. Reflections may use it once if Ray confirms it by 22 Oct (the 30 Sep IPS draft uses it in its pitch), which links the TN Analysis to the IPS.

## 2. Words never used in a note

This list quotes the banned words on purpose; `build_notes.py` checks the exemplars, not this section.

- guaranteed, risk-free, no risk, locked, lock in, safe, stable, "reduce volatility", certain, certainty, 100%, promised, pledged (overclaims; say "backed by the U.S. government" or "the U.S. government owes"). A reflection may use the case phrase "high degree of certainty" once, with its condition (s6).
- match / matched (false for funds and coupon bonds), "cash-flow matched", "needs no rebalancing".
- "a set amount" on its own, "covers her payment" or "covers the rest" (a holding covers most of a payment, and the rest depends on reinvested coupons), "delivers $50,000" (the iBonds rungs fall a few hundred dollars short even at today's yields), "less income to reinvest" as the benefit of a low coupon.
- "I bond" (a U.S. savings bond); write "iBonds" or the fund's full name. Never "repays $X" for an iBonds fund.
- Forecasts and results: will grow / will outperform, CAPE, overvalued, profit, gain, rank.
- "Laura's portfolio" or "her money" for the WInS book; the operating reserve's **size** (the case's own word for her 2033 set-aside is fine); **any 2031 dollar figure** (the range, its top or a co-sponsor amount; the TN guide says these are not expected yet); Laura quotes; her degree, heritage or Taiwan as a reason; "half the fund stays hers" (say what the half is for: her cushion). **The ideas are encouraged:** the floor, co-sponsors, the 2031 range, the half of the fund she keeps as a cushion, the residency, her operating payments.
- Jargon without a gloss: bp, basis points, duration, immunization. Say "percentage point of yield", not "point of yield" (bond prices are quoted in points).
