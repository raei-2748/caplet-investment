How this is computed
  • 20,000 of M3's 200,000 paths per model (seed 20260930), regenerated by M3's own code, with M4's floor-payout draws for the same paths. At the model's own settings the page matches the published 200,000-path figures within sampling noise (rab/page/page_check.txt).
  • Moving the return or volatility slider rescales each model's own shocks: yearly log return = ln(1 + median) + volatility × shock. The shape of each model (fat tails, history's streaks) is kept. For the uncertain-mean model the spread of its centre scales with volatility too.
  • Fund bought 2 Jan 2028 for about $41,000. 2031 top = $150,000 + share × fund value in 2031. 2033 gift = floor payout + share × the lower of the fund's 2031 and 2033 values, capped at the top. Laura keeps the rest.
  • "Keeps at least 10% of her gift in 95% of paths" is the D6 rule's flexibility test (M4_SPEC, pre-registered). "Gift at the top" uses M3's definition: the fund is worth at least its 2031 value in 2033.
  • Coupon scenarios: the gap on Laura's Book L holdings (numbers.yaml reinvest.rung.*), valued in 2031, as in m4_gap_owner.py. A yield fall after 2031 is not modelled.
