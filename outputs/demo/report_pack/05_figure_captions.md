Horizontal bar charts illustrating individual security weights (left) and aggregated GICS sector exposures (right) against the optimizer's configured position and sector caps (not official WInS limits). Generated deterministically by `wharton-ic`.

Risk-return scatter comparing eight portfolio construction methodologies (Mean-Variance, CVaR, Risk Budgeting, and Hierarchical Risk Parity) against naive Equal Weight and SPY benchmark baselines.
