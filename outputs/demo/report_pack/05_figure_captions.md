# Publication Figure Captions for Final Report Submission

### Figure 1: Target Portfolio Asset Allocation and Sector Diversification
Horizontal bar charts illustrating individual security weights (left) and aggregated GICS sector exposures (right) against Wharton's non-negotiable 15% position cap and 25% sector cap. Generated deterministically by `wharton-ic`.

### Figure 2: Quantitative Portfolio Optimization Comparison
Risk-return scatter comparing eight portfolio construction methodologies (Mean-Variance, CVaR, Risk Budgeting, and Hierarchical Risk Parity) against naive Equal Weight and SPY benchmark baselines.

### Figure 3: Fat-Tailed Monte Carlo Outcome Distribution
Forward one-year capital projection (5,000 simulations using a fat-tailed Student-t distribution, nu=5) displaying the 5th, 50th, and 95th percentile outcome trajectories and evaluating probability of achieving the client's 9% return target.

### Figure 4: Portfolio Constituent Pairwise Correlation Matrix
Correlation heatmap computed from historical daily adjusted close returns over a rolling 252-day lookback window.
