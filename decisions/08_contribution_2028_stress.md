# D8: Stress-testing the 2028 $150k contribution

**Status:** DECIDED · **Evidence:** `tables.md` → "The 2028 $150k contribution"; `results.json` → `sensitivity.contribution_2028`; Chart 03

## Question
The case says Laura *will* contribute $150k at the start of 2028, from "publishing advances, speaking engagements, licensing, and other entrepreneurial ventures." Creative income is irregular. Should we stress-test this, and how do we mention it without contradicting the case?

## Evidence
| 2028 contribution | A: P(funded) / median facility | B | C60 |
|---|---|---|---|
| As planned ($150k) | 100% / $167k | 100% / $167k | 97.2% / $182k |
| Half ($75k) | 100% / $85k | 98.5% / $90k | 88.7% / $100k |
| Delayed to 2030 | 100% / $148k | 100% / $149k | 97.3% / $163k |
| Never arrives | **100%** / $2k | **55.9%** / $4k | **59.9%** / $18k |

- Wealth managers who advise creatives stress that income is irregular: advances, royalties and fees arrive in lumps. This is why they recommend buffers and diversified income.
- Under A, the promise is funded by the *first* cheque alone. The second cheque only affects the facility.

## Debate
- **Advocate (include, briefly):** This is the strongest single argument for A over B. It shows the judge criterion "Client Knowledge": we understood *how* Laura earns, not just the numbers.
- **Opponent:** The case states the contribution as fact. Questioning it could look like we misread the case.
- **Judge:** Include it as a **robustness check**, not a forecast. Use one paragraph and chart 03, with the wording "even if…". Do not change the base projections.

## Decision
- Base projections assume the $150k arrives in Jan 2028, as the case states.
- The Final Report adds one robustness paragraph and chart 03: *"Because the operating reserve is funded entirely from the first contribution, all ten payments stay fully funded even if the second contribution were delayed, reduced, or never arrived. Only the facility contribution would change."*
- Operational point: if the 2028 money arrives late, the growth sleeve simply starts later. There is no need to change the reserve.

## What would change our mind
Nothing that would affect the base case. If the case is clarified to say the contribution is uncertain, promote this to a main section.

## Plain English for Laura
Your ten yearly payments are paid for by your first $300k alone, so they're safe even if your 2028 book or speaking income comes in late or smaller than planned. The $150k only affects how much you can give to the building.
