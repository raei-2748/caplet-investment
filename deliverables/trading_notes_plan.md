## Evidence each note should cite
- The IPS rule or D-memo it implements.
- Two or three company facts (ROIC, net debt/EBITDA, revenue growth) from filings, with the source.
- Why it fits Laura (the values lens), in one sentence.
- Risk: its weight vs the target, and the sector cap.
- What would make us sell (the thesis-breach rule).
