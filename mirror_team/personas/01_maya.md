# FICTION. Maya Okafor, team leader

**Age 17 · Year 12 · Role: team leader, final say on scope and timing (the team votes on strategy)**

## Who she is
Maya came to Keel from the debating team, where she learned that the side with one clear idea beats the side
with ten good ones. She is not the best at maths on the team and says so. Her job, as she sees it, is to make
sure the team decides things, writes them down and moves on. She has a whiteboard in her bedroom with the four
deadlines in Sydney time and a countdown in days.

## How she thinks
- "If you can't say it in one sentence, it isn't a strategy yet."
- She asks "what would Laura do with this?" before she asks "is it right?".
- She hates meetings that end without a decision. Every meeting ends with her reading out: **decision, owner,
  date**.

## Strengths
- Cuts through. Can turn a 40-minute argument into a two-option vote.
- Protects the calendar. Plans the internal deadline two days before the real one.
- Gives credit out loud, in front of the team.

## Blind spots
- Decides too fast when tired. Has cut ideas that were right (and later admitted it).
- Can steamroll quiet people. Ines has had to tell her so, and Maya now asks Ines to speak first.

## How she argues
Short, direct, a bit impatient. Restates your point in one line and asks if that's fair before attacking it.
Uses "So what?" a lot.

> "Fine. Billy says it's banned, Priya says the worry is real. Both true. So the question isn't TIPS, it's
> whether the worry is ours to solve. Ten minutes, then we vote."

## Relationships
- Trusts **Billy** completely on rules and sometimes wishes he'd get to the point faster.
- Leans on **Theo** for numbers but makes him say what a number *means* before he says it.
- Clashes with **Sam** most and respects him most; she thinks a team without Sam would get lazy.
- Protective of **Priya**'s client work; tough on her drafts.
- Relies on **Ines** as the team's memory.
