# FICTION. Sam Kowalczyk, contrarian and trader

**Age 17 · Year 12 · Role: devil's advocate, market watch, WInS orders (only Sam places orders, after Billy's check)**

## Who he is
Sam joined because he likes markets and he reads financial news on his phone at 6 a.m. He thinks most student
teams are scared of risk and that judges can smell it. He argues the other side on purpose, and he is right
often enough that no one can ignore him. He is also the only one allowed to place WInS orders, a rule the team
made after a near mis-click, and he takes it seriously.

## How he thinks
- "Who says?" He tests every assumption by attacking it.
- A plan that never loses money probably never helps much either. Where's the leap?
- Markets are made of people. He watches what moved today and asks why.

## Strengths
- Finds the weakest point of any plan within five minutes.
- Keeps the team honest about what it gave up. If the plan is safe, he makes them say what safety cost.
- Fast, careful trader: limit orders, checks the ticket twice, screenshots everything.

## Blind spots
- Chases performance. Has to be reminded (often by Billy, with a quote) that Wharton doesn't judge returns.
- Argues for the sake of it when bored.
- Confuses "bold" with "more equity". The bold idea might be something else entirely.

## How he argues
Loud, funny, quick. Exaggerates to make a point, then backs off if you push with evidence.

> "Okay, so the plan is 'buy bonds and wait seven years'. Bold. Brave. My nan would sign off on it. Tell me what a
> judge reads in that and remembers."

## Relationships
- **Maya** and he clash weekly. He'd follow her anywhere.
- **Theo** is his favourite opponent: Theo's tables are the only thing that actually change his mind.
- **Billy** finds him exhausting; Sam finds Billy indispensable and tells him so, rarely.
- Allies with **Priya** on ambition, though he wants returns and she wants meaning.
- **Ines** strikes his best lines from drafts. He keeps a file of them.
