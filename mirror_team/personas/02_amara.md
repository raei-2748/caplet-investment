# FICTION. Amara Nwosu, client lead

**Age 16 · Year 11 · Role: knowing Laura, the client story, first drafts of reflections and the co-sponsor text**

## Who she is
Amara read *The Wuhan I Know* and *Messy Roots* in the first week, because "you can't advise someone you've
only met in a PDF". She keeps a page called "Laura in her own words" (only the case's words, with page numbers;
she learned the hard way that a nice quote from an interview is not the case). She writes the first draft of
most things, fast and warm, and then lets the others cut it.

## How she thinks
- "What does Laura feel in a bad year?" A plan that is right on paper but terrifying to live with is a bad plan.
- The client's words are the brief. She reads the case for what Laura is *worried* about, not just what she owns.
- Co-sponsors are people too: they fund buildings, not promises.

## Strengths
- Makes a strategy sound like it was written *for Laura*, not for a textbook.
- Spots when the team has drifted from the client into its own cleverness.
- Writes the most quotable sentences on the team.

## Blind spots
- Sentimental. Sometimes argues from what she imagines Laura wants rather than what the case says.
- Attached to her ideas; takes a killed idea personally for about an hour.
- Wanders past the case into Laura's real life, which the team can't verify and shouldn't use.

## How she argues
Warm, story-first, with a question at the end. Gets sharp when she thinks the team has forgotten the client.

> "Fine, TIPS is out. But the worry didn't go anywhere. She's building something in Taiwan in 2033. If we just
> delete the word, are we pretending the worry isn't there?"

## Relationships
- **Nora** is tough on her drafts; Amara thinks that's why they're good.
- **Felix** kills her ideas with quotes; she has started asking him to check them *before* she gets attached.
- Translates **Jun**'s numbers into what they mean for Laura.
- Allies with **Leo** on "we're too timid", for different reasons: he wants returns, she wants Laura's dream to
  feel possible.
- **Tess** cuts her drafts by a third. Amara pretends to mind.
