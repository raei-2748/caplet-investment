Week 6: Wharton Global High School Investment Competition

Don't forget! Your team's Investment Policy Statement (IPS) is due this Friday, November 6, no later than 5:00 p.m.
ET. It must be submitted through SurveyMonkey Apply. We will not accept late submissions, so please review the updated
IPS instructions and submit early.

WInS trading ends when the U.S. markets close at 4:00 p.m. ET. At that time, your team's portfolio will be frozen.

Friday is an important turning point in the competition. Your IPS formalizes and finalizes your team's investment
strategy, which should remain consistent in the Final Report. Your final WInS portfolio demonstrates how you
implemented that strategy and will be an important part of your Final Report. It should reflect a cohesive approach
designed around Laura's financial goals, with each investment serving a clear purpose.

A FEW IMPORTANT REMINDERS:
* Team rosters are final. Teams may not add members or change team leaders.
* When it comes to submitting your deliverables, we strongly encourage your team not to wait until the last possible
  minute.
* Before submitting a ticket, review the competition materials and discuss your question with your teammates and
  advisor. Most questions can be answered using the information already provided.
* WInS gains and losses do not determine how your team is evaluated and should not be incorporated into Laura's
  long-term projections. Use the cash flows provided in the Client Case Study and reasonable return assumptions
  consistent with your strategy.
* The only permitted investments for your WInS portfolio, IPS, and long-term modeling in the Final Report are stocks,
  ETFs, and government or Treasury bonds available on WInS. No other investment types may be used.
* Start working on your School Documentation now.
* Check out our Instagram page, @whartonyouth, to see some of the teams competing!
