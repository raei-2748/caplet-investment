Investment Competition Guide      1
The Final Report is the culmination of your team’s work in the competition. It should evaluate how effectively 
your team implemented its investment strategy, applied it to Laura’s financial goals, and developed its final 
recommendations. The report should draw on your prior deliverables, final WInS portfolio, and analysis without 
repeating the content of your Investment Policy Statement (IPS) or Trading Notes Analysis.
There is no single correct strategy or recommendation. Teams may reach different conclusions about portfolio 
construction, risk, funding certainty, the operating reserve, the facility contribution, and financial flexibility. 
Your report will be evaluated based on the quality of your analysis, evidence, assumptions, judgment, and 
communication.
How the Deliverables Fit T ogether
Each deliverable serves a distinct purpose, but evaluators will consider all three together.
The strategy established in your IPS should remain consistent in the Final Report. The Final Report is not an 
opportunity to create a new strategy or revise the IPS. Evaluators will assess whether your Trading Notes 
Analysis, final WInS portfolio, assumptions, analysis, and recommendations align with your strategy and  
Laura’s financial goals.
Minimum Requirements
Your Final Report must:
 • Include all required sections.
 • Meet the minimum and maximum page requirements.
 • Actual holdings.
 • Use only permitted investments.
 • Address Laura’s financial goals and circumstances.
 • Demonstrate consistency with the strategy established in the IPS.
 • Follow all formatting, citation, and submission requirements.
Reports that do not meet these minimum requirements may be removed from semifinal consideration.
Meeting the minimum requirements makes a report eligible for evaluation. It does not guarantee a strong 
evaluation.

Investment Competition Guide      2
Permitted Investments
For all long-term portfolio modeling, teams may use only the following investment types:
 • Stocks available on WInS
 • Exchange-traded funds available on WInS
 • Government or Treasury bonds available on WInS
The following are not permitted:
 • Margin trading
 • Short selling
 • Stock-secured debt
 • Cryptocurrency
 • Derivatives, including options, futures, and swaps
 • Individual TIPS
 • Individual STRIPS
 • Any investment other than the permitted investments listed above
Required Format and Submission
 • Submit the report through SurveyMonkey Apply by the stated deadline.
 • Submit the report as a PDF file no larger than 5 MB.
 • Use 12-point Times New Roman font.
 • Double-space the report.
 • Use 1-inch margins.
 • Include page numbers in the bottom-left corner.
 • Cite all sources, images, media, and generative AI use in APA format.
 • Include a separate References page.
 • Do not include external links that evaluators are expected to open.
Tables and graphics may be single-spaced when needed for readability. Graphics should support the narrative 
and clarify the team’s analysis rather than distract from it.

Section 3: Integrated Recommendation and Reflection
Minimum: Six pages Maximum: Eight pages
Apply the investment strategy established in your IPS, as demonstrated through your final WInS portfolio, 
to develop an integrated recommendation for Laura. Support your recommendation with reasonable 
assumptions and analysis.
Your recommendation must:
 • Explain how your strategy supports the ten-year operating commitment
 • Recommend an appropriate operating reserve
 • Recommend a responsible facility contribution while preserving financial flexibility
 • Provide a credible dollar range that Laura could communicate to potential co-sponsors in 2031
 • Explain the level of confidence associated with that range and the factors that could affect it
 • Provide language Laura could use when communicating with prospective co-sponsors
For your long-term analysis, begin with Laura’s $300,000 investment at the beginning of 2027 and add the 
additional $150,000 contribution at the beginning of 2028. Do not add WInS gains or losses to Laura’s  
projected portfolio value.
Clearly identify important assumptions, uncertainties, and limitations. Explain what Laura or her asset 
management team should monitor as 2031 and 2033 approach and how changing conditions could affect 
your recommendations.
You are not expected to:
 • Estimate the facility’s total cost or calculate the complete funding gap
 • Prepare a construction budget or detailed business plan
 • Account for Laura’s personal taxes
 • Address the legal or regulatory requirements associated with establishing the residency

Conclude your report by explaining how your team’s research, collaboration, disagreements, decision-

Evaluation Criteria
Evaluators will consider all three deliverables together using five high-level criteria. 
Investment Strategy
The team establishes and applies a clear and coherent strategy that reflects mid-term and long-term thinking, 
appropriate diversification, and consistency across the IPS, Trading Notes Analysis, and Final Report.
Client Knowledge and Objectives
The team demonstrates a meaningful understanding of Laura’s circumstances and financial goals, including the 
ten-year operating commitment, operating reserve, facility contribution, co-sponsor considerations, liquidity 
needs, and financial flexibility.
Investment Competition Guide      5
Portfolio Analysis
The team uses quantitative and qualitative analysis to evaluate its final WInS portfolio and support its long-term 
recommendations for Laura. The analysis demonstrates purposeful investment decisions, appropriate allocation 
and diversification, reasonable assumptions, consideration of uncertainty, and an understanding of the portfolio’s 
strengths and limitations.
WInS gains and losses are not treated as evidence of the quality of the strategy.
Articulation of Competition Experience
The team explains how research, collaboration, disagreement, decision-making, uncertainty, and learning  
shaped its investment thinking and final recommendation.
Creativity and Presentation
The team presents a compelling, well-organized, and authentic recommendation. Supporting data, projections, 
graphics, and co-sponsor communication are used purposefully and effectively.
Final Standard
Addressing every required element is necessary, but it does not by itself demonstrate strong analysis. The 
strongest reports will connect the team’s established strategy, portfolio decisions, analysis, assumptions, and 
understanding of Laura’s goals into a cohesive and well-supported recommendation.
There is no single correct answer. Your goal is to demonstrate that your team can apply its investment strategy 
thoughtfully and responsibly to a realistic client scenario.
