Week 3: Wharton Global High School Investment Competition

Thank you for submitting your team roster! You may now return to your team's SurveyMonkey Apply account to submit your Trading Notes Analysis.

Please note that your team is now finalized. Teams may not add members. Requests to remove a team member due to extenuating circumstances must be submitted in writing by the Advisor and approved by the Investment Competition team. Teams that remove a member without prior approval will be disqualified.

A FEW IMPORTANT REMINDERS

- Teams are not permitted to contact the client.
- Your investment strategy is the cornerstone of the Wharton Global High School Investment Competition.
- WInS provides your team with the opportunity to put your investment strategy into action using real-world market conditions.
- Use the simulator to research investments, construct your portfolio, make investment decisions, and experience how changing market conditions affect your holdings.
- You are not being evaluated based on:
  - Your portfolio ranking
  - How many trades you make
  - Whether you outperform other teams
  - Whether your portfolio makes money during the competition
- WInS is a tool for implementing your investment strategy. It is not the competition scorecard, and gains or losses generated during the WInS trading period should not be added to or subtracted from the client's long-term portfolio projections.
- Those projections should begin with the cash flows provided in the Client Case Study and use reasonable return assumptions consistent with your strategy.
- A long-term investment strategy cannot be judged solely by what happens in the market over a few weeks. Instead, your portfolio provides evidence of the decisions your team made and how you put your strategy into practice.
- Official trading will end on November 6, when your Investment Policy Statement is due. At that time, your WInS portfolio will be frozen, and your team may no longer trade or change its holdings.

IMPORTANT DATES

- Friday, October 23, 5:00 p.m. ET: Trading Notes Analysis due
- Friday, November 6, 5:00 p.m. ET: Investment Policy Statement due and trading ends
- Monday, November 9, 9:00 a.m. ET: Instructions and requirements for the Final Report posted
- Friday, December 4, 5:00 p.m. ET: School documentation and Final Report due
