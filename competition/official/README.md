# Official Wharton Competition Documents

This directory is the designated vault for official, unmodified competition files provided by the Wharton Global High School Investment Competition.

## Required Official Files for the 2026–2027 Competition:

1. **`client_case.pdf` / `client_case.txt`**:
   The official competition case study describing the client's identity, background, financial goals, investment horizon, liquidity needs, values, and special preferences.

2. **`approved_securities.csv` / `approved_securities.xlsx`**:
   The Wharton-approved stock and ETF universe list. All trading in the competition must strictly be drawn from this authorized list.

3. **`competition_rules.pdf`**:
   The official rules packet detailing trading limits, minimum and maximum stock count, sector concentration rules, cash buffer requirements, and WInS platform guidelines.

4. **`ips_guidelines.pdf`**:
   Wharton's instructions and rubric for the Investment Policy Statement (Midterm Report).

5. **`final_report_rubric.pdf`**:
   Wharton's official rubric and page limits for the Final Strategy Report.

---

> [!IMPORTANT]
> When the official 2026–27 files are released by Wharton, place them in this directory and run:
> ```bash
> wharton-ic ingest-official
> ```
> This command validates the official approved universe and checks for schema discrepancies.
