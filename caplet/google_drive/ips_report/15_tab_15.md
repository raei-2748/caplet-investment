**Investment Strategy Elevator Pitch**  
 Laura’s co-sponsors may fund the building without paying to run it, so Root-and-Branch funds the running costs first. Its roots, about $292,000 of Treasuries, cover ten $50,000 payments, and more Treasuries back her minimum contribution. Its branch, a global index fund, grows her gift above that minimum.

**Investment Policy Statement**  
 This policy governs the portfolio Laura Gao funds with $300,000 in January 2027 and $150,000 in January 2028 for the Taiwan creative residency she opens in 2033. Its first objective is to fund ten $50,000 operating payments, each January from 2033 through 2042, with high certainty from these two deposits alone. We match each payment with a Treasury bond or defined-maturity Treasury ETF maturing before it, so each payment depends mainly on the US government paying its debts. The second objective is a facility contribution with a floor Laura can promise co-sponsors and growth above it.

With her living costs met elsewhere and no withdrawals due before 2033, Laura’s ability to bear risk beyond the payments is high. Her willingness depends on the objective. As a statistics graduate and entrepreneur she accepts measured risk, but her tolerance for a missed payment is low, since the artists would bear the loss. Above the facility floor, we judge her tolerance above average.

Root-and-Branch secures the roots (the payments and a facility floor) before investing in the branch. The 2027 deposit funds the latest payments first, since their cost moves most with interest rates. At late-September 2026 yields all ten cost about $292,000; if falling yields push that above $300,000 before January, the 2028 deposit completes them. From the 2028 remainder, just enough goes into a 2032 Treasury ETF to grow back to the full remainder; that sum is Laura’s facility floor. The rest forms the branch, a global index fund of thousands of companies. The roots rely on one issuer by design, since no dollar asset is safer.

In 2031 Laura tells co-sponsors a range from the floor, quoted at the 2032 ETF’s projected payout rounded down, to the floor plus half the equity fund’s value then. In 2033 she contributes the floor plus half the fund’s value, capped at that top, so she cannot miss the range. A range built to hold protects her credibility better than a narrower one she might miss. The other half stays with Laura as a cushion if costs rise or co-sponsors fall short.

A fall in stocks can shrink only the part above the floor. Moving floor money into equities would raise the expected contribution, but we do not, as it could break a number Laura has quoted. Inflation or a stronger Taiwan dollar raises costs in Taiwan, and the half Laura keeps is her defense.

Until 2033 the portfolio is held without trading beyond investing the 2028 deposit, with income reinvested in the holding that paid it. Each January we review it with Laura and act only if her goals change or a holding becomes unavailable. We replace an unavailable holding with a Treasury maturing earlier. In 2033 the payment Treasuries become the operating reserve, shrinking as each payment is made. Any payment shortfall is covered first by the 2027 leftover, then the half Laura keeps, then the contribution above the floor. Our WInS portfolio shows this allocation, scaled down.

#### **改了什么，为什么**

- **段落顺序。** V5 在第 4、5 段就用了 “the half Laura keeps”，直到第 6 段才解释这一半从哪来。我把 2031/2033 区间规则提到风险段之前，这样先定义，再使用。
- **Pitch 里的 “rarely” 改成 “may”。** "共同出资方很少承担运营费用"是一个关于普遍情况的事实判断，我无法确认案例支持这个说法。如果 case study 原文就是这么写的，可以改回去。另外，策略名两边的引号去掉了。
- **“secures the roots, the payments and a facility floor,”** 读起来像三个并列项，改成了括号。
- **“Markets can shrink... but never below the floor”** 改成 “A fall in stocks can shrink only the part above the floor”。floor 放在 IBTM 里，你们自己的交易记录也写了它的最终支付额不固定，所以 “never” 说过头了。新句子按结构本身就成立。
- **“raises facility costs” 改成 “raises costs in Taiwan”。** [INFERRED] 如果运营支出用台币结算，台币升值也会减少每笔 $50,000 能买到的东西，影响的不只是设施成本。
- **“Until 2033 the portfolio is held without trading”** 和 2028 年 1 月的买入是矛盾的，补上了 “beyond investing the 2028 deposit”。原来的 “when we substitute” 不清楚是跟着哪个条件走的，所以拆成了两句。
- **“rounded down after any expected shortfall”** 有两种读法：付款缺口，或者 IBTM 支付额不足。资金缺口的补足顺序从不动用 floor，所以我按第二种理解，写成了 “the 2032 ETF’s projected payout rounded down”。如果你们原意是第一种，请告诉我。

#### **需要团队决定，我没有替你们填**

- **2027 年剩下的约 $8,000 放在哪里？** Tab 7 的计算（40K = 32K + 8K）显示它进了增长部分。如果它在 VT 里，"covered first by the 2027 leftover" 用的其实是会下跌的股票资金，IPS 应该说明这一点。
- **交易记录写着 2037 年 2 月和 2038 年 5 月到期的债券，收回的本金会先放进短期 Treasury ETF，但 IPS 里没提。** 如果要补，大约需要 13 个词（例如 “Proceeds from a bond maturing months early wait in a short-term Treasury ETF.”），那就要从别处删掉同样多的词。
- **“she cannot miss the range” 成立的前提是 IBTM 的支付额不低于向下取整后的报价。** [INFERRED] 发生这种情况的概率很低，但不是零。保留 “rounded down” 就是为了留出这个缓冲。
