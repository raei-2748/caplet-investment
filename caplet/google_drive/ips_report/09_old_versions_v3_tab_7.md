**2. Good news on flexibility.** Nonprofit reserve guidelines (NORI 2008; Candid) suggest keeping 3–6 months of expenses, and no more than 2 years. For a $50k-a-year operation, that's about [equation]**25k minimum and $100k maximum**. Laura's retained half, probably in the tens of thousands, falls inside that band. That gives you a professional benchmark for "appropriate flexibility," which answers the question you asked earlier. Add a one-line rule for when the cushion can be used.

**3. The guaranteed floor is what persuades co-sponsors.** Research on seed money and lead gifts (List & Lucking-Reiley 2002; Andreoni 2006) finds that committed money is what draws other donors in. Pledge accounting rules (FASB 2018) also count only the unconditional part as firm. So lead with the floor in the co-sponsor draft and present the upside as conditional. This also argues against shrinking the floor too much in the trade-off table.
