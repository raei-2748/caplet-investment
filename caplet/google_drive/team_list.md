### **Wharton Global High School Investment Competition**

##### **2026–27 Information Pack**

**What is Wharton?**  
The Wharton School at the University of Pennsylvania is one of the world’s leading business schools, particularly recognised for finance, economics and management.

**What is the competition?**  
The Wharton Global High School Investment Competition is a **free international investment competition** in which high-school students work in teams to develop an investment strategy for a real-world client. Teams manage a **$500,000 virtual portfolio** through the Wharton Investment Simulator (WInS), research companies and industries, and justify how their strategy meets the client’s long-term objectives.

#### **Who can participate?**

- **Team size:** 4–6 students
- All students must attend the **same high school**
- Students are generally **14–18 years old** and currently in secondary/high school
- Each student can participate on **only one team**
- Every team requires a **student team leader aged 16+**
- Every team requires a **teacher/educator from the school as its official advisor**
- One advisor can supervise **up to 5 teams**
- There is **no limit on the number of teams from one school**
- **Entry is free**

#### **What do teams actually do?**

Teams receive a case involving a real Wharton graduate as their prospective investment client. They must:

- Analyse the client’s goals, circumstances and risk tolerance.
- Develop an overall investment strategy.
- Research industries, companies and securities.
- Build and manage a virtual investment portfolio.
- Submit an **Investment Policy Statement (IPS)**.
- Submit a comprehensive **Final Report** explaining and defending their strategy.
**Important:** This is **not a competition to make the highest portfolio return**. Wharton judges teams primarily on the quality, creativity and justification of their investment strategy and how effectively it addresses the client’s objectives.

#### **Registration**

Students **cannot register themselves**. The team's teacher advisor must complete the official registration process on behalf of the team.

Before registering, teams should have:

- 4–6 committed students
- One student leader aged 16+
- One teacher advisor
- Agreement on team responsibilities and availability during September–December

#### **Academic Integrity & AI**

Wharton permits generative AI for **brainstorming and generating ideas**, but teams may not submit AI-generated work as their own. Paid competition advisors, consultants and non-Wharton courses claiming to coach students specifically for the competition are prohibited.

#### **Why Participate?**

The competition provides practical experience in:

**Investment Research • Portfolio Management • Financial Analysis • Risk & Diversification • Strategy • Teamwork • Leadership • Written Communication • Presentations**

It also gives students the opportunity to compete internationally and, for the strongest teams, present their work to finance professionals and Wharton judges.
