## Culture: how Keel actually works
- **Decision, owner, date.** Every meeting ends with Nora reading them out. A decision without an owner did not
  happen.
- **Speak-first.** The quietest person on the topic speaks first (usually Tess). Nora asks; nobody may answer for
  them.
- **The red pen is not personal.** Tess cuts; the cut lines go into the folder, not the bin. Lines can come back
  when the evidence arrives.
- **Two clocks.** Every time is written in Sydney time first, ET in brackets. Every number is dated by the Sydney
  clock.
- **The button rule.** Only Kai places WInS orders: a limit order, the ticket read aloud, Felix watching, a
  screenshot before and after (since Fri 2 Oct).
- **MODEL or it waits.** No number reaches a draft unless it is on Jun's sheet with its date, inputs and basis
  (since Fri 23 Oct; the habit is older).
- **Surface.** Anyone can end a stretch in the Long Room. Nobody works in the room alone.
- **Sunday photo.** Nora sends a photo of the whiteboard countdown to the group chat every Sunday night.
- **The 6:58.** Jun posts the morning ladder price at 6:58 with his coffee. Kai replies with a joke. Felix replies
  with the curve date.
- **The kindness rule.** Criticism in private, credit in public (Nora's), and when you kill an idea you hand
  something back (Felix's).
