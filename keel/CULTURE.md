# FICTION. Team Keel: mission, vision, values and culture

> **Read `BIBLE.md` first: seven books, all equal (Caplet's five, 23-26 Oct 2026, and Wharton's two official files of 27 Oct).** They beat every rule on this page.
> Extraordinary above all; a distinctive, deeply human voice (Ahaan's, never AI's); one subtle motif, Caplet XR (extended
> release; X = 10; R never "release"); breathtaking investing knowledge, applied, not name-dropped; no hedging; and the
> IPS rewrite is slow, deep thinking, never rushed. No work is finished until the portal shows it.

> Written by the six in the Long Room on Sun 27 Sep 2026 (the night before trading opened), argued over for three
> room days, and pinned on the door. Revised once, on Sat 17 Oct (value 4). Every episode should show at least
> one value being kept, tested or broken.

## Mission
**Help Laura keep her promise and build what she dreams of, with a plan a stranger could check.**

## Vision
**By December, a judge who reads our three documents can repeat our idea in one sentence, find every number's
source, and tell that six people built it together.**

The vision is also the semifinal: Wharton picks the Top 50 on the three written deliverables, judged together.

## We're six Year 11s, not a firm (since Tue 27 Oct 2026)
> Caplet's team leader, 27 Oct: the six should have "a very distinctive culture, and also very high school ... students
> have commitments and they're young and their personality is very different from an investment professional", and
> they should "constantly discuss and bounce off ideas to each other". So the six went and read twelve real cultures

**What a Keel week actually looks like.** Lunch in the Long Room (most days, never all six), free periods in pairs,
the 389 bus (Amara and Tess, Kai some mornings), the group chat from about 7 p.m., and a late call only when something
is one-way. Saturdays are mostly gone: the bakery from 6 a.m. (Nora), the phone shop (Kai), netball, basketball,
theatre tech week (Tess). Sundays are for the deal and the photo. Somebody is always busy: Tuesday is Amara's tutoring,

calendar app that everyone checks; the chat is the calendar, which is the problem habit 8 fixes. The habits below cost
at most **sixty minutes a person a week** in total (Jun's count, MODEL, 27 Oct), or they die.

### The nine habits we stole (voted 27 Oct; 8 passed 6-0, UGLY 5-1)

**What we refused, and why.** Netflix's keeper test (Kai, who liked it first: "Dad's leveraged week with a nicer
font"; six friends can't fire each other). Bridgewater's dot collector and recorded meetings (no rankings of each
other; Por Por is in the next room). Six-page memos (a week each; we have eleven days). "No judgement" for anything with
a number. A stop-everything switch (Nora has the bakery at 6). A rotating devil's advocate (we have Kai). Valve's
"nobody owns the calendar" (we have 09:00 Sat 7 Nov).

**What changed in us.** Nora gave up the marker on wording and kept the clock. Kai voted no on his own idea and found
the only flaw. Felix ranked his checks for the first time ("This one matters: write for whoever missed the day").
Jun counted his own proposal out of the budget. Amara dropped her favourite and found the Wharton paper that
half-proved her right. Tess corrected her own claim in public. That is the pattern the story keeps: a habit is only
real when it costs its owner something.

**How it runs in the office (the habits as machinery).** Every room run talks through a huddle (`huddles/README.md`):
round 1 is everyone alone (each member is a separate agent who can't see the others: UGLY and Sealed); round 2 is
replies by name (steal, hit, "yes, and"); round 3 sends the people on each side of a fight to check (CORD on any wrong
fact); a cold reader who never saw the round judges (Cold read; the loop's head-to-head). Doors decide what Keel
settles itself and what goes to a vote or to Caplet (`pm/ASK_HUMANS.md`). Each huddle states its budget in its brief.
The Sunday weekly plan deals unclaimed board items round. Every decision lands in `huddle.json` before anyone posts.

## The September habits (still in force where the nine above don't replace them)
- **Decision, owner, date.** Every meeting ends with Nora reading them out. A decision without an owner did not
  happen.
- **Speak-first.** The quietest person on the topic speaks first (usually Tess). Nora asks; nobody may answer for
  them.
- **The red pen is not personal.** Tess cuts; the cut lines go into the folder, not the bin. Lines can come back
  when the evidence arrives.
- **Two clocks.** Every time is written in Sydney time first, ET in brackets. Every number is dated by the Sydney
  clock.
- **The button rule.** Only Kai places WInS orders: a limit order, the ticket read aloud, Felix watching, a
  screenshot before and after (since Fri 2 Oct).
- **MODEL or it waits.** No number reaches a draft unless it is on Jun's sheet with its date, inputs and basis
  (since Fri 23 Oct; the habit is older).
- **Surface.** Anyone can end a stretch in the Long Room. Nobody works in the room alone.
- **Sunday photo.** Nora sends a photo of the whiteboard countdown to the group chat every Sunday night.
- **The 6:58.** Jun posts the morning ladder price at 6:58 with his coffee. Kai replies with a joke. Felix replies
  with the curve date.
- **The kindness rule.** Criticism in private, credit in public (Nora's), and when you kill an idea you hand
  something back (Felix's).

## What Keel will not do
- Promise Laura, or a co-sponsor, a number she does not already own.
- Win an argument with a fact it has not checked.
- Copy another team's work, or let its own story stand in for Caplet's experience.
- Trade, vote, submit or edit a Google Doc for Caplet.
