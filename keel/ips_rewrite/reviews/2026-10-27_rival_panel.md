## Where V25 lost points (the same five, from all three judges)
1. **The device doesn't earn its words.** J3: "the pharmacist 'XR / do not crush' device and the brand name answer
   none of Wharton's five points." J2: "'X is ten and R is redemption' plus 'Do not crush' spend words on branding."
   J1: "may read as a gimmick." Caplet XR is locked (Book 5), so this is not a vote to drop it. It is a vote to cut the
   decoder-ring sentence and make the motif do an investing job where it appears ("the reserve changes itself",
   extended release), as Book 2 asks: "a tool, not a crutch".
2. **Products named where a framework was asked for** (all three): IBTM and VT by ticker. Book 7: "rather than
   describing individual investments". Say "a Treasury fund that turns itself into cash" and "one world index fund";
   the tickers live in the TNA and the Final Report.
3. **Dense, sometimes cryptic** (all three): "before the purchase" (which purchase?), "capital-protected note with
   the bank taken out" needs a reread for a non-specialist. Same finding as the Shark Tank panel.
4. **Growth and flexibility are thin** (J1, J2): "a real, sized role rather than a closing sentence"; the stock slice
   is small and the case for that is "only implicit".
5. **Articulation 7:** nothing shows how the team reached its call. J2: "none shows, even in one sentence, how the team
   reached its call (what it tested, what it threw out)".

## Caveats
- These are AI judges. They may reward analytical density more than human volunteers do, and the Shark Tank panel
  (34/50) and Ray both find V25 cold. Read this as "V25 wins on substance", not "V25 is finished".
- The judges ran inside this repo, whose project file mentions Caplet XR. They were told to ignore it, and they marked
  XR down, not up, so a home-team bias is unlikely but not ruled out.
- The rivals are simulated. The raw-AI ones (A*) are the closest thing we have to what AI-assisted teams will submit.
