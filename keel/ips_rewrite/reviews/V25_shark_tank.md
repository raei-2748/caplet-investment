## Reader 1: the shark (300 IPSs in a weekend, backs 50)
**Pitch.** "Laura's ten $50,000 cheques are debts, so we pay them with debts owed to her." That one gets me to lean
forward: it's a chiasmus that's also the strategy. "Caplet XR, extended release:" in front of it is a label I haven't
been given a reason to care about yet. It costs four words and delays the hook.

**IPS, first 60 seconds.** "Every team will promise Laura her ten cheques. We would rather buy them." Best opener in
my pile: it picks a fight with the other 299. Paragraph 2 lost me at the pharmacist. Three sentences of glossary
come before I've seen any stakes. Then the sceptic paragraph pulled me back in.

**Do I care about the residency?** No: it is only "the building", "the project". Nobody lives or teaches there;
"she breaks her word" (paragraph 6) is the only human cost on the page.

**Would I back them?** Yes, as a maybe-plus. In one sentence: "They bought the bills with bonds, and they set
the 2031 promise at the point where over-promising and under-promising cost the same." The trouble is that I had to
work out the second half myself. The page never shows that "three times worse" and "three times in four" are one idea.
**What I didn't get:** any reason to believe *this team* over a good textbook. Nothing on the page says "we tried X,
it broke, here's what we learned".

## Reader 2: the portfolio manager
**Extraordinary, and applied:**
- Human capital as wrong-way risk: "income that shrinks in the same slump that makes bonds dearer." This is a real
  insight and it's correct. The 2028 deposit falls at exactly the moment the ladder top-up gets more expensive, so it
  funds the hope and never the cheques. This is the best investing line in the draft.
- Capital-protected note "with the bank taken out": mechanically right (floor = remainder discounted to Dec 2032).
- The reserve "changes once ... after that it changes itself". This answers the form's "composition over time" ask in
  one move.

**Mid:**
- "If anything else changes: cheques first, then the floor and the stocks shrink together." "Shrink together" could
  mean pro rata, or stocks first, or something else. The form asks how the strategy guides decisions "as the client's
  needs change", and this is the only sentence that tries to answer. Give it one concrete trigger and one action.
- Diversification and the return assumption are both missing. "World index fund" is all we get. The locked TNA
  already says ~10,000 stocks, 0.06%, about 7.5% (JPM). One clause would cover the form's "portfolio construction and
  diversification".
- Reserve size is implicit. A PM infers "the whole ladder", but a judge with a checklist won't.

**Wrong or exposed:**
- **The gift cap is gone.** The original design caps the gift at the announced top, and anything above it stays
  Laura's (CLAUDE.md, insight_v1 REC). V25 never says so. Without a cap, a boom puts the gift *above* the range,
  which is "outside the range" on the case's own confidence test. With a cap, the bottom is owned and the top is
  binding, so the gift lands inside almost surely. Say it in one clause. It's the strongest certainty claim available
  and it's free.
- **"Before the purchase" is ambiguous.** Which purchase? Jan 2027 and Jan 2028 both buy rungs, and yields can fall
  between them. It's also MODEL on the 22 Oct curve (ground truth s5), so re-run it before anyone signs.
- **Exactness gets claimed twice.** "We can name exactly where it breaks", then three paragraphs later "The one soft
  spot". A PM reads that as two different failure modes. Fold the coupon gap into the break-point sentence, or call
  the break-point "the cheques' only failure", with the coupon gap priced and parked.

## The 5 most "mid" things
1. **"Do not crush."** (the ending) *Why mid:* it ends on the motif when it should end on the ask (s7 beat 5).
   It's a kicker on top of a kicker, and it's the second "do not crush". *Fix:* end on what Laura can say to
   co-sponsors in 2031: the owned bottom, the capped top, and how often she reaches it. Keep the single "do not crush"
   in the XR paragraph only. Sample: "So in 2031 Laura can name a bottom she already owns, and a top she reaches three
   years in four."
2. **"We judge the second about three times worse."** *Why mid:* this is the panel's only real "aha" (the newsvendor
   point), but the page presents it as an opinion and never links it to "three times in four". *Fix:* give the reason
   it's 3x, from the case ("damage her credibility" against a smaller-than-hoped pledge), and spend one clause showing
   that the top sits where the chance of reaching it matches that cost ratio. Then the shark can retell it.
3. **"To a pharmacist, XR means extended release ... To us, X is ten and R is redemption – ten holdings, each redeemed
   in time for one January."** *Why mid:* that's three sentences of glossary before any stakes, and the motif explains
   itself rather than doing work. *Fix:* cut it to one sentence and attach it to the mechanism it mirrors: a fixed dose
   released on a schedule is fixed dollars on fixed dates. Move it after the sceptic paragraph so it lands as the
   answer, not the preface.
4. **"Whatever that deposit leaves becomes the facility gift"** (and "the building", "the project" throughout).
   *Why mid:* the stakes are abstract and the case's own word ("contribution") has been swapped out. *Fix:* in
   paragraph 2, one clause from the case: a residency in Taiwan where artists, writers and educators live and teach,
   and the co-sponsors who join on her word. Use "contribution". No sentiment, just who a missed January lets down.
5. **"That is our high degree of certainty: we can name exactly where it breaks – yields falling more than two
   percentage points before the purchase while her 2028 deposit more than halves."** *Why mid:* it's the right
   instinct (a certainty claim with an address), but "the purchase" is undefined, the later "one soft spot" contradicts
   "exactly", and the cap that would make the range near-certain is missing. *Fix:* name the date, fold the coupon gap
   in as priced and parked, and add the cap in the range paragraph. Then one sentence covers certainty, evaluation
   and assumption, which is three things the case asks for.
