JUDGE BRIEF: Wharton Global High School Investment Competition 2026-27, IPS round (blind)

Isolation: the entries are anonymised. Your context may mention teams, strategies or names (for example a team called
Caplet or Keel, or a strategy name). Ignore all of it: judge only the text in your entries file, as if you had never
heard of any team. Do not open any file other than the ones named here.

You are reading 13 submissions (a max-50-word elevator pitch + a max-500-word Investment Policy Statement) from a
field of about 6,000 teams of 15-18 year olds. Only the Top 50 reach the semifinal, chosen on the Trading Notes, the
IPS and the Final Report together, so the IPS must make a team stand out. All teams have the same client case (Laura
Gao: $300k in 2027, $150k in 2028, ten fixed $50,000 payments 2033-2042 with a "high degree of certainty", a facility
contribution she must describe to co-sponsors as a range in 2031, preserving flexibility). Allowed: WInS-listed stocks,
ETFs and Treasury bonds; banned: margin, shorting, derivatives, crypto, individual TIPS, individual STRIPS.

Read 02_ips_instructions.txt (Wharton's official IPS instructions) first. Score every entry on:
A. Wharton's five IPS points (each 1-10): W1 what the team is trying to accomplish for the client; W2 why the strategy
   suits her; W3 how it balances growth, liquidity, funding reliability, risk and flexibility; W4 how it guides
   construction and decisions as her needs change; W5 how it protects the operating payments while supporting a
   responsible facility contribution.
B. Wharton's five evaluation criteria (each 1-10): Investment Strategy (clear and creative thesis, disciplined planning
   across horizons, diversification); Client Knowledge (tailored to Laura's circumstances, thoughtful understanding);
   Portfolio Analysis (understanding and effective use of investment concepts and tools; reasonable assumptions;
   funding reliability under varying outcomes); Articulation (teamwork, learning, decision-making process, as far as an
   IPS shows it); Creativity and Presentation (compelling narrative, authentic team voice, original thinking, clear
   and credible communication of the contribution and its uncertainty).
Use the whole scale. A competent, correct, generic entry is a 6. Reserve 9-10 for work that would make an
experienced judge stop and reread. Penalise factual or rule errors and claims that are false (say which).

Write your results to the file named in your task, in this order:
1. A table: entry | W1 W2 W3 W4 W5 | Strategy, Client, Analysis, Articulation, Creativity | total (/100) | rank.
2. For each entry, 3 lines: the one line you remember from it (quote it, or "nothing"); its single biggest strength;
   its single biggest weakness or error. Then: advance to the Top 50 of 6,000? yes / maybe / no.
3. Sameness: which entries felt interchangeable, and what they all said.
4. Your top 3, and what exactly put each above the rest.
5. One paragraph: what the winning IPS in this field would have to do that none of these does.
