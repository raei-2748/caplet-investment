## The halfway email's tips, as checks
1. "Let your investment strategy lead": every paragraph serves the thesis.
2. "Know your client": case facts only, read closely.
3. "Use WInS to demonstrate your strategy": nothing in the IPS contradicts the frozen book.
4. "Do not focus on portfolio performance": never cite WInS gains.
5. "Explain your decisions ... why each investment belongs": each holding gets its job in a clause (Treasuries and
   iBond funds = the cheques; IBTM = the first cheque and the floor; VT = the contribution's upside).
6. "Follow the instructions": two boxes, 50 and 500 words, no charts, links, footnotes or citations.
