## Two ideas from the dig (model-tested; R06), as corrected by the huddle
- **D1. Her stock share rises as the payments are made. Not the hook; one clause at most.** At market value the
  share is 6.5-7.1% in 2028-32 and 46% in 2041, but the stock dollars Laura keeps grow only from about $24k to $41k
  (1.7x), and in 7% of paths she keeps nothing. The share rises because the bonds are spent (Kai: "a denominator
  trick"), it is not Pfau and Kitces' mechanism, and four raw-AI rivals also ladder first. It still answers a question
  Book 7 asks twice ("How will the portfolio's asset allocation and composition change as future funding needs
  approach and payments are made?"), so use the mechanism, not the number: each January one holding redeems, one
  cheque goes out, nobody trades.
- **D2. One test, three Januaries** (the pension de-risking glidepath, with Laura starting at its end state): are the
  ten payments fully bought in Treasuries we hold to maturity? If not, the next dollar closes the gap, longest-dated
  first. If so, every other dollar belongs to the contribution: the floor first, then stocks. The test acts in Jan 2027
  (47% of paths), never after the 2028 deposit (0%), and once more in Jan 2033 (the coupon re-match). It fills
  champion slot C13, the framework no draft has had. With the lock, January 2031 becomes a fourth date under the same
  rule ("once a number is said aloud, it is bought").
