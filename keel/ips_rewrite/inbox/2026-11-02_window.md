## 1. The one false line (fix before Wednesday; zero words)
**P2: "Break it open and you ruin how it works."** False for the best-known XR capsules. Adderall XR's Medication
Guide (Takeda, revised 04/2026): "If you or your child cannot swallow the capsule whole, open it and sprinkle the
medicine on applesauce." Effexor XR (Pfizer, revised 8/2022): "The capsule may also be administered by carefully
opening the capsule and sprinkling the entire contents on a spoonful of applesauce", beside "do not divide, crush,
chew, or dissolve". What the labels forbid is crushing; ISMP Canada gives the reason, "released all at once", and says
it of tablets, which is what a caplet is. Proof: `../../rooms/window/W-07_the_capsule_you_may_open.md`.

**Offer (497 -> 495), Kai's repair of the Window's first try:** "To a pharmacist, XR means extended release: a caplet
that delivers its dose on schedule, labelled do not crush. Crush it and the schedule is gone." A real extended-release
caplet carries the label ("Swallow whole; do not crush, chew, split, or dissolve", TYLENOL 8 HR Arthritis Pain
extended-release caplets). "The schedule is gone" is true of the pill (no timed release) and of the ladder (sold early,
no rung per January), and with "one redeemed for each January" in the next sentence, "Do not crush." at the end says
what it costs with no explainer, which both V52 cold readers asked for. The Window's first version, "Crush it and the
whole dose arrives at once." (497), lost: ISMP says "a large amount", and a ladder sold early pays the market's price,
not the cheques.
