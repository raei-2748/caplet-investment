## The one idea, and the opponent (the Marks move)
One idea: Laura's ten cheques are ten dated bills, so they are bought outright with the money she holds today, and
only the second deposit is allowed to hope. The opponent is the growth-first investor, and it is us: paragraph 1
gives that case at full strength in three short declaratives (six years away; thoughtful risks all her life; stocks
beat bonds over six years), then "So our first plan put three-quarters of her money in the stock market". Paragraph 2
is the turn ("Then we ran it through our model") and the dismantling: the flaw was the calendar, not the return. The
piece ends on what we will do (the reserve runs itself down; the fixed order; the pill).

## Content items cut, and why
- **5, the early-cash leak** (coupons and two early rungs earn less if yields fall; priced and set aside in 2031).
  A second limit after the first-plan confession; the guide allows one per piece, and item 4 already carries the
  yield-fall risk. Belongs in the Final Report with its number.
- **6, capacity not willingness** (the residency as the big bet; income from advances, speaking and licensing shrinks
  in the recessions that make bonds dear; so the 2028 deposit funds hopes). The hardest cut: it is the most
  Laura-specific idea in the list and the best Client Knowledge sentence. It cost about 45 words told properly, and
  the alternative was squeezing three other paragraphs. One trace survives in paragraph 1 ("She has taken thoughtful
  risks all her working life") as the opponent's point. If a reviewer wants it back, the trade is item 4's second
  half (the $125,000 clause and "one borrower, on purpose", about 30 words) plus the Taiwan-dollar bracket.
- **8, the price honestly** (60/40 usually leaves her almost $200,000 richer by 2042; about one in three lands below
  its range or misses a cheque, M7). A second model result against a second alternative; the first-plan paragraph
  already plays the opponent and admits the cost in one number (one in thirty). Final Report.
- **10, the capped range's catch** (on naming day the promised half moves into Treasuries; 97 in 100, L-13). A good
  mechanism but a third layer of moving parts, and its 97 is honest only with the whole mechanism beside it. Final
  Report, where the range is actually set.
- Also dropped in the cutting: the "promise gets a shopping list, hope gets a market" opening paragraph (its content
  moved into line 1 and paragraph 5); the "no exit strategy, because the exit is the date" line (the same idea as
  "nothing is sold before it pays"); "to pay the one that is due" after "forces a sale at the bottom" (redundant once
  the ten one-year bills are on the table); the headings.
