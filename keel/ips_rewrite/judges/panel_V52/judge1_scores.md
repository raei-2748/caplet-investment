## My overall view
The best entries were the ones I could retell at dinner. Entries 4 and 6 both put Laura's promise first, buy it with bonds that mature on the payment dates, and let the rest grow in one broad fund, and both say clearly what changes in 2031 and 2033. Entry 6 wins for me because it lays out the changes as steps and says what a bad market does in one clear sentence. Entry 1 is the most memorable and has the best sense of Laura as a person, but its metaphor and bank comparison made me work, and its reinvestment risk was never sized for me. Entry 3 is the most careful on paper and I could not follow it, which for a judge who reads each entry once is a loss. To win, the best entry would need a plain story that carries the whole plan, one clear sentence on how confident the team is in the range and what would move it, and a short account of what the team learned on the way. Fewer terms and more examples would help it more than another number.
