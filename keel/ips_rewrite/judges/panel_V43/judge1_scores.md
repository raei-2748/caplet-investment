## My overall view
The best entries were the ones I could repeat to a friend without a glossary. The three entries built around the same idea (pay the ten cheques from bonds that mature in time, then grow a capped-range flexibility fund) were close, and what separated them was whether they told me, in plain words, what the promise really depends on and what the range really means. Entry 4 did both, with a clear definition of "high certainty," a clear explanation of the name, and a client story that made sense. Entry 3 has the most careful risk thinking but I could not follow its mechanism, and Entry 6 had the most memorable idea but no answer to the question Laura actually asked. To win, the best entry would need to keep the plain story of Entry 4, state each number it relies on with a short reason, and show the range Laura would actually name in 2031 with the confidence it has.
