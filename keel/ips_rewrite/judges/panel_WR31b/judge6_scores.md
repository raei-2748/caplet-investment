## My overall view
The best entry (5) wins because it answers the question the IPS is for: what is promised, how sure the team is of it, and what Laura can give, with the reasoning and trade-offs on the page. The Caplet XR entries have the clearest voice and the best client fit, but Entries 2 and 6 are the only ones that give a dollar scale and a cost to the choice, and Entry 1 gives neither. Entry 3's staged facility design departs from the case's timetable and leaves the range top unclear. For Entry 5 to win outright, it would need the 2031 dollar range in one sentence, its key assumptions in a line each, the CPPI mechanics cut to what Laura needs to understand, and some account of what the team learned along the way.

One observation for whoever reads these: Entries 1, 2 and 6 share the same 50-word pitch and most of the IPS word for word. I scored each on its own text, but a judge would ask why three teams sent the same pitch, and that costs all three on original thinking and authentic team voice.
