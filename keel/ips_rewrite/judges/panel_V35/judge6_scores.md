## Ranking (1 = best)
1. Entry 5: answers all five of Wharton's questions in one line of logic, names the one real leak in the commitment and prices it, and backs its tradeoffs and 2031 range with numbers.
2. Entry 3: the same architecture as Entry 5 with the most quantified portfolio analysis, but it overstates how safe the cheques are.
3. Entry 2: the clearest answer to the question of protecting the payments first and then the facility, with a stated shortfall order and an explicit tradeoff, but thin on numbers.
4. Entry 4: sharp reasoning on correlation and the coupon leak, but the mechanics of the floor and the range are hard to follow and the range confidence is not stated.
5. Entry 6: well organised and the most explicit about assumptions, but conventional, and it promises a confidence level it never gives.
6. Entry 1: a clever venture-staging idea that carries more of the text than the portfolio does; thin on analysis and on the 2031 range, and it moves the facility decision outside the case's timeline.

## My overall view
What separated the best from the rest was the combination of an ordered set of priorities, a stated tradeoff, and numbers that stand behind the 2031 range. The best two entries (5 and 3) both make the promise and the hope separate things, show why the stock-heavy first plan failed, and quantify what the alternative would have bought Laura; they differ only in that Entry 5 names its one real leak and prices it. Entry 2 is the cleanest on sequence and client fit but gives the judges too little to test. The middle group has real insight (Entry 4's correlation point, Entry 6's assumptions) but each drops a required element: a stated confidence level, a clear mechanism, or a sense of Laura as a specific person. Entry 1 is the most inventive but spends its words on the analogy. To win, the best entry would need to state the confidence level for the 2031 range and show how it was reached, give the size of the coupon set-aside, and tie the facility decision to Taiwan costs and Laura's own income in one place.
