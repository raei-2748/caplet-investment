## My overall view
The best entries were the three that tell one story from the promise to the gift: Entries 2, 4 and 1 all use the same plan, so the question was which one explains it best. Entry 2 won for me because it answers the questions Wharton asks in the order I needed them, names the way the range can go wrong, and gives Laura's real situation (her risk is already used up by the residency) rather than only a slogan. The middle group (6, 3, 5) each had one strong idea I could repeat, but each left a required piece thin: a range confidence number, a 2033 contribution timed correctly, or a rule I could follow once. To win, an entry needs the Entry 2 structure with a plainer explanation of the leak and the range catch, a stated confidence level for the 2031 range, and a team voice that sounds like they had to work it out.
