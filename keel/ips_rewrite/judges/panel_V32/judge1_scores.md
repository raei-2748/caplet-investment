## My overall view
What separated the best from the rest was whether I could explain the entry to a friend after one read, and whether it answered the question of who the number is for. Four of the six (Entries 1, 3, 4 and 5) are close relatives of the same plan, with the same promise-first logic and the same "XR" framing, so their differences come down to how clearly each explains the 2031 range and the certainty test. Entry 5 explained those best, but still reads as one of a pack. Entry 2 is the easiest to follow but says the least that is specific about 2031. Entry 6 stood out because it turned the 2031 range into a story about co-sponsors, and that is the part of the brief I can picture being judged in front of a room. To win, the best entry would need the key sums in one place (the two deposits, the reserve size, the floor and the top in dollars), a plain statement of the trade-off with its basis, and the cleverness cut back so the reader can follow every step on one read.
