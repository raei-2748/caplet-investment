## My overall view
The field separated on whether an entry could say exactly where the promise breaks and what that does to the gift. The two strongest did three things the rest did not: a principle that every rule visibly follows, a stated certainty standard with the conditions under which it fails, and a 2031 range that already covers the reinvestment gap before the range is quoted. The cleanest entries (5 and 6) were easy to follow but generic on Laura and thin on evidence, and Entry 5 is missing the one confidence figure the case asks for. Entries 1 and 3 had good reasoning but each has a gap a programme judge would catch: no defined certainty in one, and a range top missed a quarter of the time in the other. Several entries share a pitch or much of their architecture; if that reflects a shared template, the originality credit should be read with that in mind. To win, the best entry would need dollar sizing for the reserve, the floor and the gift, one stated confidence figure for each end of the range, an explicit statement that the 2028 deposit is correlated with recession risk, and no repeated lines.
