## My overall view
The entries separated on three things: whether the IPS answered the question Wharton asked in its own terms (central idea, principles, the balance of growth, risk, liquidity and flexibility, how the plan changes as payments approach, and how the gift is protected), whether the 2031 range came with a stated confidence level, and whether the trade-offs were argued rather than asserted. Entries 1, 3 and 4 are the most enjoyable to read and share one plan, but their account of how the top of the range is protected is the weak point. Entry 2 is less pleasant to read, but it is the only entry that states its assumptions, its confidence level and the risks it accepts. To win, the best entry would need a clear explanation of what the range top is backed by, a plain statement of what the growth sleeve adds for Laura, and a reason for this client that goes beyond a single line.
