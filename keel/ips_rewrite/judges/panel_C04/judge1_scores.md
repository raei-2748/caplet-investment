## My overall view
Entries 3 to 5 read like one plan in three versions: the same pitch, the same payment ladder and the same 2028 floor. Their strength is that they argue with themselves. The rival is named, the cost is in dollars, and I can see the reasoning. Their weakness is that the 2031 range is described rather than sized, and several sentences were beyond me. Entry 6 comes out on top for me because it covers all five questions, names a confidence level, lists its own risks, and I could follow its logic even where the mechanics lost me. To win, the best entry needs to say in plain numbers what Laura can promise in 2031 and how sure the team is of it. It should keep the honest rival comparison from Entries 3 to 5 and cut the jargon and the repeated lines.
