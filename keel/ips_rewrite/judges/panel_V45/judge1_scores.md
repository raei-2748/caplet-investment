## My overall view
What separated the best from the rest, for me, was whether I could explain the plan to a friend after one read. Entries 5, 2 and 4 (the same plan in three versions) did that best: they tie the Treasury ladder to Laura's own risk, show what the alternative would have done, and use plain words. Entry 6 had the best story and the weakest numbers. Entry 1 is the most rigorous and the hardest to follow, and Entry 3 is the easiest to follow but says the least that is new. Entries 2 and 4 read as near-copies of each other, and I judged them only on what I read. For the best entry to win a judge like me, it needs a dollar range for 2031 with its confidence stated as one number, a plain sentence on what the 2028 deposit is for, and fewer unexplained figures. None of the six has all of that.
