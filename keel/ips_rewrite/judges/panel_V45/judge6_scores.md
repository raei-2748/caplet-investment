## My overall view
Renee's test is whether each entry answers the question Wharton asked, with reasons Laura would accept. Entries 1, 2 and 5 do this in the most readable way, and they share one plan, so the comparison among them comes down to detail. Entry 2 wins that comparison because it says what the 2031 range is for, puts a dollar figure on the shortfall, and reads Laura's own risk most closely. Entry 3 has the best numbers and the clearest discipline, but it is harder to read once and says less about the client. Entry 4 is sound but leaves its 2031 floor untested. Entry 6 has the best idea about flexibility but no numbers to test it with. To win, the best entry would need the Entry 2 client reasoning, the Entry 3 stated confidence at both ends of the range, a sized 2031 floor that survives a fall in stocks, and a short account of how the team's own view changed.
