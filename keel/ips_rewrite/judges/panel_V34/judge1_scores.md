## My overall view
The best entry is the one I could explain to a friend in one breath and still picture after a day: Entry 2 wins on that test because the images, the numbers it tested and the tailoring to Laura all point the same way, and its candour about the capped range is what a co-sponsor would trust. Entries 1 and 4 are behind it for different reasons: Entry 1 has the best story for raising money but very little evidence behind it, and Entry 4 has nearly the same plan with less of the reasoning. What separates the rest is clarity: the clearest plans (Entry 3) were generic, and the most original ones (Entries 5 and 6) were too dense to land on one read. To win, the best entry would need to keep Entry 2's vivid ideas and reasoning but say each one in plain words, and state the confidence level and the result of the tests it claims to have run, so a reader can see how much the promise can be trusted.
