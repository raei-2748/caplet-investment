### Entry 3
- Investment Strategy 8/10, Client Knowledge 8/10, Portfolio Analysis 6/10, Articulation 5/10, Creativity & Presentation 8/10, Overall 7/10
- Top-50-of-2,300? yes
- The same architecture as Entry 2, argued in its own way. Its main contribution is the case about concentration: ten bonds from one government are not ten independent safeties, and a fixed-dollar withdrawal turns a crash into a forced sale. It deals with the hard points directly: the risk in the 2028 deposit, the coupon-reinvestment leak settled in 2031 with a cushion and a rule that anything uncovered comes off the range before it is quoted, and a reserve that is topped up once and then only shrinks. The weaknesses are evidence and precision. It reports almost no model results, so a judge has to take its conclusions on trust; it describes the range ends as sure "as their miss is costly" without a confidence level; and it uses a loose term for the floor holdings where the instrument should be named. The team's first plan is used as evidence, which helps Articulation a little.
- Did not understand: what "the first cheque's fund" refers to, and whether it is the same holding as the floor or a separate one; and who "every team" is meant to be in the opening framing.

- The strongest client and story fit in the set. ## My overall view
What separated the best from the rest was whether each entry could show its reasoning rather than only assert it. The top two set a clear rule (the promise is bought first and the growth is not allowed to touch it) and backed it with figures a judge could test, and both admitted a trade-off, most clearly that a capped range gives up upside on the day it is named. The middle entries had sound structures and good client points but told the judge the conclusion without the numbers or the confidence level behind it; Entry 6 had the best client fit but was let down by the thin portfolio side. Entry 1 is coherent but shallow. To win, the best entry would need to keep Entry 2's evidence and reasoning, add an explicit confidence figure for the 2031 range, and show how the team arrived at its design, which is the part of Articulation the IPS currently lacks.
