## My overall view
The best entries gave one idea a non-specialist could repeat. Entry 4's roots and branch and Entry 1's one promise and one hope both did that, and both tied the plan to Laura's own risk: her income falls in bad years, and the artists would bear a missed payment. Most of the others had the right pieces (a bond ladder for the ten payments, growth above it, a range for 2031), but they lost me where several rules were folded into one sentence, especially in the 2031 range and the 2033 contribution. To win, the best entry would keep its single picture and then explain, once and in plain words, how the range is set and what a bad year does to Laura's payments and her gift. A clean one-page explanation would earn more from me than another rule, and stray notes or unexplained acronyms cost it trust.
