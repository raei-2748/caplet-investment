## My overall view
The best entries did two things a friend could repeat: they said what the plan is, and they said what it costs to choose it over the alternative. Entries 2, 4 and 6 were the same plan, and on one read I could not reliably separate them; Entry 2 stood out for its complete account and its order for bad outcomes. Entry 1 was the one I would remember by name, but it was thin on numbers and on how confident the range is. Entry 5 may be the most careful thinking in the batch, but the mechanics buried the idea and I lost the plan. To win, the best entry would need a confidence figure for the 2031 range, a plain dollar picture of a bad year, the rival argument kept, and every term explained in ordinary words.
