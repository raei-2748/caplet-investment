## My overall view
What separated the top entries from the rest was a team that tested its own idea against a serious alternative and told me what it costs, with numbers I could check against the case. Entries 6 and 2 do that best; Entry 1 does it differently through its list of accepted risks. The entries in the middle and at the bottom either give a standard answer without proof (3) or put the weight on a theme instead of the analysis (5). I distrust how finished Entries 2, 4 and 6 sound, and I would want to hear the students explain the 2031 mechanics aloud before believing they wrote every line. Still, the thinking is on the page, and that counts for more with me than plainness alone. The best entry would win by showing its assumptions a little more openly (what drives the return and rate paths behind the percentages), by stating its confidence in the bottom of the range as clearly as the top, and by sounding less like a slogan and more like students describing what they found.
