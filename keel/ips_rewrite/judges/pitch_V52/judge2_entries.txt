===== Pitch 1 =====
Laura has promised her residency $50,000 every January from 2033 to 2042. Caplet XR buys those cheques outright in 2027, as ten Treasury holdings that each mature before a January. Her 2028 deposit buys a Treasury floor for her building gift, and the interest it should earn buys world stocks.

===== Pitch 2 =====
Laura's ten $50,000 cheques are a promise, so we don't invest for them. We buy them. Her first $300,000 buys Treasuries that each mature before a January. Her 2028 deposit buys a Treasury floor for her building gift, and only the interest that floor should earn goes into world stocks.

===== Pitch 3 =====
Laura's ten $50,000 cheques are a promise, so her first $300,000 buys them outright: Treasuries maturing before each January. Her 2028 deposit buys a floor for the building gift, and only its expected interest goes into stocks. When she names a range in 2031, every dollar is already in bonds.

===== Pitch 4 =====
Laura's 2031 promise to co-sponsors should be the floor her portfolio defends. Treasuries bought in 2027 pay all ten $50,000 payments with near-certainty. The 2028 deposit runs a stock-or-Treasury rule that protects her announced minimum. Anything earned above it stays hers, preserving flexibility.

===== Pitch 5 =====
Laura promised her residency ten yearly cheques of $50,000 and hopes for a gift. Caplet XR doesn't invest the promise. Her first $300,000 buys it, in Treasuries maturing before each January. Her 2028 deposit buys Treasuries that repay it in 2032, and what's left buys world stocks for the gift.

===== Pitch 6 =====
Laura's ten $50,000 payments are a promise, so we buy the promise first: a ladder of US Treasuries, each maturing just before a payment, held to the end. Only money left over is invested for growth in one global stock fund, giving co-sponsors a guaranteed minimum and an honest upside.

===== Pitch 7 =====
Caplet XR releases Laura's money on schedule: ten Treasuries, one redeemed before each January payment. Her 2028 deposit builds a floor for the facility gift and buys world stocks with the expected interest. Stocks move the ceiling. Never the floor.
