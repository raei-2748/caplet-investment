## My overall view
The best entries were the ones I could explain to a friend after one read. Entry 3 won because it breaks the plan into a few plain principles, says what "high certainty" means in ordinary words, and tells Laura's story in one line, without leaning on a cute metaphor to carry it. Entry 2 was close behind with clear headings and a concrete growth mix. The Caplet XR group (Entries 4, 5 and 6) had a memorable name and a useful client point about the 2028 deposit, but the metaphor and some compressed sentences made it harder to follow, and those three read so alike I could hardly separate them. Entry 1 had a sound shape but buried its growth rule under terms I could not translate. To win, the best entry would need to keep Entry 3's plainness and add a short, concrete answer to how the 2031 top of the range is worked out and what the growth mix would do in a bad year, in plain numbers.
