### Entry 2
- Investment Strategy 7/10, Client Knowledge 7/10, Portfolio Analysis 8/10, Articulation 6/10, Creativity & Presentation 6/10, Overall 7/10
- Top-50-of-2,300? maybe
- This is the one entry with a different idea. It uses a rule-based stock/Treasury mix (CPPI) with a multiplier of three, and it states its assumptions, simulation and risks, including gap risk and cash-lock. That is real honesty about trade-offs. ## My overall view
Four of these six are the same plan with different amounts of reasoning around it, so what separated them was how much the writers showed their thinking and how staged the voice felt. I rank the entries that explain why they rejected alternatives (pension-style matching, "shares are safe long-term", portfolio insurance) above those that just announce a clever design. Entry 2 is the only truly different idea and the most honest about its risks, but it reads like jargon-heavy consultancy. Entry 3 is plain but unremarkable. The best entry would need to keep Entry 6's reasoning, drop the tablet stunt or at least cut it down, give one number-backed sense of how likely the shortfall and the range are, and sound a bit more like teenagers who did the work.
