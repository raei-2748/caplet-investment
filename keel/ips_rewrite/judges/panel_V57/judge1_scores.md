## My overall view
The best entries answered the question I kept asking: what does Laura get, and what does she give up? Entry 3 and Entry 6 both made that easy. Each one said plainly that the cheques are safe because Treasuries are already bought, that the growth money can fall without touching them, and what the 2031 range is built on. Entries 1, 2 and 5 are the same plan, and the difference between them is how clearly they explain the one real weakness, the reinvestment risk. Entry 4 may be the most rigorous, but rigour I cannot follow does not help me recommend it. To win, the best entry would need the Entry 3 or Entry 6 clarity, plus a stated confidence number for the 2031 range and a short, plain explanation of how big the reinvestment gap could be. Those two additions would make me comfortable recommending it to a friend and to Laura.
