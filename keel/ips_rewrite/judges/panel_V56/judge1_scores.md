## My overall view
Most of these entries have the same bones: Treasuries bought in 2027 to pay the ten cheques, the 2028 deposit set to a floor and then into world stocks, and a 2031 range that starts at the floor. What separated the best from the rest was how easily I could explain it afterwards. Entries 1 and 3 gave me one idea I could repeat. Entry 2 has the most numbers, but it lost me in its own vocabulary, which I think a Shark Tank style judge would also find hard to follow. Almost every entry is vague on the top of the 2031 range, and almost every entry leaves the team's own story out. To win, the best entry would need one memorable idea, a plain statement of how wide the range is and how likely the top is, the reinvestment risk named and sized in dollars, and one real sentence about what the team learned.
