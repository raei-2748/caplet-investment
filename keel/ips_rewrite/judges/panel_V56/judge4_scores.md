## My overall view
The three Caplet XR entries share a plan that is genuinely thought through for this client and admits where it can fail; that honesty about reinvestment risk, and linking Laura's variable income to the structure, is what separates them from the rest. Entry 2 is plainer and credible but thinner; Entry 3 is correct but generic; Entry 5 shows the deepest analysis but may overreach. The best entry would win by keeping its reasoning and trade-offs while loosening the branding and saying more in plain, student-sounding words about inflation and how confident they are in the range.
