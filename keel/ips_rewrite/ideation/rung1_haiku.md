# Rung 1 (wide): 60 motifs for the Caplet IPS and Final Report

Brief: one unifying motif (used sparingly) and a strategy name from it. Each motif is mapped against four parts:

- **P1** Jan 2027: all $300k buys ten Treasury holdings, latest-dated first, held to maturity, one matures per payment year (2033-2042).
- **P2** Jan 2028: the $150k deposit fills any gap first; a floor fund matures Dec 2032 and grows to the remainder; about $32k goes into one global stock index fund, untouched until 2033.
- **P3** 2031: a range for the 2033 gift, from the floor to floor + half the stock fund. 2033 gift = floor + half, capped at the 2031 top; the other half stays with Laura.
- **P4** 2033-2042: the ladder becomes the operating reserve, one holding per year.

Mechanism check: the brief says the plan's residual risk is reinvesting coupons. That is true only for coupon-paying holdings. A motif that talks about "coupons" must not be used if the holdings are zero-coupon. Individual Treasuries with coupons are what the WInS rules allow, so the coupon framing stands, but the team should confirm the holding types in WInS before any draft uses a coupon image.

Banned motifs (roots, trees, branches, gardens, seeds, keels, sails, ladders as the motif, building, foundations, scaffolding, bridges, safety nets, ink, pages, manuscripts, chapters, comic panels, print runs, anchors, lighthouses, compasses, North Stars, journeys, chess, water licences, Firm Yield, dry-year rules, Pre-Registered, Press Day) were not used. Ballast, keel-type images and water-licence ideas were avoided on purpose.
