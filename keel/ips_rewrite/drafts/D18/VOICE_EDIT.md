Where it goes off, it is for one of three reasons. **(a) Pronoun squeezes.** Two sentences new to this version save a
word by swapping a noun for "it", and the ear loses the thread (6, 9). That is the squeeze Book 10 forbids: keep the
small words, cut whole ideas. **(b) Two thoughts in one breath.** A sentence holds a build and its landing together,
joined by "and", ";" or ", but", so the landing never lands (14, 18, 22, 29). His habit is the reverse: let the long
one run, then put the point in a short sentence of its own. **(c) One accountant's word.** "the whole remainder" (19),
the phrase cold readers on panels V31, V34 and V37 kept tripping on.

## The three changes that would most raise how human and distinctive the whole piece sounds (each costs no words)

1. **Answer the certainty question short (14).** "In our model, only two things break a cheque. The US government
   defaults. Or …" The question in 13 is the case's own phrase and the IPS's central claim, and at the moment its
   answer is the longest sentence in the piece. The four-word deadpan line is his most recognisable rhythm, and
   nowhere does it buy more. V34 offered it too; it is still the biggest single gain. (33 → 33.)
2. **Give the opponent a full stop (22).** "… a 60/40 portfolio usually leaves her richer. But it breaks a promise (a
   cheque, or the gift she named) about one time in three." It is the piece's only voiced opponent. Allowing it to be
   right for one sentence before "But" is his signature concede-then-turn, and "she named" fixes a referent that
   currently has the portfolio naming the gift. (34 → 34; 35 → 35 split on hyphens.)
3. **Say "all of it", not "the whole remainder" (19).** "Most of it buys a floor: Treasuries that grow back into all
   of it by late 2032." It replaces the one phrase every cold-reader panel tripped on with a small piece of
   wordplay that is also the mechanism. (17 → 16.)

Runner-up, the cheapest fix in the list: **9, "So we built Caplet XR."** (6 → 5). It mends a pronoun that currently
names the crash, not the plan.
