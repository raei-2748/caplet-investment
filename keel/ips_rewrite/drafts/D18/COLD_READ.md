5. P5 says the coupon money is set aside in 2031, "before she names anything", but the residual miss in P9 is the same

answer to "a high degree of certainty"). Word-neutral (32 to 31 words).**
Now: "In our model, a cheque fails only if the US government defaults, or if yields drop two points before we buy and her
2028 deposit comes in under about $125,000 instead of $150,000."
Replace with: "A cheque fails only if the US government defaults, or her 2028 deposit can't fill the gap: if yields fall
two points by January, our model says it needs about $125,000."
Why: "only if" is no longer false (the failure is the deposit not filling the gap, and the $125,000 is that frontier at
-2pt, per A25). "Before we buy" becomes a date. "Our model" no longer claims to model a default. And "the gap" now
appears before P6 uses it, which softens the "buys all ten" clash.
