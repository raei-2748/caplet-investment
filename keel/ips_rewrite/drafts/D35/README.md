## The habits that made V54 sound like a machine (and what V55 does instead)

| Habit | Example from V54 | V55 |
|---|---|---|
| Slogan flips | "we don't invest for them. We buy them." | "so we pay for them up front" |
| Two-word kickers at the end of a paragraph | "Shares don't expire." / "Crush it and the schedule is gone." | Cut; the paragraph ends on its last real point |
| "That is X." staccato | "That is her floor." "That is under a tenth of her money." | Folded into the sentence that needs it |
| Announcing instead of saying | "The weak point is reinvestment." then explaining | Kept once, but the explanation follows in normal sentences |
| Stilted asides | "That, to us, is what ..." | "That is what we mean by ..." |
| Aphorism openers | "Laura's career already behaves like a stock." | "Her income is a second reason to keep shares away from the payments." |
| Metaphor explained twice | "To a pharmacist, XR means ... To us, X is ten ..." | One sentence for the tablet, one for X and R |
| One-line final paragraph | "Do not crush." | A full sentence that pays off "do not crush" |

The rule underneath all of these: a human paragraph explains and then stops. It does not wind up for a punchline.
