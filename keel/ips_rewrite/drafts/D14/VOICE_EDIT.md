## The three changes that would most raise how human and distinctive the whole piece sounds (each word-neutral)

1. **Break the certainty sentence (12) into a build and a four-word beat** ("Only two things break a cheque in our
   model. The US government defaults. Or …"). It is the IPS's central claim and, at the moment, its hardest sentence
   to read. The deadpan short line is his most recognisable rhythm, and nowhere does it buy more. (35 → 35 words.)
2. **Split the 60/40 line (20) into a concession and a "But"** ("… usually leaves her richer. But one time in four it
   breaks a promise, mostly the gift."). It is the piece's only opponent. With a full stop, the opponent is allowed to
   be right for a moment before it loses, which is his signature move and what makes the argument sound like a
   person's. (29 → 29.)
3. **End on the act, not the motto.** Move the rule line up a place as "Our rules fire on dates, whatever the market
   does." so the IPS closes on "you don't crush an XR pill … so the reserve runs itself down". The image returns once
   at the end and the slogan disappears from the line the judge reads last. (9 → 9, sentences reordered.)
