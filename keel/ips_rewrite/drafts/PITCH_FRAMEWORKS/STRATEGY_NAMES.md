# Strategy name brainstorm — AI DRAFT (Claude, 4 Nov 2026)

Current name: Caplet XR (V57). Brainstorm for the human-capital version of the strategy.

## Shortlist
1. **Ballast** (recommended). Her entrepreneurial career is the sail and catches the wind; the portfolio is the
   weight low in the hull that keeps the boat upright when the wind turns. Names the human-capital insight in
   one plain word.
2. **Steady Roots**. A callback to her memoir *Messy Roots* (case p.1): the residency gets steady roots.
   The most client-specific; risk of reading as cute.
3. **Caplet XR** (keep). Extended release, "do not crush" = hold every Treasury to maturity. Describes the
   mechanism well, not the career insight. V57 is already written around it.

## Also considered
- The Other Half: the half of her wealth that does not move with markets.
- Counterweight: same idea as Ballast, less vivid.
- Sail and Ballast: the full metaphor; two words longer.
- Ten Rungs / The Promise Ladder: the ladder only; generic.
- Floor and Window: the floor plus the shares; mechanical.
- Opposite Number: the portfolio as the career's opposite; too clever by half.

## Note
In finance, a "caplet" is an interest-rate option, one piece of an interest-rate cap. Our plan explicitly
refuses the option a capital-protected note buys. A judge could read that as a neat irony or a slip; if the
name stays, the team should know it.
