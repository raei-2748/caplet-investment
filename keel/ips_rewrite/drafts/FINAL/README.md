**What the plan says.** Laura makes two commitments: the ten $50,000 operating payments, and the credible range she
names in 2031. Each is bought in Treasuries before anyone relies on it. Her first $300,000 buys one Treasury holding
per payment, each kept until it is redeemed (the X and the R). In 2028 she buys more of the fund behind the 2033
payment, enough to give back about what is left of that deposit by late 2032; that sets the bottom of her range, and
the small difference buys world shares. In 2031, before she names the range, she buys extra Treasuries so the payments
would be met even if waiting money earned nothing (half her shares pay for that), then sells the other half to set the
top. She gives what those Treasuries pay, capped at the top.
