## The case in brief (official: `competition/official/2026_27/Laura_Gao_2026_Client_Profile.pdf`)
- **Deposits:** $300k at the start of 2027 and $150k at the start of 2028. The 2028 money comes from "publishing
  advances, speaking engagements, licensing, and other entrepreneurial ventures". No other money comes in.
- **Payments:** ten fixed nominal $50k payments, each January 2033-2042, funded by the portfolio "with a high degree
  of certainty".
- **The facility:** set aside an operating reserve at the start of 2033, then make a responsible contribution to an
  artists' and writers' residency facility in Taiwan, keeping flexibility.
- **The 2031 range:** in 2031 she tells co-sponsors a dollar RANGE with a confidence level, and it must protect the
  payments.
- **Permitted instruments (both contributions):** WInS-listed stocks, ETFs and Treasury bonds only. No TIPS, STRIPS,
  direct T-bills, derivatives, shorting or margin.

## The plan as it stands (V25 + the 28 Oct Courtroom)
1. **January 2027:** buy the ten payments first, with five iBond Treasury funds (IBTM-IBTR) and five Treasury bonds
   (2037-2041), each maturing before its January. This is Caplet XR: extended release, X = ten, R = redemption.
2. **January 2028:** the deposit fills any gap. It then buys a floor (more of IBTM, sized to grow back to the
   remainder by December 2032) and puts the rest, about the floor's interest, into VT (world stocks).
3. **2031 range:** the bottom is the floor (owned). The top adds half the stocks, reached about 3 times in 4. The
   range is set by weighing two costs: over-promising is judged about 3x worse than under-promising (a newsvendor
   decision). The contribution is capped at the top, and the rest stays Laura's.
4. **January 2031 ("lock what you announce", Courtroom):** the promised half of the stocks moves into Treasuries.
5. **January 2033:** one re-match sets aside the coupon-reinvestment gap. After that, the reserve "changes itself".

**What changes in the plan:** re-measure the coupon gap in January 2031, before the range is named, and keep the 2033

below the bottom from 4.9% to 0.03%, for about $160 of mean gift. Our own first idea, a 2028 hold-back, cost about $9k
of mean gift (MODEL, K07 T1-T3).
