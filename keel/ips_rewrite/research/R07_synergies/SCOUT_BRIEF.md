# Scout brief (rung 2): verify, critique, find the fusion hook

You are one domain scout. Read these first: `README.md` (the case and the plan), `COVERED.md` (don't resubmit
anything listed there) and `rung1_haiku_wide.md` (the wide sweep: its attributions are unverified, some may be
wrong or invented). Also read `../../../BIBLE.md` section "Keel's reading" and Book 7 (Wharton's IPS
instructions), so you know what the IPS must do.

**Your job:** find the 5-7 strongest ideas in your domain for THIS case. Take them from rung 1's list for your domain
numbers, from the seeds in your prompt, or from anything better you find. For each one:
1. **Verify it.** Open a real source with WebSearch or WebFetch: a paper, a regulator's page, a book page, or a
   reputable history. Give the URL and quote a short exact phrase. If you can't verify it, say so and drop it, or
   mark it UNVERIFIED. Never invent a source, a quote or a number.
2. **What it is**, in two or three plain sentences.
3. **Critique it.** What it misses or assumes, where it failed in practice, and why a professional might reject it.
4. **Fit to Laura.** Does it fit, partly fit, or seem to fit and really not? Say exactly why, in terms of the case
   (fixed nominal $50k payments, the 2028 deposit, the 2031 range, permitted instruments only, the 2033 reserve).
   A clear "seems relevant, isn't, and here's why" is as valuable as a fit. It shows judgement.
5. **Fusion hook.** Which other idea, covered or new, it combines with to make something neither has alone. Name
   the synergy in one line.
6. **Logic device**, if any: a thought experiment, a causal chain followed to its end, an analogy that holds up, or a
   case study a judge would remember.
7. **Lands in:** IPS (one clause, an idea and not wording), Final Report, or bin, with the reason.

**Rules**
- Real sources only. Numbers come from the source, or say "Jun would need to model this".
- No IPS prose. Plain English, British/Australian spelling.
- Most valuable: ideas from far-off fields that still have real investing mechanics. Name-dropping is worthless.
  Each idea must change what the plan does or how it is argued.

**Output:** write `scouts/S<n>_<slug>.md`, with one section per idea in the order above. End with a 5-line "best
synergy from my domain" paragraph. Then report back a 10-line summary.
