**Convinced me:** with payments taking two thirds of the money, stock risk hits the leftover third as if borrowed, so the facility money should not carry it.
**Question:** how is the 2028 money split, and is the bottom fixed in 2028 or 2031?

## What a fix must convey
- A s9-11: the stake is sized to grow back to the whole leftover, so shares risk only that growth.
- A s12/15, B s20: the bottom is provisional until the 2031 cover is bought.
- A s3: what "the building" is. A s15: introduce the two halves before using one.
- B s7: drop "exactly" or signal the waiting-cash weakness.
- B s2-3: why pension pricing matters to Laura, in the same breath.
- Both: why the gift can land below an owned top.
- B lacks A's leverage argument, the strongest "why" in either. B has no spare words; the pension pair is the obvious cut.

## Verdict
**Easier to follow: B, clearly.** It runs in date order; nothing stopped me. A is organised by idea, refers forward, and its key 2028 sentence lost me.
**Believed more: B, narrowly.** A has the best single argument, with a number, but I trust a plan I can check step by step, and A's "bought in 2028, lowered in 2031" made me doubt the floor.
**Pick: B**, carrying over A's leverage point.
