# Blind comparison: facts and the locked record

Fri 6 Nov (Sydney). Strict counts: A pitch 50, IPS 495; B pitch 50, IPS **500, margin 0** (the record aims for 490 because SMApply's count is unverified). `ladder_pv.py` on the 23 Oct close (last on file): $297,636, headroom $2,364 (8bp), 66.1% of $450,000 (MODEL).

## Draft A

**HALF-TRUE**
1. "once the cover is bought, no market can put a payment or a named figure out of reach, barring a US default." The draft's only absolute. Payments holds with a zero-interest cover in the same IPS (A33 deletion test: 0 cheques worse off). But the stake and five rungs are funds that "do not seek to return any predetermined amount" (casestudy pack s3), and the named bottom rests on a "margin" the draft never sizes. Reflection 1 says "near-ironclad", and A3 forbids hardening it. A fix must convey that the claim rests on the cover and margin as sized: the leftover risk is small, not nil.

**UNSUPPORTED (hedged; defensible)**
2. "a slump could also shrink co-sponsors' giving": the case never links co-sponsors to markets. "Could" is the limit.
3. "probably leave her richer by 2042": no listed source models to 2042. The repo's 2033 run backs the direction only.
4. "its interest makes up the difference": part of IBTM's return is pull to par (distribution yield 4.05% v YTM 4.94%, pack s3c). Acceptable.

**Confirmed:** "about two thirds of her $450,000" (66.1%; 63-70% within half a point); a 2027 shortfall bought by the 2028 deposit (IBTR note); one holding per payment, kept to redemption (reflection 2); the extra stake in the 2033 payment's fund (IBTM note's "two jobs"); bottom less a margin "because a fund's payout isn't fixed" (A5); "thousands of companies" (VT 10,048); waiting money leaves payments short (Note 1, Nov notes); cover before naming, half the shares first, then the stake, "lowering the bottom before she names the range" (A31, A33, stated correctly); top = bottom + half the shares moved to Treasury funds (VT note, A18); gift capped at the top; "inflation can raise what the building costs, never what she owes" (case p.3, A2); credibility (case p.3); reserve set aside without a trade (reflection 1).

## Draft B

**FALSE**
1. "Falling shares alone, before 2031, lower only the top, and rising shares lift it." B's own P5 sizes the cover at zero interest, so it is needed even at unchanged rates (A33: median $12,688, base case). The cover comes first from the kept shares, and "If that falls short, the 2032 fund covers the rest and her range starts lower." So a share fall alone can lower the bottom. The record's model lowers the range on naming day in 27 futures in 100 (A33 rule 3). A fix must convey that before naming, a share fall can lower the bottom too, and after naming nothing moves.

**HALF-TRUE**
2. "dollar Treasuries match them exactly": true for inflation, false for amount and timing. Five rungs pay NAV, "not fixed" (IBTM and IBTO notes, reflection 2), and the Feb-2037 bond matures 10.5 months early (Note 1). A11 names "exactly" as the word that breaks the NAV line, and B's own P5 contradicts it. A fix must convey a match in currency, not to the dollar.
3. "markets can change how large a range Laura names, but never whether she meets a payment or a number already named." An absolute with no default exception (prospectus: "circumstances could arise that could prevent the timely payment"), against "near-ironclad" (A3). A fix must tie it to the cover and except a US default.
4. "American public pensions are allowed to price their promises that way, and they have taken on more risk to keep up." They discount at their whole portfolio's assumed return (median 7.0%, NASRA Apr 2026, library B4a), not at "what shares usually earn", and under GASB 67 only while their assets are projected to cover benefits. The risk-taking is published (Andonov, Bauer & Cremers, RFS 2017) but not in the record. A fix must name the whole-portfolio return, with the source in the Final Report.
5. "the tenth redemption pays the last $50,000": the Nov-2041 note says "reinvested coupons funding part of each".
6. "interest and early repayments sit for months": coupons wait years (Nov-2039 note: "reinvested coupons fund the rest").
7. "as sure of landing inside it as of Treasuries": the bottom rests on a fund's payout plus a margin. With no cap stated, the gift can land above the top.
8. "the one source that won't": before 2031 her top falls with shares.
9. "Whenever Laura commits to a sum, she buys Treasuries": the bottom is bought in 2028, three years before it is named.

**UNSUPPORTED (hedged):** "co-sponsors, grants and program fees can shrink in the same recessions": not in the case. "Can" holds.

**Confirmed:** pricing at share returns "makes it look cheaper" (B4a: $250k at 7% v $292k); "one maturing before each January"; "topped up if needed" (IBTR note, A24); more of the late-2032 fund sets the minimum (A30); the world fund as the place "allowed to be wrong" (reflection 3); living costs outside the portfolio (case p.2); bottom "rounded down" (A5); top moved into Treasuries before naming (A18); cover from the kept shares, then the 2032 fund (A33); "no operating payment depends on a share price" (holds with that cover, A33 rule 1); nothing sold early from 2033 (A4).

**Both:** call the iBonds funds "Treasuries" (the IBTR note's word, A11: acceptable).

## Verdict
**A is clearly the safer draft on facts.** A has no FALSE line. Its one absolute already excepts a US default, and it states correctly the mechanism B denies (a share shortfall lowers the bottom before naming). B has one FALSE sentence, a clash with a locked word ("exactly", A11), an unqualified "never", two lines that understate the locked coupon notes, an outside pension fact only half in the record, and no words to spare for repairs.
