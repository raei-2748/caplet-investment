## CONTRADICTS (stop until fixed)
**P3: "a crash in 2029 or 2030 can shrink the top of her range but never its bottom."**
- Record: IBTM note: "giving co-sponsors a minimum figure in 2031 ... although the final payout is not fixed". The
  Archive's standing rulings C12 and A5 bar any bottom that "never" moves (FACTS never-say list: "never below",
  "the bottom never moves").
- R7 contradicts itself too. P5: "If it can't, the rest comes out of the 2032 fund, and her range starts lower." That
  is the bottom shrinking.
- How it fails: a share crash alone can't touch the bottom, because the bottom holds no shares. But crashes often come
  with falling rates (2008 brought both). Falling rates widen the ladder's waiting-money gap. P5 covers that gap first
  from the kept half of the shares, which a crash has just shrunk, and then from the 2032 fund. So the bottom falls in
  the very crash the sentence names.
- Fix: scope the claim to what is true, and drop the "never". Something like this (the Writer's words, not mine): a
  crash can shrink the top, but not the bottom, which holds no shares. Any rewording works as long as no absolute
  says the bottom can't move.

## Strains
1. **P6: "nothing in it is ever sold."** Note 1 says the Feb-2037 bond's money waits "in a short-term Treasury ETF".
   The May-2038 note says the same: "Its proceeds stay in a short-term Treasury ETF for 7.5 months". Those holdings
   are sold to pay their January. Reflection 1 only promises the bonds sit untouched until they end. Fix: "no holding
   is sold before it ends" or "nothing is sold early".
2. **P6: "markets can change how large a range Laura names, but never whether she meets a payment or a number already
   named."** This is stronger than reflection 1's "near-ironclad". The funds' payout "is not fixed" (IBTM, IBTO, IBTP,
   IBTQ notes), and P5 itself names the reinvestment weakness. It is defensible because P5 buys the waiting-money gap
   at zero earnings before naming. Book 5 says strong words may stay when they are true, so this is the Writer's call.
   Low to medium: a judge reading the TNA's "not fixed" beside "never" may notice.
