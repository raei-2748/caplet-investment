## Sentences I had to stop on (5 of 29: pitch 2, IPS 27)

1. **Pitch, s2** – "Only the interest on that minimum goes into world shares." Interest that hasn't been earned yet

2. **IPS P1, s1** – "...she buys the Treasuries that will pay it that same day." On first read "pay it that same day"
   sounded like the payment happens that same day. Moving "that same day" next to "buys" (or "on the day she commits")
   would fix it.
3. **IPS P1, s3** – "...have spent two decades taking on more risk to keep up with it." "It" still unclear. This is its
   third round as a stop.
4. **IPS P3, s1** – "...more of the late-2032 fund that pays her first January..." P2 never said that a late-2032 fund
   pays the first January, so "the late-2032 fund" refers back to something I hadn't been told. I worked it out, but I
   stopped.
5. **IPS P6, s2** – "that set-aside" is still never introduced. Fourth round as a stop.
