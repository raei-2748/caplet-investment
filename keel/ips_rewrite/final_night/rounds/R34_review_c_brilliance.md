## 2. Biggest strength: stated, half argued, barely priced

Two holes:

## 5. Packaging to cut
- **"Near-ironclad"** defines one adjective with another; the colon's mechanism is the definition.
- **"Thousands of companies"** is diversification by head-count.
- **The name line** reads as a glossary. Keep X and R, but let redemption's two senses (a bond repaid, a promise kept) meet once, at the thesis, so the name states the rule.
- **The pitch** is all mechanism, no payoff, no idea, and "a world index fund sets its top" contradicts its first sentence. It must say what the rule buys her (no number she says can be broken by markets), plus one idea, ideally the gearing turn.

- **Three halves** ("that half", "the half she keeps", "half her shares"), and "that shortfall" two sentences from its noun.

- **The close** carries four ideas, and "makes the last" is elliptical. The full circle works; the waiting-interest clause crowds it.
