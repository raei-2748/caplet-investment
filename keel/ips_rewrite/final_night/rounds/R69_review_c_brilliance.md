nothing, shares could be worth nothing, and fund payouts are rounded down. Co-sponsors can be shown it, unlike a
model's odds; that is what "credible" means.
- **Stated:** yes (pitch, first sentence).
- **Argued:** half. Missing: the case says overpromising costs more than caution, so the best promise is one she
  can't miss; and a holding can be checked, a probability can't.
- **Priced:** barely. "Probably richer by 2042" gives the cost no kind and no limit. The IPS must show the price's
  shape: she gives up share growth on each sum from the day it's secured (most on the payments, three years on the
  bottom, two on the top), and against holding only Treasuries the most the shares can cost her is the interest.
