## 2. The biggest strength

the 2031 move argue it. It is priced only against a balanced mix, plus "gives up growth". Three costs go unpriced:
- **Against all-Treasuries, the hardest question.** Shares cost her a lower certain bottom than Treasuries alone would

- **The cap on that cost.** The stake grows back to about what was left of the 2028 deposit by late 2032, so shares can
  cost at most the interest. The draft stops one clause short of its most elegant fact.
- **The 2031 move.** It gives up about two years of growth on the promised half.

The strength's strongest form is that markets change only how big her next figure is, never whether an earlier one is
kept. The draft has that as mechanism in P5, but not as its thesis, and the pitch leaves it out.
