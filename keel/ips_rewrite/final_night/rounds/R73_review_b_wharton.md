## 3. Flags

**Contradictions and ambiguity**
1. "a rise before then lifts it": "then" reads as 2033, but the shares are sold in 2031 (TNA note 3: "a rise before 2031"). Must convey that only a rise before the 2031 sale lifts the top.
2. "There are ten holdings": the plan later adds the stake, a world fund, the cover and the top's Treasuries. Must convey ten payment holdings.
3. TNA note 3: stocks "only set how far her gift rises above her promised minimum". Here a rate fall plus a share slump can cut the bottom, and paragraph 3 reads as if the bottom is fixed in 2028. Must convey the minimum is set when named in 2031, after the cover.
4. Flexibility: cover, top and bottom take nearly everything beyond the payments, yet the last paragraph promises flexibility. Must name its source (unused cover, the part of the top the building doesn't need, unsold shares).
5. "survives any market" vs "not a fixed sum": must convey the guarantee starts once the 2031 cover is bought.

**Facts**
- "the 2033 fund": it matures December 2032 (IBTM). A bond reader may take it as the Dec 2033 fund (IBTO), which the plan holds for the 2034 payment; "by December 2032" only half-fixes it.
- "about two thirds of her $450,000": checks (ladder ~$297.6k, 23 Oct close).
- "probably leave her richer by 2042": an unbased model claim, tolerable as a concession.

**Wharton's don'ts.** No charts, links, citations, detailed calculations, final range or team history. Nearest to "describing individual investments": the bond-fund mechanics sentence.

**Pitch.** Clear; "half the shares it bought" is detail a first reader can't place.
