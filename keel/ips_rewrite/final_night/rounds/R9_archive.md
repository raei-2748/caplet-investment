## CONTRADICTS
**P4: "Falling shares before 2031 can lower only the top, and rising shares lift it."**
- R8 read "A crash **on its own** lowers only the top". R9 drops the scope, so the sentence now says that falling
  shares never touch the bottom.
- P5, the reinvestment rule, makes that false. If rates have fallen, the waiting-money gap is covered first from "the
  half of her shares she kept". Falling shares shrink that half. Then "If that falls short, the 2032 fund covers the
  rest and her range starts lower". So falling shares together with falling rates do lower the bottom, and crashes
  and rate falls often come together (2008).
- Record: IBTM note, "although the final payout is not fixed". The Archive's standing rulings C12 and A5 bar any
  bottom that only ever moves one way. This is the same break as R7's "never its bottom".
- Fix: put the scope back, e.g. "On their own, falling shares before 2031 can lower only the top". That is the
  Writer's wording to choose; the two words "on their own" (or "alone") are what's needed. With them, the sentence is
  true: a fall in shares with no fall in rates leaves no gap to cover, so the bottom isn't touched.
