## Flags

**Contradictions**
1. Pitch: payments "are bought in 2027". IPS: "if not, the 2028 deposit buys the rest", plus a 2031 cover. The pitch must claim no more than the IPS.
2. Two bottoms: P3 "set a little under that sum", P4 "lower". One definition needed: what the stake still pays after the cover.
3. "No market can touch a payment" against Note 2's "final payout is not fixed": leave room for fund payout drift.
4. The half rule can leave P6 empty (see the professor).

**Facts**
- "About two thirds of her $450,000" checks (~$297.6k on the 23 Oct close, 66%); "current" must match the last re-run.
- "Far harder than the share market": nothing measures it, so "far" overreaches.
- "Redemptions meet the payments": later ones also need reinvested coupons (WInS Nov-2039, Nov-2041 notes); fully true only after the cover.
- "A short deposit": the case fixes $150,000; untied to her ventures, it reads as doubting the case.

**Wharton's don'ts**
- Clean on citations, charts, links, a final range, the reserve sum and co-sponsor text.
- Nearest to "describing individual investments or presenting detailed financial calculations": P3's stake mechanics ("costing less than ... about that whole amount by December 2032"). A strict judge would mark it.
- "its X and its R" is allowed, but decoding a name is not strategy.
