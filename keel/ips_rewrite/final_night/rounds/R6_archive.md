## The R5 strains
1. **Pitch, the 2027 purchase: FIXED.** "Her $300,000, topped up in 2028 if needed, buys ten $50,000 operating
   payments." This now matches the IBTR note ("the 2028 deposit would top up the ladder, leaving less for the
   facility"). P2's "If falling rates make it cost more than $300,000 ... her 2028 deposit makes up the difference"
   AGREES too.
2. **Funds' payout: FIXED.** "Held to its end, each one pays out what its Treasuries repay, whatever markets do". For a
   fund, what it pays out is what its matured Treasuries repay, which is its net asset value, and the line promises no
   par sum. So it sits within reflection 2 ("a net asset value, as opposed to a promise of par") and the "not fixed"
   notes. Don't let a later round turn this into "repays what it promised" or "exactly $50,000".
3. **The 2028 minimum framed as a commitment: PARTLY.** The IPS body is fine: "that extra sets her minimum facility
   contribution". The pitch lists it among the sums she buys "the day she commits to" them: "That deposit buys her
   minimum facility contribution". That makes 2028 the day she commits to a facility figure. On the record, the
   minimum is "a minimum figure in 2031" (IBTM note) and "her promised minimum" (VT note). So the TNA does allow the
   word "promised", which makes this NEW-but-compatible rather than a contradiction. But the case says "There is no
   predetermined facility contribution", and the Kitchen's V58 check ruled the 2028 purchase is "the range's bottom
   bought ahead, not a commitment". Fix, if wanted: let the pitch say the deposit buys the range's bottom ahead of 2031.
