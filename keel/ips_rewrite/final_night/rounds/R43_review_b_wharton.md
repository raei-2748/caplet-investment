## Flags

1. **Contradiction (fix first).** Paragraph 3 says "a slump before 2031 can't shrink it"; paragraph 4 says the cover's shortfall "comes off the range ... top first", so the bottom shrinks once the gap passes the top. A fixed version must convey that the bottom is safe from share losses but is the payments' last backstop, or why the cover cannot reach it.
2. **Pitch vs IPS.** The pitch says the first $300,000 "covers" all ten payments; the IPS admits it may not "if bond prices rose". The pitch must not promise more than the IPS.
3. **WInS coherence.** The IPS puts no shares in the 2027 money; the WInS book and TNA Note 3 already hold VT and a floor-sized IBTM (the post-2028 shape, scaled). Not a breach of the locked reflections, but the Final Report must explain it.
4. **Locked reflections:** no contradiction found.
5. **Facts and Wharton's don'ts:** nothing wrong; no calculations, citations or charts. "five bonds and five funds of same-year Treasuries" comes nearest to describing individual investments; defensible.
6. **Readability.** Paragraph 4 needs a second read: "money that waits for its payment" is unnamed reinvestment risk, and leftover flexibility could be shares or Treasuries.
7. **Trigger without direction.** "once she sees what inflation has done to building costs" never says whether dearer building moves her towards the top or the bottom.
