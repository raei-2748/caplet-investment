## 3. Flags

1. **Cover timing (must fix).** If the range can "come down" after 2031, that breaks the case's credibility warning and the IPS's own "will land in the range". A fix must say the cover is bought before naming. Since it is priced at zero, nothing after 2031 then moves the range.

3. **"Held in Treasuries" vs "a fund's payout isn't fixed".** The draft applies the fund caveat to the floor only (the PM's doubt).
4. **TNA, soft tension.** Reflection 3 says VT is "not standing between Laura and a payment deadline", yet shares fund the reinvestment cover first. They fit only because the range's Treasuries back the cover, and the fix should say so.
