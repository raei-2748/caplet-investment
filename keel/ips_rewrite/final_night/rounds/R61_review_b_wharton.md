## Flags

**Contradictions**
1. "The other half is sold for Treasuries too" vs "shares she can sell any day": read literally, no shares survive 2031. A fixed version must say shares remain only when the cover needs less than its half.
2. "repay the whole deposit" vs "return almost all the deposit": these must agree, and if the gap is the rounding or the fund's unfixed payout, say which.
3. "the bottom barely moves in a slump" vs "the range she names is smaller". "Smaller" is also ambiguous (lower or narrower?); say which end gives way first.
4. Pitch: the first $300,000 buys the payments outright; IPS: conditional. Minor.

**Facts that look wrong**
- "some of those years have no listed Treasury": true of WInS (nothing maturing Feb 2031 to Feb 2036), false of the market, where notes mature several times a year. Make it read as the competition's list.
- "each redeemed": term funds wind up at net asset value, not par (locked IBTM note: "final payout is not fixed"). "Rounded down" covers the bottom; P1 still implies par.
- "about two thirds of her $450,000" checks (23 Oct ladder about $297.6k).
