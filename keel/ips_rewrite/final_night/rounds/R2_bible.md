## The three failures that matter most
1. **B2 / I86: the biggest strength is never named.** Ray asked it in so many words. One sentence, early (end of paragraph 1 or the start of the range paragraph), should say that the strength is that Laura never names a number she does not already own, and then the range paragraph proves it. As written, a judge has to assemble that themselves.
2. **W27: 132 words over – and the cut is where the voice dies.** Cut whole ideas, never squeeze. Candidates (each goes to `FINAL_REPORT_BANK.md`): "Cash only looks safer..." (2 sentences, ~30 words); the residency-diversification clause (~25); the pensions' history clause (~20; also fixes B33); "Her 2028 deposit tops up the ladder..." can shrink to a clause by dropping the mechanism, not the words. Keep the dividend comparison: it is the best piece of logic in the draft.
3. **B9/B11/B13: the endings turn into slogans.** "dates, not decisions", "if it is to mean anything", and the "first/second/last" triad are the AI tells Ray and Ahaan keep naming. End those paragraphs on the thing itself (the redemption, the Treasuries she moved, the lower range).

Also fix: why *half* the shares (B48/W13 – one plain reason, or a rule); the thoughtful-risk opponent (W18/B14 – the natural place for the missing concession-then-"But", B12); and the three unclear phrases in B6.
