## Flags

1. **The cover is never called Treasuries.** Under the name "Caplet", a judge may read a low-rate hedge as an interest-rate floor (a banned derivative). The fix must say the cover is extra Treasuries bought outright.
2. **The pitch doesn't match the IPS.** The pitch's "Her 2028 deposit comes back as the bottom" leaves out "rest of", "rounded down" and the cover drawn from the bottom. "half the shares ... set its top" reads as top = half the shares, but the IPS has top = bottom + that half.
3. **Certainty against timing.** "no payment ever waits on ... interest rates" sits beside the deposit buying "whatever $300,000 couldn't if prices have risen". Before 2031, payments do lean on rates. The fix must say when the claim becomes true.
4. **"up to half" has no reason.** Judges want "the reasoning, assumptions, and tradeoffs".
5. **"funds included" can't be parsed.**
6. **Borderline individual-investment detail.** "Five of the ten are funds ... pay out that December": no tickers, and it carries the liquidity point.
7. **Clean on the rest.** No citations, charts, tickers, banned securities or model statistics. "two thirds of her $450,000" matches the 23 Oct ladder close (~$297.6k). Nothing contradicts the locked reflections (near-ironclad certainty, the IBTM floor, VT setting only the top, the 2028 top-up, reinvestment risk).
