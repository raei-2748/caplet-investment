Before Laura commits to any amount, Caplet XR makes sure she already holds it in Treasuries, and only money she has promised to no one goes into shares. Her ten operating payments of $50,000 come first, because co-sponsors may help pay for the facility but cannot be relied on for those. In January 2027 her first $300,000 buys ten Treasury holdings, each paying out in the year before the January it pays for, and their interest makes up the rest of the $500,000. The plan is named for those ten redemptions (X for ten, R for redemption).

The payments must be made in full whatever markets do, so in a balanced mix of shares and bonds every loss would fall on the money left for the facility, which would swing harder than the share market, just as a company's shares grow riskier as it borrows. So shares sit only where a loss costs no payment, and we read a high degree of certainty as near-ironclad: no payment rests on a share price, a forecast or a sale before maturity.

The 2028 deposit first buys whatever part of the ten the first $300,000 couldn't afford. Most of the rest buys more of the Treasury fund that pays the first January. Its extra payout, rounded down because a fund's payout isn't fixed, becomes the bottom of the range she will name in 2031, so a slump before then can lower only the top. What is left, about the interest those Treasuries will earn by 2032, goes into a world index fund, so even if every share were lost, she would get back about her deposit.

The plan's weak point is the money that arrives before its January, mostly interest, which waits in a short-term Treasury fund and, if rates fall and stay low, earns less than the ladder was sized on. In 2031, before naming her range, Laura moves half of her shares into Treasuries, and the top is the bottom plus that half. From the other half she sets aside enough Treasuries to cover that gap, as if the waiting money earned nothing, and if that half falls short, the difference comes off the top, then the bottom, before she names a number.

Owning so few shares will probably leave her less to give. But a named top left in shares would take every fall, while any rise above it only came back to her. Barring a US default, her contribution will land in the range, at a point she chooses in 2033 once the facility's cost is known.

Whatever remains of her kept shares stays outside the range as her financial flexibility, sellable on any trading day and the one part of her money with a chance to keep pace with building costs in Taiwan. From 2033 her operating reserve is everything still waiting for a payment, and it shrinks by one holding each January until the tenth redemption makes the last operating payment in 2042.
