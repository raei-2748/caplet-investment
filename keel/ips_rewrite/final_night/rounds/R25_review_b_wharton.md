## 3. Flags

1. **Contradiction.** Paragraph 3 says only the top can fall. Paragraph 4 says the bottom can fall "before she names a number". The fix needs one rule: the bottom is final only after the 2031 cover, or the cover never reaches it.
2. **Locked TNA.** Note 3 says shares "only set how far her gift rises", and its reflection calls VT "the only investment not standing between Laura and a payment deadline". Paragraph 4 draws the payment-gap cover from the kept shares (also paragraph 1's "promised to no one"). The fix must say that no payment depends on a share price, because the floor stands behind the cover.
3. **Paragraphs 2 and 4.** "No payment rests on... a forecast" sits next to "earns less than the ladder was sized on", and that sizing is a forecast until 2031. Say when the claim becomes true.
4. **Facts.** (a) Of the "ten redemptions", five are fund wind-ups paying net asset value, not par, as the TNA itself says. (b) "About her deposit" holds only if the deposit didn't top up the ladder. (c) "Barring a US default" leaves out that the funds' end dates can move. (d) If a return figure is added, note the TNA's locked "about 7.5% (as per JPMorgan)": JPM 2026 gives 7.00% for AC World (7.50% is EAFE).
5. **Wharton's don'ts.** None broken.
6. **WInS.** The frozen book holds VT and a floor-sized IBTM, which is the post-2028 plan scaled down. Paragraph 1 has January 2027 buying only the ten holdings, so the Final Report must explain the difference.
