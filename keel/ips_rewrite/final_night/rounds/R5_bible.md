2. **"Whatever markets do" now covers the funds (accuracy, new).** Two paragraphs later the draft admits falling
   rates hurt the ladder. Keep the strong wording, aim it at price ("however its price moves before then"), and the
   contradiction goes.
3. **The bridge from rule to strength (B2) and the erudition (B28).** Put the "real strength" sentence straight after
   the range's "both ends ... bought before anyone hears it", where it is proved, or open paragraph 1 with it. Spend
   the freed frame words on the dividend sentence. It is also the cheapest answer to I89's "more original insight".
