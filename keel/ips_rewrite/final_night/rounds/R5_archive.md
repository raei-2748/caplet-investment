1. **Pitch: "her ten $50,000 operating payments in 2027"** (as bought "that day"). STRAINS the IBTR note: "If yields fall
   more than about 0.2 percentage points by January, the 2028 deposit would top up the ladder, leaving less for the
   facility." The IPS body already keeps the condition ("if rates leave it short, her 2028 deposit finishes it" –
   AGREES), but the pitch states it flat, and on the 4 Nov close the ladder costs about $600 more than $300,000
   (FACTS N1). The pitch is read first and alone. Fix: any wording that leaves room for the 2028 top-up.
2. **IPS P2: "Each holding pays out at its end date whatever markets do."** STRAINS the IBTM/IBTO/IBTP/IBTQ notes ("the
   final payout is not fixed"; "Its December payout is not fixed") and reflection 2 ("their terminal payout is a net
   asset value, as opposed to a promise of par"). Five of the ten holdings are funds, so "whatever markets do" reads as a
   fixed sum for all ten. Fix: keep "whatever markets do" for the bonds only, or say the funds pay out what they hold
   when they close.
3. (low) **P6: "until the tenth redemption pays the last $50,000 in 2042."** Nov-2041 note: "with reinvested coupons
   funding part of each". The redemption pays most of it, not all. A reader would only notice beside the note; fine
   to leave if P5's reinvestment sentence stays.
