## 4. The five criteria (my read, big picture first)
- **Investment Strategy (~8/10).** Clear, creative thesis (commitments bought the day they're made; the range bought before anyone hears it); disciplined across horizons. Loses on "appropriate diversification".
- **Client Knowledge (~6.5).** The weakest. R7 never says who Laura is or why this suits her: no income source, no residency purpose, Taiwan absent. "Recommendations that can earn her confidence" is carried only by the certainty line.
- **Portfolio Analysis (~7).** Good concepts used, not name-dropped: pricing a liability at Treasury cost vs the public-pension practice, reinvestment risk with a cover rule. "Varying market outcomes" is half done (no boom).
- **Articulation of Competition Experience (~5).** Mostly a Final Report criterion; "the one place the plan is allowed to be wrong" echoes the TNA (good for the "consistent across all three" line).
- **Creativity and Presentation (~7.5).** "as confident of landing inside it as she is of Treasuries" is the best co-sponsor-credibility line yet; XR now explained and paid off at the end.
