## Flags

1. **The opening against paragraphs 3-4.** "Already holds it in Treasuries" is not true of the payments until 2031: a January 2027 gap waits for the 2028 deposit, and the interest share waits on reinvestment rates. A fixed version must say when the test is met and what stands behind the payments until then.
2. **The pitch against the IPS.** "A world index fund sets its top" reads as if a committed amount sits in shares, against the pitch's own first sentence. The IPS locks the top in Treasuries first. The pitch must say so or stop implying otherwise.
3. **"Grows back to about her whole deposit"** holds only if no top-up was needed.
4. **The "So" in paragraph 4** ties reinvestment risk to moving half the shares, but the move locks the top. The 2031 step does two separate jobs and should read that way.
5. **"Changes only as payments leave it"** skips the 2031 shortfall Treasuries joining the reserve. Minor.
6. **Describing investments.** Paragraph 3 ("the fund that pays the 2033 payment", "a fund's payout isn't fixed") explains how instruments work, the closest the draft comes to what Wharton says not to do.
7. **Trading notes.** No contradiction. One to watch: note 3 says stocks "only" set the rise above the minimum, while the IPS also makes the kept shares the first cover for the interest gap.
8. **WInS book.** It holds the world fund and an outsized 2033 holding bought from $300,000 (the 2028 picture, scaled), while the IPS says the first $300,000 is all Treasuries. The Final Report must explain this.
