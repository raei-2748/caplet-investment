## Flags

1. **Pitch vs IPS timing.** The pitch has both ends in Treasuries "Before she names" the range; the IPS sells the
   second half of the shares "That day". Both fields must give one timing.
2. **Held vs sold.** "each kept until its redemption" sits beside a liquidity reason ("easier to sell") and a planned
   sale of "part of the stake". A fixed version must say what the ease of sale is for.
3. **Looks wrong.** "funds of Treasuries maturing in one year" reads as one-year bills. It must convey that each fund
   holds Treasuries all maturing in one named year.
4. **WInS book.** The IPS buys shares and the extra stake in 2028; the frozen book has held VT and an oversized IBTM
   since October. The WInS notes say "scaled to the model portfolio", so nothing contradicts, but the IPS never says
   the book is the post-2028 plan scaled.
5. **Unused cover.** If rates never collapse, the 2031 cover is surplus; the IPS doesn't say it returns to her.
6. **Reflections.** No contradiction: certainty, the IBTM floor and unfixed payout, VT setting the top, reinvestment
   risk and nominal Treasuries all agree.
7. **Wharton's don'ts.** No tickers, citations, charts, final range or co-sponsor text. "Five are funds ..." comes
   closest to describing individual investments. "Two thirds of her $450,000", the only calculation, holds on the
   23 Oct close (ladder $297.6k, the latest on file).
