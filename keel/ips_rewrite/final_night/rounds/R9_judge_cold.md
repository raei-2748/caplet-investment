## The strategy and its biggest strength, as I understood them
**Strategy.** Whenever Laura commits to an amount, she buys Treasuries that repay it in time. Her $300k buys a ladder for the
ten $50k payments; her 2028 deposit fills any gap, then buys extra units of the late-2032 fund as the minimum facility
contribution, with roughly their interest in a world share fund. Before naming the 2031 range she moves half her shares into
Treasuries, so both ends are owned; the other half stays hers as her financial flexibility.
**Biggest strength (as stated).** Markets can change how large a range she names, but never whether she meets a payment or a
number already named.
