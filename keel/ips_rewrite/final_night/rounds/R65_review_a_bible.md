## KILL rows failed

A fixed version must say that the 2028 deposit mostly buys a Treasury holding that repays it by late 2032, which becomes the bottom, and that the small rest goes into world shares. "Never beaten" only makes sense if the reader knows a rule the draft never states: she never gives more than the top. The opening participle also has no clear subject. A fixed version must say that once co-sponsors hear the top, a fall in shares would break her word and a rise would add nothing she had promised.
