4. **Pitch, "half become Treasuries":** reads as half the deposit but means half the shares.
5. **TNA:** no contradiction. The floor sits in the 2033-payment fund (Note 2), shares only lift the top (Note 3), bonds stay untouched and reinvestment risk is named (Note 1), and the ladder top-up comes first (IBTR note).
6. **Facts:** "about two thirds of her $450,000" matches the 23 Oct close (about $297.6k). "Near-zero rates ... would leave the payments short" holds only because the ladder was priced on waiting money earning interest, which the draft never says; lay judges will ask why idle money falls short.
7. **Wharton don'ts:** no citations, charts, links, model statistics, tickers or team history. The nearest thing to "describing individual investments" is the 31-word sentence on how the five funds pay out and trade. Para 3's opening needs a second read.
