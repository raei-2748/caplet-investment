## 3. Flags

1. **False:** "each January one is redeemed ... in January 2042 the tenth redemption pays the last" contradicts para 1 and the locked notes (Dec/Feb/May/Nov maturities; Nov-2039: "comes due 47 days before"). Fix: one timing story, para 1's.
2. **Certainty overstated:** fund payout (reflection 2: "terminal payout is NAV not par") and reinvestment sit outside "only if the US government missed". Fix: the definition names what remains and how it is covered.
3. **Overclaim:** "ten years of running costs are already bought, so the building ... won't stand empty." The payments go "toward the residency's operating expenses"; support "beyond Laura's commitment" may come from co-sponsors.
4. **Unsourced:** "Shares nearly always beat Treasuries over ten years" (the US 2000s didn't).
5. **Pitch:** "puts only their interest into world shares" reads as interest swept in later; the IPS invests an equal slice up front.
6. **Top-up case:** "grow back to roughly the whole deposit" and "hand back her deposit" fail if a top-up was needed (IBTR note: "less for the facility"). Say which shrinks.
7. **Gap order:** which half covers the gap is unsaid; the floor as last backstop is implied only.
8. Minor: funds are paid out by the fund, not "repaid by the US government"; "this autumn" is spring in Sydney.
9. **Rules clean:** no TIPS, STRIPS, derivatives, margin or cash holding ("earned nothing" is a calculation). Locked record otherwise consistent. Low risk: WInS holds VT inside its $300,000 (the scaled post-2028 plan), which only the Nov-2041 note's "scaled to the model portfolio" explains.
