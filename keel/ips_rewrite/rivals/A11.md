# A11
STRATEGY NAME: Protect the Promise, Grow the Vision
## PITCH
We split Laura's money into two jobs. A Safety Bucket of laddered U.S. Treasury bonds locks in all ten $50,000 residency payments, so her promise never depends on markets. A Growth Bucket of diversified, low-cost ETFs builds her facility contribution, giving co-sponsors a credible range backed by secured commitments.
## IPS
**Objective**
Laura's portfolio has two jobs with different deadlines and different tolerances for failure. The first, ten fixed $50,000 operating payments from 2033 through 2042, is a promise and must be met with a high degree of certainty. The second, a facility contribution in 2033, is an opportunity: it should be as large as responsibly possible, but it can flex. Our strategy gives each job its own bucket, so that risk taken for growth can never endanger the payments.

**Risk and Return**
Laura has taken thoughtful entrepreneurial risks, so she has the willingness to accept volatility. Her capacity is narrower: the payments are fixed in nominal dollars, she adds no money after 2028, and her income is irregular. We therefore define risk not as volatility, but as the chance of missing a payment or overpromising to co-sponsors. Our return objective is to fully fund the payments first, then maximize the expected facility contribution with the remaining capital.

**Safety Bucket: the operating commitment**
We match the payments with a ladder of U.S. Treasury bonds, each maturing shortly before a payment date and held to maturity. Because the payments are nominal and the bonds repay a known amount, price swings between now and 2042 do not change what the ladder delivers; only a U.S. government default could. That is our definition of high funding certainty: every payment is covered by a Treasury that matures in time, assuming bonds are held to maturity. The 2027 contribution funds the ladder first, starting with the most distant payments, where today's yields buy the most. Any shortfall is completed from the 2028 contribution before anything else is invested. At the start of 2033 this ladder becomes the operating reserve. Its composition then changes naturally: each year one bond matures, pays that year's $50,000, and the reserve shortens.

**Growth Bucket: the facility contribution**
Everything above the ladder's cost goes into a diversified, low-cost portfolio of broad U.S. and international equity ETFs, with a smaller allocation to intermediate Treasury ETFs. With roughly five years to grow, equities give the best chance of a meaningful contribution, and broad diversification avoids single-stock bets. We rebalance annually back to target weights.

**Glide path and the co-sponsor range**
As 2033 approaches, the Growth Bucket shifts from equities toward short Treasuries. In 2031, when Laura approaches co-sponsors, we lock in a meaningful share of the bucket's value in Treasuries maturing before 2033. That locked amount becomes the bottom of her range, a floor she can promise without risk, while the remaining equities determine the top.

**Facility contribution and flexibility**
In 2033, after the reserve is fully funded, Laura contributes the locked floor plus part of any further gains, keeping the rest as a flexibility buffer for cost overruns and changes as the residency develops.

**Constraints**
Only stocks, ETFs and Treasury bonds available on WInS. No margin, short selling, derivatives, cryptocurrency, TIPS or STRIPS. We review the policy each year and after major market moves, but the Safety Bucket's purpose never changes.
