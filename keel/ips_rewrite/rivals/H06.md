# H06
ARCHETYPE: Values-led team with Taiwan-diaspora members; heavy on Laura's story and the residency's mission; ESG funds and a Taiwan ETF (EWT) to "align" with the facility and hedge currency; emotional writing.
COUNTRY / LEVEL: United Kingdom (London sixth form; three members of Taiwanese heritage; strong English and EPQ writers)
STRATEGY NAME: Rooted & Rising
## PITCH
Laura's roots shaped her stories; now her stories will shape a home for others. Rooted & Rising protects every $50,000 residency payment with U.S. Treasuries, grows the facility fund through sustainable global companies and Taiwan's own economy, and keeps Laura's money as true to her values as her work.
## IPS
Who we are investing for
Laura Gao's work has always been about belonging. The Wuhan I Know began as an answer to misinformation and hate; Messy Roots turned one family's story into something classrooms around the world now share. Several of us have parents and grandparents from Taiwan, and when we read that Laura plans to build a creative residency there, we did not see a cash-flow problem first. We saw a home being built for people who, like her, belong to more than one place. Our strategy tries to deserve that.

Principle 1: the promise is sacred
The ten $50,000 payments are what make the residency real. If one is missed, artists lose their place and Laura loses trust she has spent years earning. So the payments are never left to chance. From 2027 we build an operating reserve in U.S. Treasuries: short and intermediate Treasury ETFs (SHY, IEF) and individual Treasury notes maturing between 2033 and 2042. By 2033 the reserve holds enough to cover all ten payments, plus a 10% cushion that protects them against Taiwan's rising costs.

Principle 2: grow with purpose
The rest of the portfolio, the facility fund, has six years to grow. We invest it in companies Laura would be proud to own:
- 60% ESG-screened U.S. and international equity (ESGU, ESGD), excluding weapons, tobacco and the worst polluters.
- 25% iShares MSCI Taiwan (EWT). Taiwan is where the residency will live. Owning Taiwanese companies ties the fund's growth to the island's economy and gives Laura New Taiwan dollar exposure, a natural hedge as her spending moves from US dollars into Taiwanese costs.
- 15% U.S. Treasuries (IEF) to soften falls.
We rebalance once a year. We do not chase trends, and we do not abandon our values when a screened fund lags the market.

Principle 3: honesty with co-sponsors
Laura told the truth about Wuhan when it was difficult. She should speak to co-sponsors the same way. In 2031 she will share a range rather than a single number, and her contribution will come only from the facility fund, never from the operating reserve. We would rather she promise a modest figure and exceed it than make a promise the markets could break.

Principle 4: freedom to keep creating
Laura has never stopped at one project. After the reserve is set and the facility contribution made, at least a fifth of the facility fund stays invested, so a construction delay or a new idea never forces her to choose between the residency and her own future.

How the portfolio changes
2027-2030: the reserve is built steadily from both contributions; the facility fund is fully invested.
2031-2032: as co-sponsor conversations begin, the facility fund's Treasury share rises to 40%, so the range she shares stays credible.
2033-2042: the reserve pays one year at a time; what remains of the facility fund stays balanced.

Laura once wrote that the only person who needs to believe in something is yourself. Our job is to make sure that, when she believes in this residency, the numbers believe in it too.
## SIMULATOR NOTES
Does well: the most human, memorable voice in the field; genuine client knowledge and a clear "promise first, gift from surplus only" rule that judges will remember.
Slip / blind spot: the payments are fixed US dollars, yet the cushion "protects them against Taiwan's rising costs" and EWT is sold as a currency hedge; 25% in one country (the residency's own) concentrates risk, the reserve is never sized, the 2031 range has no method or confidence, and it attributes the case's pull quote to Laura's writing.
