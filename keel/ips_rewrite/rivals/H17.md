# H17
ARCHETYPE: "Bridge" metaphor team: the portfolio as a bridge from Laura's US career to the Taiwan residency (spans, pillars, load-bearing)
COUNTRY / LEVEL: US-Taiwan (Taiwanese-American and Taipei-based members, mixed grades 11-12); strong, AI-assisted, heavily edited
STRATEGY NAME: The Load-Bearing Bridge

## PITCH
We are building Laura a bridge from Texas to Taiwan. Its pillars, US Treasuries maturing in time for each $50,000 payment, carry the operating commitment no matter what markets do. Its deck, a global stock portfolio, carries the facility contribution. We never let the deck hold up the pillars.

## IPS
Purpose
Laura's portfolio has one structural job and one creative job. The structural job is ten fixed $50,000 payments from 2033 to 2042. The creative job is a facility contribution large enough to show co-sponsors she is serious, without leaving her stranded. Our strategy treats these like a bridge: load-bearing pillars that must never fail, and a deck that can flex.

Principle 1: Pillars before deck
Every dollar is first assigned to the payments. Before buying any stocks, we buy US Treasury bonds and defined-maturity Treasury ETFs that mature shortly before each payment date. Held to maturity, a Treasury pays back its face value regardless of interest rates or stock prices, so the payments depend only on the US government paying its debts. This is our definition of a high degree of certainty: each payment is matched to a specific government-backed maturity, not to a forecast. At today's 4-5% yields, most of Laura's first $300,000 builds them in 2027.

Principle 2: A safety factor
Engineers do not build bridges to carry exactly the expected load. We add a margin: if the first deposit cannot fully fund every pillar at 2027 prices, the gap is the first use of the 2028 deposit, and we hold a small extra Treasury buffer worth about one payment.

Principle 3: The deck can move
Everything above the pillars and buffer forms the deck: a low-cost global stock fund (VT), with a small Taiwan allocation (EWT) because Laura's project, partners and future costs are tied to Taiwan's economy. With six years to 2033, the deck can take equity risk; losses here can shrink the facility contribution but can never touch a payment.

How the bridge changes over time
The pillars need no rebalancing; each rung simply matures into its payment. In 2033 the remaining pillars become the operating reserve, already invested exactly as it will be spent: ten maturities for ten payments, shrinking by one each year. From 2031 we gradually move part of the deck into short Treasuries so that a market fall in 2032 cannot shrink the promised minimum.

Facility contribution and flexibility
In 2033 Laura contributes from the deck only. We recommend she commit at most about two-thirds of the deck and keep the rest liquid, because the case is clear that committing everything limits her flexibility as the project develops, and new residencies often meet unexpected costs.

Speaking to co-sponsors in 2031
Because the payments are already secured, Laura can talk about the facility honestly. Our 2031 range will start at the value already moved to Treasuries, which is close to certain, and rise with what the remaining stocks could plausibly reach. The Final Report will give the numbers and our confidence level.

Why this suits Laura
Laura built her career by taking creative risks on a stable base. This bridge gives her the same thing: unshakable pillars for her promise to the residency, and a deck free to grow toward her vision.

## SIMULATOR NOTES
Does well: clear, memorable structure where the metaphor maps one-to-one onto real mechanics (maturity-matched Treasuries, held to maturity), defines certainty explicitly, and keeps the reserve's composition change simple.
Blind spot / slip: the EWT "Taiwan tilt" is justified by costs in Taiwan although the payments are fixed US dollars (adds currency and concentration risk for no matching benefit); the "safety factor" buffer double-dips into the 2028 deposit already used to fill any gap, and the 2031 range method stays vague.
