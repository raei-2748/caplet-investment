# A10
STRATEGY NAME: Roots & Wings
## PITCH
Roots & Wings gives Laura's residency a foundation that cannot wobble and room to soar. "Roots" locks in the ten $50,000 operating payments with a ladder of Treasury bonds matched to each payment date. "Wings" invests the rest in diversified global ETFs, growing a facility contribution Laura can promise credibly.
## IPS
**Purpose.** Laura has two goals that need different kinds of money. The ten $50,000 operating payments (2033-2042) are a promise to her residency community and must be met with near-certainty. The facility contribution is an opportunity that must never come at the expense of the payments. Roots & Wings gives each job its own pool of money.

**Roots: the operating reserve.** Roots is a ladder of U.S. Treasury bonds available on WInS, arranged so one matures shortly before each payment date from 2033 to 2042. Held to maturity, a Treasury's payoff is known in advance: market swings change its price, not what it pays. We define a "high degree of certainty" as every payment being covered by government bonds, held to maturity, that mature before it is due, with no reliance on stock returns or outside money. Roots is funded first: the 2027 deposit begins the ladder and the 2028 contribution completes it, so the reserve is locked in years before 2033. As each bond matures and a payment is made, the reserve simply shrinks; it is never shifted into riskier assets.

**Wings: the growth portfolio.** Everything not needed for Roots goes into Wings, a diversified mix of low-cost, broad-market ETFs: U.S. and international stocks for long-term growth, with a smaller bond ETF position to soften drawdowns. Laura is a successful entrepreneur whose living costs are covered outside the portfolio, with a six-year horizon to 2033, so she can accept volatility in this sleeve. Spreading money across thousands of companies and many countries keeps any single stock from deciding her outcome. Low fees and transparency suit a client who values clear thinking.

**The glide path.** Wings grows more conservative as 2033 approaches. From 2027 to 2030 it stays growth-oriented. From 2031, when Laura begins talking to co-sponsors, we gradually move Wings toward short-term Treasuries and bond ETFs, so the amount she announces is protected from a late market crash. By 2033, most of Wings sits in low-volatility assets, ready to fund the facility.

**Facility contribution and flexibility.** In 2031 Laura will share a range, not a single number: the bottom anchored on what Wings has already secured in safer assets, the top on reasonable growth for what remains invested. In 2033, after Roots is confirmed, she contributes from Wings, but not all of it. We will keep a meaningful share uncommitted as a flexibility cushion for cost overruns, a slow fundraising year or new opportunities as the residency grows. Our projections use nominal dollars, matching the fixed payments, and recognise that inflation may raise facility costs, another reason to hold that cushion. A promise she can keep beats a bigger one she might walk back.

**Discipline.** We review the portfolio annually and rebalance Wings when its allocation drifts meaningfully from target. Roots is never sold to chase returns, and Wings is never relied on for the payments. Every decision answers one question: does this keep the roots secure while giving the residency room to fly?
