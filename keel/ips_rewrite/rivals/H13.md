# H13
ARCHETYPE: Inflation-obsessed team; gold, commodities, short-term Treasuries and real assets to defend real value; slips on TIPS / I-bonds
COUNTRY / LEVEL: US (Miami, public magnet school); one member born in Argentina; passionate, well-read on macro, light on the rules
STRATEGY NAME: Real Value Shield
## PITCH
Inflation quietly shrinks every dollar Laura has saved. Shaped by one teammate's family memories of Argentina, our strategy protects real value: short-term Treasuries and inflation-protected bonds secure the ten $50,000 payments, while gold, commodities, real estate and global stocks grow a facility fund that keeps pace with rising construction costs.
## IPS
Why inflation is our central risk
One of us grew up hearing how Argentina's inflation passed 200% in 2023, wiping out families' savings in months. The US is not Argentina, but in 2022 US inflation hit 9.1%, the highest in forty years, and almost every forecast missed it. Laura's plan runs sixteen years. Even 3% inflation means a 2042 dollar buys about 40% less than a 2026 dollar. Her payments are fixed at $50,000, so their real value will fall, and the facility she wants to build in Taiwan will cost more each year we wait. A strategy that ignores inflation is betting that 2022 never happens again.

Central idea
Protect purchasing power first, then grow. We split the portfolio into a Payment Shield and a Real Growth sleeve.

Payment Shield (the ten payments)
- A ladder of TIPS maturing each year from 2033 to 2042, so the principal rises with inflation and each rung pays at least $50,000.
- Short-term Treasuries (SHY, SGOV) to hold cash safely until bonds are bought and to stay flexible if rates rise. Short maturities avoid the losses long bonds suffered in 2022.
- If allowed, a small I-bond allocation each year as an extra inflation-linked layer.
- Funded first from the 2027 deposit, completed with the 2028 deposit.

Real Growth sleeve (the facility)
- Gold (GLD) 15%: holds value when currencies lose credibility.
- Broad commodities (PDBC) 10%: rose sharply in 2021-2022 when stocks and bonds both fell.
- Real estate (VNQ) 15%: rents and property values tend to rise with prices, and the residency itself is a building.
- Global stocks (VT) 60%: companies can raise prices, so shares beat inflation over long periods.
Rebalanced every year.

Operating reserve (2033)
In 2033 the Payment Shield becomes the operating reserve. As each payment approaches, the next rung matures into cash and is paid out. Because the principal is inflation-adjusted, any extra above $50,000 builds a small cushion that rolls into later years. We define high certainty as payments covered by US government bonds held to maturity, never by selling growth assets.

Facility contribution and flexibility
The facility contribution comes from the Real Growth sleeve. Because building costs in Taiwan will rise with inflation, a fund of real assets is a natural hedge: if inflation surprises, gold, commodities and property should rise too. We recommend Laura pledge about 75% of the sleeve and keep 25% invested for overruns, which inflation makes likely.

The 2031 range
In 2031 we will give co-sponsors a range in today's dollars and in 2033 dollars, so they can see the real value of Laura's pledge. The low end assumes a commodity and stock downturn; the high end assumes inflation stays high, which our real assets should benefit from.

Assumptions
Inflation averages 3% with spikes possible; real assets roughly keep pace; stocks earn about 4% above inflation; Treasury yields reflect current rates.
