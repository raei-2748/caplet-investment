# The lens deck: ways of looking at today's question

> Part of the research loop (`README.md`). Nora draws one at 07:00; it shapes the scouts' briefs and the Lab's
> pairings. No lens may be drawn twice in five rounds. Keel may add lenses (any member, any run); a lens that never
> produced a kept find in three draws is retired by the Sunday review. Ideas from Brian Eno and Peter Schmidt's
> Oblique Strategies (1975), STORM's perspectives (`README.md` sources) and the habits in Ray's essays.
