## How to pick today's three
1. One **near** field (finance) that is `unvisited` or `promising`; one **adjacent**; one **far**.
2. In the explore phase (to Thu 29 Oct) at least one of the three is `unvisited`; from Fri 30 Oct at least one scout
   deepens a champion's field instead.
3. Never three fields from the same family; never a field a scout visited in the last three rounds.
4. Each scout also walks through one of their own doors (`README.md`, "How the members go out").
5. A field that only exists because it sounds clever (no mechanism that could touch a payment, the 2031 range, the
   stock half, the reserve, the name or a risk) is not a field: cross it out with why.
