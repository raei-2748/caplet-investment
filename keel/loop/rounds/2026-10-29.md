### Amara · near · sovereign wealth funds, stabilisation vs savings · door: the gurdwara committee's two tins · Sonnet 5.5
- **A1. Chile ranks its two pots in law: the pension pot is paid first, the stabilisation pot gets what is left.**
  Mensaje Nº 199-373, President of Chile to the Chamber of Deputies, 17 Sep 2025 (bill Boletín 17.856-05, to reform
  Ley 20.128), mirrored by Diario Constitucional,
  https://www.diarioconstitucional.cl/2025/10/18/gobierno-propone-nuevas-reglas-para-fortalecer-fondos-soberanos/mensaje-boletin-no-17-856-05/,
  accessed 29 Oct 2026. Quotes (verified): the 2006 law "estableció un orden de prelación que prioriza el FRP por sobre
  el FEES" ("set an order of priority that ranks the pension fund above the stabilisation fund"); "este último fondo
  recibe solamente recursos residuales" ("the latter receives only residual resources"); the reform would split a
  surplus "un 50% al Fondo de Reserva de Pensiones y el otro 50% al Fondo de Estabilización Económica y Social", and
  needs a law when "los retiros del FEES superen el 0,3% del Producto Interno Bruto". **Breaks:** Chile's pots are fed
  by a surplus every year and the rule decides who gets it; Laura has no inflow after Jan 2028, so there is nothing
  new to allocate, only what exists to protect. The 0.3%-of-GDP trigger is pegged to an outside number she lacks. The
  reform is a bill, not law (status not checked). The law text itself (bcn.cl) didn't load; the bill's own summary of
  the law is the source. **Mechanism:** each pot has one job and the law ranks them, so a crash doesn't reopen the
  argument about which pays. And when the ranking is changed, it is changed in public, by a bill that gives reasons.
  **Could touch:** V28 para 10's "cheques first" is already a ranking of one; Chile's is a full list, so ours could be
  written as one: cheques, then the named gift, then the unnamed gift and Laura's half. **Ninety-second rule:** "Two
  pots, one order of payment, written before the storm."
- **A2. Norway: a big move in the fund must not move spending at once.** Ministry of Finance, Meld. St. 1 (2023-2024),
  National Budget 2024, ch. 3, https://www.regjeringen.no/en/documents/meld.-st.-1-20232024/id2998356/?ch=2, accessed
  29 Oct 2026. Quotes (verified): "major changes in Fund value should not immediately entail major changes in Fund
  spending"; "The fiscal guideline calls for Fund spending to instead be modified over several years"; "Fund spending
  should in normal times be well below 3 per cent"; "The expected real return on the GPFG is projected to be 3 per
  cent." Follow-up: Ministry press release, 16 Feb 2017, which cut the return estimate from 4 to 3 per cent and kept
  the rule, https://www.regjeringen.no/en/aktuelt/increased-equity-share-in-the-government-pension-fund-global-and-adjustment-to-the-expected-rate-of-return-in-the-fiscal-policy-guidelines/id2539299
  (the scout's reading, not re-checked). **Breaks:** Norway's fund never ends and spends an *expected* return, a
  forecast, which is exactly what the cheques side refuses to do; a fixed $50,000 due in 2036 can't be smoothed or
  delayed. Smoothing a promise is the wrong way round: it would let a crash go unrecorded. **Mechanism:** the rule fixes
  a ceiling and says in advance that shocks are absorbed slowly. When the forecast proved too high, Norway changed the
  number and kept the rule. **Could touch:** only the hope side: the unnamed gift (2028-31) may move slowly with the
  stocks, and the 2031 top should sit "well below" the most it could be. **Ninety-second rule:** "Spend the rule, not
  the market."
- **A3. Alaska: a fixed share of a smoothed value, and never more than the pot holds.** Alaska Permanent Fund
  Corporation, "FAQs & Helpful Information", https://apfc.org/?p=4896, and "Generating Revenue for Today & Tomorrow",
  https://apfc.org/era-durability/, both accessed 29 Oct 2026. Quotes (verified): "The draw is subject to
  appropriation and is set in statute at 5.0%"; the average is taken "for the first five of the preceding six fiscal
  years; this average creates a smoothing effect across years"; "Under this structure, appropriations are limited to
  the ERA balance." **Breaks:** Alaska is permanent and wants a steady payout; Laura's gift is named once, so a lagged
  average would overstate what she owns after a crash and could promise money she no longer holds. And "subject to
  appropriation" means politicians choose each year, which a cheque can't rely on. **Mechanism:** each year's draw is
  a formula on past values, so one bad year barely moves it; but whatever the formula says, spending is capped by the
  cash already banked in a separate account. **Could touch:** the cap, not the smoothing: the 2031 top is the lower of
  the formula and what she owns that day. V28 already does this (top = floor + half the stocks *she holds*); Alaska is
  the outside proof that a formula needs a hard cap behind it. **Ninety-second rule:** "Never more than the pot holds."

### Jun · adjacent · precommitment, escape clauses, Ulysses contracts · door: his coach's race splits · Haiku 5.5
- **J1. Germany's debt brake may be broken only by a vote, and the vote must carry its own repayment plan.**
  Grundgesetz Art. 115(2), sentences 7-9, gesetze-im-internet.de, https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/gg/art_115.html,
  accessed 29 Oct 2026. Quotes (verified): "Im Falle von Naturkatastrophen oder außergewöhnlichen Notsituationen, die
  sich der Kontrolle des Staates entziehen" ...; "Der Beschluss ist mit einem Tilgungsplan zu verbinden." ("The
  decision must be combined with a repayment plan."); "Die Rückführung der nach Satz 7 aufgenommenen Kredite hat binnen
  eines angemessenen Zeitraumes zu erfolgen." ("...within an appropriate period."). **Breaks:** "appropriate period" has
  no number, so the repayment can slide (Kasseckert, *Wirtschaftsdienst* 2024(5), argues it won't be repaid in full:
  UNVERIFIED, quote not re-checked). The trigger is a shock outside the state's control, not a change in one client's
  needs, and Laura has no parliament: she is the only voter. **Mechanism:** the bend needs a recorded majority, never the

  named smaller, the 2033 top-up taken from her half) is written with its way back in the same line, with a date, not
  "in time". **Ninety-second rule:** "A rule may bend only by a recorded decision, and that decision says how it
  comes back."
- **J2. The EU's escape clause opens only for a storm that hits everyone, and the rules keep running under it.**
  European Commission, Communication to the Council on the activation of the general escape clause of the Stability
  and Growth Pact, COM(2020) 123 final, 20 Mar 2020 (German text),
  https://economy-finance.ec.europa.eu/document/download/4039fca6-615f-4115-a9ad-cdce4be17279_de, accessed 29 Oct
  2026. Quotes (verified): "im Falle eines schweren Konjunkturabschwungs im Euro-Währungsgebiet" ("in the event of a
  severe economic downturn in the euro area"); "Durch die allgemeine Ausweichklausel werden die Verfahren des
  Stabilitäts- und Wachstumspakts nicht ausgesetzt." ("The general escape clause does not suspend the procedures of the
  Pact."). **Breaks:** the trigger is a region-wide event; the fetched text doesn't say how the clause closes (it was
  deactivated later; not checked). **Mechanism:** the bend is allowed only for a shock everyone can see, so no member
  can claim one privately; and the checks carry on while the targets are relaxed. **Could touch:** a bend in Laura's
  plan should hang on a market fact anyone can read (a yield, a fund price), not a feeling, and the January check of
  each rung against its cheque never pauses. **Ninety-second rule:** "Bend for a storm that hits everyone, and keep
  the logbook running."
- **J3. A Ulysses contract is signed while well and can't be revoked in the crisis it names.** Potthoff, Finke,
  Scholten, Gieselmann, Vollmann and Gather, "Opportunities and risks of self-binding directives: A qualitative study
  involving stakeholders and researchers in Germany", *Frontiers in Psychiatry* 13, 21 Oct 2022,
  https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychiatry/articles/10.3389/fpsyt.2022.974132/full, accessed 29 Oct 2026. Quotes
  (verified): service users "can consent in advance to involuntary hospital admission and involuntary treatment during
  future mental health crises"; such directives "cannot be revoked under the circumstances for which they were
  written". (A second source, Atkinson, *Psychiatric Bulletin* 2004, "Ulysses' crew or Circe?": UNVERIFIED, kept out.)
  **Breaks:** the sources never say who wins when a later wish fights the earlier one, and Laura's crew is plural

  you bind yourself while calm for the moment you won't be, and the binding works only if someone else carries it out.
  **Could touch:** the 2031 move of the promised half into Treasuries is the plan's one-way door; the IPS can say it is
  decided now, for the day the stocks look too good to sell. **Ninety-second rule:** "Lock the decision while you're
  calm, for the day you won't be."

### Nora · far · hospital early-warning scores, "track and trigger" · door: the chart behind her mum's pharmacy counter · Opus 5.5
- **N1. One extreme reading triggers on its own, however good the total looks, and worry overrides the number.**
  Royal College of Physicians, *National Early Warning Score (NEWS) 2: Standardising the assessment of acute-illness
  severity in the NHS*, executive summary, Dec 2017, https://www.rcp.ac.uk/media/ctulqqbn/news2-executive-summary_0.pdf,
  accessed 29 Oct 2026. Quotes (verified): "A single red score (3 in a single parameter) is unusual, but should prompt
  an urgent review by a clinician"; "Concern about a patient's clinical condition should always override the NEWS";
  "The NEWS should not be used in children (ie aged <16 years) or in women who are pregnant". Also NICE CG50: "The
  frequency of monitoring should increase if abnormal physiology is detected" (scout's reading, not re-checked).
  **Breaks:** a patient is treated where the trouble is; a short rung can't be treated by selling it (held to
  maturity), so the response must come from outside the ladder. And the plan looks once a year, not every hour.
  **Mechanism:** NEWS2 adds a few readings into one score but also fires on any single extreme reading, because a
  healthy total can hide one failing organ. And it says in writing which patients it must not be used on. **Could
  touch:** the plan checks "the ladder" as a whole; a whole can be funded while the 2041 rung is short after coupons are
  reinvested low. A per-rung trigger, plus one line on where the framework doesn't apply (a US default; a change in the
  cheques themselves). **Ninety-second rule:** "A healthy total never excuses one failing part."
- **N2. Every exception carries a signature and an expiry date, and silence means the default happens.** NSW Health,
  Policy Directive PD2025_014, *Recognition and management of patients who are deteriorating*, 7 Apr 2025,
  https://www1.health.nsw.gov.au/pds/ActivePDSDocuments/PD2025_014.pdf, accessed 29 Oct 2026 (the policy behind the
  Clinical Excellence Commission's "Between the Flags"). Quotes (verified): calling criteria may be altered "in
  consultation with the AMO/delegated clinician responsible who authorises the alterations" (s3.4); "Acute alterations
  are set for a defined period of time, not longer than 12 hours" (s3.4); "Patients with acute alterations to calling
  criteria must have daily medical reviews" (s1.2); "escalate according to the local CERS if the call is not attended
  within 30 minutes" (App. 9.2.2); "If a clinician is worried or unsure whether to initiate a CERS call" (App. 9.2.1).
  **Breaks:** on a ward the person who signs the exception (a senior doctor) is not the patient; Laura is client and
  decider at once, so her signature binds only if the condition is written before she needs it. The clocks differ by
  orders of magnitude (12 hours vs a year). **Mechanism:** a clinician may change a patient's triggers, but only with a
  named authoriser, a fixed expiry and a review, after which the standard criteria return by themselves. If the agreed
  response doesn't arrive in time, the system escalates without anyone deciding to. **Could touch:** V28 has no
  override at all, which is either a strength or a fiction. A bounded one: Laura may delay one hope-side step (naming
  day's lock, the 2033 top-up from her half) once, in writing, for at most a year; never on the cheque side; if nothing
  is logged by the date, the default executes. **Ninety-second rule:** "Every exception carries a signature and an
  expiry date, and silence means the default happens."
- **N3. Scores fail when an alarm stays on and people stop answering it, and when readings drift to the comfortable
  value.** Ede, Jeffs, Vollam and Watkinson, "A qualitative exploration of escalation of care in the acute ward
  setting", *Nursing in Critical Care* 25(3), 2020 (online 2019), https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7217245,
  accessed 29 Oct 2026. Quotes (verified): "Triggering patients were not always re‐escalated if observations remained
  the "same level" of abnormal"; "others justified inaction based on observations because "that is usual for the
  patient."" One step further: Badawy, Nguyen, Clark, Halm and Makam, "Is everyone really breathing 20 times a minute?",
  *BMJ Quality & Safety* 26(10), https://qualitysafety.bmj.com/content/26/10/832, accessed 29 Oct 2026: recorded
  breathing rates came "with values clustered at 18 and 20" (verified). **Breaks:** Laura's readings are market prices,
  far harder to round to "normal" than a breath count. The soft readings are the ones the team writes itself: the
  assumed reinvestment rate and the stocks' "expected interest". **Mechanism:** an alarm that stays at one level stops
  getting answered, and a reading taken by habit at the reassuring value makes the whole score lie. **Could touch:**
  the coupon gap is measured each January at market yields, never at the model's rate; thin headroom that lasts years
  rings at every check, not once. **Ninety-second rule:** "Read the gauge from the market, not the model, and an alarm
  that stays on must ring again."
