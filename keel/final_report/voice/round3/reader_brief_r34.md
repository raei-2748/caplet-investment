# Blind reading, voice rounds 3 and 4 (Mon 16 Nov 2026)

You are a careful reader. Six Sydney high-school students (team "Caplet") wrote an investment policy statement (IPS) to
their client, Laura Gao, for the Wharton Global High School Investment Competition. They are now writing a longer Final
Report. Your job is to judge, passage by passage, whose voice you hear.

1. Read the team's IPS twice, the second time slowly, as writing by people: `/home/user/caplet-investment/keel/final_report/FINAL_IPS_as_submitted.txt`.
   Do not open any other file in that repository.
2. Then read your pack of passages (path in your brief). They are mixed: some may be the team's own writing, some written
   by others trying to sound like the team, some by others. You are not told how many of each.
3. For EACH passage write:
   - **Verdict:** could the people who wrote the IPS have written this, as part of a longer report? One of: Surely / Probably / Probably not / Surely not.
   - **Why:** one or two sentences about the passage as a whole (how it moves, how it sounds, what kind of person it sounds like), not a list of words.
   - **Meaning:** what the passage says, in one sentence.
   - **Check question** (answer only the one that fits the passage, if any):
     - Operating reserve: "If interest rates fell to zero in 2029 and stayed there, would the ten payments still be paid in full? Why?"
     - Monitoring: "In 2030 world shares fall 30%. Does Laura change the plan? Why?"
     - More shares: "Would putting more of the 2028 money into shares let Laura promise co-sponsors more in 2031? Why?"
     - The first plan: "In one sentence, why did the team's first plan fail its own test?"
   - **Image:** if the passage uses an extended image or metaphor: did it help you understand, or get in the way? One sentence.
4. Then rank ALL the passages from most like the IPS's writers (1) to least.
5. Then, in three or four sentences: what separates the passages you ranked highest from those you ranked lowest?

Write your answers to the output file named in your brief, starting each passage's block with `### Passage n`, and the
ranking under `### Ranking` as a numbered list of passage numbers. Be honest and specific; do not try to guess which
are "real". Do not commit or edit any repository file other than your output file.
