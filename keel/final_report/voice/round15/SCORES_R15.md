How the rules were applied:
- N: the reader says the text did not say, and gives no answer or only a hedged guess ("presumably", "the nearest it comes"). This holds whatever the key says.
- If the reader says the text did not say it "directly" and then works out a definite answer from what it did say, the answer scores P at most.
- A part of a question that the reader calls unsaid earns nothing. The rest of the answer is still scored.
