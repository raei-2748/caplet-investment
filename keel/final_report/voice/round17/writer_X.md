=== C ===
So what will you be able to tell your co-sponsors in 2031? Before your first meeting, we'll move half your shares into the same Treasury fund as your floor – so whatever figure you name, the Treasuries that will pay it are already held for you. At today's yields, your range would be $95,000 to $115,000: the bottom held from January 2028, the top from January 2031. Which figure you sign for is your call, but we'd suggest the top. That half of your shares was the building's half all along, so giving the top leaves your flexibility exactly where it was. And do sign – a letter with the amount, the purpose and the date – because holding the Treasuries shows the money exists, but only your signature says you'll give it. Add the 10 payments of $50,000, complete from January 2028, and you're offering about $615,000, with every dollar bought before you promise it.
=== L ===
We have Laura offer co-sponsors a signed pledge letter as well as the money held for her: holding the Treasuries shows the money exists, while a letter with the amount, the purpose and the date says it will be given, and a letter is what a funder's gift office can count. A signature alone is not enough when the money behind it does not exist yet: in October 2007 Lehman Brothers itself pledged $10 million to Spelman College over five years, to be paid from the bank's own future money, and when the bank failed in 2008 the college received $3 million of it. Laura's money is held for her before she signs.
=== R ===
In 2033 I will open a small residency in Taiwan where artists, writers and designers can live, work and teach for a while. I believe stories can change how people see the world, and I want to give others the room to tell theirs.

For the building, my range is $95,000 to $115,000. The bottom, $95,000, has been held for me in US Treasuries since January 2028. The top, $115,000, has been held for me since January 2031, when I moved half of my shares into Treasuries. Those Treasuries are guaranteed by the US government, and no market can change what they will pay. I am signing for the top, not the bottom, because the shares I moved in January 2031 were always meant for the building. Giving the top still leaves the other half of my shares free, for costs that run over.

I am also giving $50,000 a year to the residency's running costs from 2033 to 2042. The Treasuries for all ten payments were bought from January 2027, and the last of them in January 2028. In all, I am committing about $615,000. I am putting all $615,000 in writing now, in 2031. I will pay the $115,000 in January 2033.

=== NOTES ===
Only R's three building-figure sentences differ: "my range is $95,000 to $115,000", the bottom and the top become, in F, the figure she signs for (with the only "you" in the passage), $95,000 of it held since January 2028, and the rest since January 2031 ending in the range. No later word had to change: "Those Treasuries" still points at the Treasuries named in the sentence before, and "the top" now names the $115,000 already given, so "I am signing for the top, not the bottom" reads as the reason rather than news, though "signing" comes twice in F. Hardest was making R's range read as a record of when each part was held rather than as doubt (hence "my range is", never "will fall between"), with the strong words on the Treasuries alone and none on the fund that holds them. The case gives no reason for Taiwan, so the purpose sentences name it as the place and give her reason (stories) without inventing one for Taiwan.
