## From memory

1. **How much, and when?** $115,000 towards the building, paid in January 2033. She gave a range of $95,000 to $115,000 but said she signs for the top.

2. **Could it end up lower?** I am not sure. Her own words say the $115,000 is already in US Treasuries, guaranteed, and that no market can change it, so by her words: no, not after 2031. But she still names $95,000 as a bottom, and nothing in her words says what would bring it down there. From the earlier letter I remember that a Treasury fund's payout is not a fixed sum, so perhaps a little lower than $115,000 for that reason, and before 2031 a fall in shares would have lowered the top. As low as $95,000 at most, I think, but I cannot say what would cause it.

3. **What is the $95,000 in her words?** The bottom of her range: money held for her in US Treasuries since January 2028.

4. **How sure is she, and why believe her?** Very sure: she says the money is already in US government Treasuries before she signs, so her pledge is backed by money that exists, unlike Lehman's pledge to Spelman, which came from future money and was mostly never paid. She also gives a signed letter with amount, purpose and date. That is why I would believe her, if I could see the holdings.

5. **What I would write down, and what I would still ask.** I would file her own funding share as $115,000, a signed pledge payable January 2033 (with a note that $95,000 is the part held since 2028), plus $50,000 a year for running costs 2033-2042. Before committing ministry money I would ask for one thing: a current statement from the bank or broker holding the Treasuries, showing the amounts, maturity dates and that they are held for her, as proof of her resources.
