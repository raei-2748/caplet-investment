# Voice round 14: Keel's prediction, written before any of the round's text was read (Sun 22 Nov 2026, evening)

Round 12 found that a per-sentence "why, from the page alone?" column catches none of the five version 61 sentences
Caplet could not follow and every simulated reader passed (V12, V13, V16, V20, V21). Round 14 tries a cold retelling:
each reader reads a text once, then, without looking back, tells a friend each step and the reason the text gave, in
the reader's own words; a separate blind scorer marks each retold reason as explaining (E), restating the step (R),
missing (M) or wrong (W). Writers Y and Z first carry the IPS on from its last line, then write two passages for the
report as the same people: P3 the 2031 range and P5 the half moved and the half kept.

What Keel expects:

1. **Calibration on version 61.** A sentence counts as caught when, for at least half of the four readers, its step is
   missing from the retelling or its retold reason is scored R, M or W. Keel expects the retelling to catch 2 of the 5
   (likeliest V13 and V16, whose steps are hard to retell with a reason; least likely V21, a plain fact), so it would
   fail the bar set in round 12 (at least 3 of 5). False alarms on the four sentences Caplet followed (V01, V05, V06,
   V08): 1 of 4. Over all seventeen Caplet failed, 9-12 caught.
2. **The writers.** Rereads 16-24% (round 12's writers 24% and 32%; Caplet's own paragraphs 8%). Retold reasons scored
   E: 60-75% for the writers' passages against 70-85% for Caplet's IPS paragraphs.
3. **"We".** Carrying the letter on should put people back in the sentences: 1.5-2.5 per 100 words in P3 and P5
   (round 12's writers 0.7; Caplet's IPS 2.4), and above 3 in the warm-up continuation, which still speaks to "you".
4. **Wit.** Writer Z, told the team likes a dry touch, uses one or two; the readers' scores do not separate Y from Z by
   more than writer variance (round 10).
5. **The topics.** P3 (the range) again fails more than P5 on rereads, and P5's "why half" is retold with a reason by
   most readers, since the fact sheet now gives it.
