## Any pattern

- **Numbers that change without the step shown**: $1,500 becomes $2,388; $102,000 becomes $95,000 "with room to spare"; "whatever" is given a precise figure.
- **Connectives that claim a logic they don't deliver**: "so for a year there are two gaps", "because nothing comes in after it", "Because the two ends say different things".
- **Signpost sentences that announce a point instead of making it**: "each can be pictured", "The honest limit is ...", "The top waits for her shares". They only made sense after the following sentences did the work.
- Most failures are in the compressed texts (the letter, Passages 1 and 3). The plainer ones (5, 7, 8) slip at most on one number or one opening contrast.
