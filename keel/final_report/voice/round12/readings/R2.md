## Any pattern

- **Asserted conditions with no mechanism.** The reinvestment condition ("the ten are only enough if interest earns today's implied rates") appears in P207.5, P208.4 and P210.6. None of them says why the Treasuries need it, i.e. that on their own they pay less than each $50,000.
- **"Could never be closed" conclusions** (P207.7, P210.8) whose reason ("nothing comes in") doesn't exclude money already inside the portfolio.
- **Splits with one side missing.** "Half" the shares is sold, and the other half's role is never given (P202.6, P204.5). The flexibility is kept in shares with no reason (P205.2).
- **Steps stated by purpose or outcome, not reason:** why shares at all (P201.3, P204.4), why a fixed order (P208.1), why Treasuries fix an amount (P208.9), why ETFs "pay what they say" (P203.2).
- **Literal shortcuts** where a figure or a range end is "owned" or "held" (L17, P202.12, P201.7), payments are "bought" (P208.2), a value "is a share price" (P209.4), or the building needs "$11,500 more than her $115,000" (P206.7).
