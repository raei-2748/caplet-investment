## Any pattern

- **Pointing off the page.** Most stalls were words that lean on something not given on that page: "Caplet XR", "the X and R", "a different matter", "$11,500" with no base, "all of it", an "It" with two candidates.
- **A role given instead of a reason.** The share fund is often described by what it does to the top ("helps determine the maximum", "the rest buys VT") rather than why it is held at all; only Passages 3 and 5 say it is there to grow.
- **A split with the other part unexplained.** "Half the shares are sold" works when the other half is named as flexibility (Passages 1, 6, 7, 10) and fails when it is not (Passages 2, 5).
- **Overclaim where a reason should be.** "The only other place", "pay what they say", "not dependent on yields": each stands in for an argument and breaks on a professor's first objection.
- **Statistics with an unclear base** (which flexibility size gives "98 in 100", why 1 in 6 becomes 80 in 100) slowed reading but did not fail.
