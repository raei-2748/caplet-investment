## What each reader said, in their words (selected)

### Reader 1

On the top group: "eager, slightly overreaching vocabulary and casual asides, with confident overstatement and sentences that change direction on a dash. They sound like teenagers keen to sound professional and to reassure, not like someone landing polished lines." Lowest: "rhetorically finished (staged attacks, repeated sentence patterns, neat reversals and morals) or clipped into dense summary".
On K1: "built as a staged drama, attacking the promise to test it, with short punchy sentences and quotable turns ... a practised writer's set piece".
On K2: "a teacher's guided-tour structure, a neat reversal that sums up the point, and a measured 'honest warning'".
On K3: "a rhythmic, essayistic build-up that repeats one sentence shape for effect, and closes on a 'what it taught us about ourselves' reflection".
On B1: "quotes their own earlier promise back with a wry 'awkward', ends on an easy 'so yes, we'd do it again' ... the students explaining a fix they're a bit proud of".
On B3: "a group remembering a real argument ... concedes a teammate's point candidly".
On X2: "a skilled summariser condensing the plan, not the students talking".

### Reader 2

On Text A: eager, a little cheeky, slightly show-off; exclamations; reached-for words; explanations tumble forward on dashes; enjoy the idea and want Laura to enjoy it; one neat flourish saved for the end.
Top-ranked (the real reflections): "eager, slightly over-reaching student energy ... real pleasure in the idea". Next group (fresh writers): "lost some of that bounce, but a person still seems to be talking in plain, slightly uneven sentences ... end on one modest flourish rather than a string of polished lines". Lowest: "too clipped and impersonal (Keel's old IPS) or too smoothly crafted, with staged turns and aphorisms (main session)".
On K2: "moves like a practised essayist ... a neat reversal and an 'honest warning' set piece, and every sentence lands where intended. Text A is never this smooth."
On K1: "shaped like a performance: a staged attack on the promise, punchy short verdicts and pointed contrasts."
On K3: "a polished reflective essay with a built rhetorical parallel and a neat turn about the lesson being 'about ourselves'."
On A3: "the measured, balanced cadence of a mature essayist drawing a moral at the end."
On B3: "a person thinking aloud, conceding a point and then working back to the reason ... feels lived and specific."
On X2 (Keel IPS): "tightly compressed and impersonal, a set of rules delivered in clipped, polished sentences. No one seems to be talking."

### Reader 3

On Text A: "eager and sure of themselves, a little showy, and warm toward Laura ... a grand phrase where a simple one would do ... students pleased with their plan and keen to talk it up."
Top: "eager, slightly overreaching confidence, where a simple point is dressed up and any doubt is waved off with cheer ... explain in quick bursts with asides". Lower: "smoother and more balanced, with tidy contrasts, staged set pieces and rounded endings, which read like a practised writer".
On X2: "very compressed and procedural ... 'that fund' is never introduced" (the only comprehension failure noted).
On K1: "staged as a neat little contest with an obvious attack, a quieter one and a verdict".
On B2: "warm and a little informal, and it explains in short, eager steps".
On A1: "plain and earnest ... like students working through a list ... a person talking, a little unpolished in places, the way Text A is".
