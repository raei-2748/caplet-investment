## Every CHANGE first

1. **US Census, 2009 receipts (Section 2, para 1).** The 7.8% fall is for sole proprietors only (legal form S),
   $12,811,801 thousand in 2008 to $11,808,168 thousand in 2009. All one-person businesses in NAICS 7115 together
   fell 6.2% ($14,555,179 thousand to $13,658,262 thousand). FACTS_PLAIN's "independent artists, writers and
   performers working for themselves fell 7.8%" can be read as the whole group. Say: "the receipts of US independent
   artists, writers and performers working as sole proprietors fell 7.8% (US Census Bureau), and did not pass their
   2008 level again until 2011." The 2011 part matches ($12,921,702 thousand against $12,811,801 thousand; 2010 was
   $12,315,458 thousand, still below).
2. **IBTA's early end (3e, the watch list).** "IBTA ended 15 days before its scheduled date" counts to its last
   trading day. iShares' closed-funds page: last price "$25.35 as of 11/30/2021", final distribution "as of
   12/03/2021"; iShares' own case study gives IBTA's "Termination Date 12/3/2021"; the prospectus said it "will
   terminate on or about December 15, 2021". A judge who checks iShares' termination date counts 12 days. Say:
   "IBTA stopped trading 15 days before its stated end date and paid out 12 days before it", or "about two weeks
   early".
3. **The California State Lottery Commission's policy date (part 1, "Ten payments, ten dates").** FACTS_PLAIN says
   "approved for 2025 on 25 September 2025". The document says "REAFFIRMED AND ADOPTED ON THE 25th DAY OF SEPTEMBER
   2025". Say "reaffirmed and adopted on 25 September 2025" (SECTION_NOTES already says "reaffirmed"). The quoted
   words themselves match (row 1).
