## 5. APA 7 rules that matter here

1. **Group authors.** Spell out the full name of the group as the author; abbreviate only in the text, for example

   https://apastyle.apa.org/style-grammar-guidelines/abbreviations/group-authors (Sections 8.21 and 9.11) ;
