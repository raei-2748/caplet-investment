## 5. APA 7 rules that matter here

Each rule below was read on the APA Style page given (9 Nov 2026).

1. **Group authors.** Spell out the full name of the group as the author; abbreviate only in the text, for example
   "(U.S. Department of the Treasury [Treasury], n.d.)" the first time and "(Treasury, n.d.)" after. When the author and
   the publisher are the same, leave the publisher out. For a specific agency inside a larger one, the specific agency is
   the author and the parent becomes the publisher.
   https://apastyle.apa.org/style-grammar-guidelines/references/elements-list-entry ;
   https://apastyle.apa.org/style-grammar-guidelines/abbreviations/group-authors (Sections 8.21 and 9.11) ;
   https://apastyle.apa.org/style-grammar-guidelines/references/examples/report-government-agency-references
2. **Data sets.** Put "[Data set]" after the title; name the publisher if it differs from the author; give the URL.
   FRED series name the original agency as author (the Board of Governors for DGS1 and DEXTAUS, the Bureau of Labor
   Statistics for CPIAUCSL, the St. Louis Fed for T10YIE), as each FRED page's own suggested citation does.
   https://apastyle.apa.org/style-grammar-guidelines/references/examples/data-set-references (Section 10.9)
3. **Fact sheets.** Use the report format and add "[Fact sheet]" after the title (row 116).
   https://apastyle.apa.org/style-grammar-guidelines/references/examples/fact-sheet-references (Section 10.4)
4. **Retrieval dates.** Give one only for an unarchived work that is designed to change, such as a fund page, a FRED
   series or the Treasury rates page. A fixed PDF or article gets none. Do not write "Retrieved from" before a URL
   without a date. For the data pages, use the date the team actually downloaded the file (the model's files in
   `competition/official_market_data/` and `archive/rab/data/fred/`), not the date of this register.
   https://apastyle.apa.org/style-grammar-guidelines/references/elements-list-entry ;
   https://apastyle.apa.org/instructional-aids/creating-reference-list.pdf
5. **Missing elements.** No date: "(n.d.)". No title: a description in square brackets in the title's place. A source the
   reader cannot recover at all is a personal communication, cited in the text only.
   https://apastyle.apa.org/style-grammar-guidelines/references/missing-information ;
   https://apastyle.apa.org/style-grammar-guidelines/citations/personal-communications (Section 8.9)
6. **The case and Wharton's documents.** None of them prints an author, and the case and the Final Report instructions
   print no title or date. The Final Report's readers hold the same documents, so they are recoverable and go in the
   reference list: author "Wharton Global Youth Program" (the program that runs the competition, confirmed on its
   site), the year 2026, and a bracketed description where there is no title (rows 120 to 122). The Guide has a printed
   title and a public URL, so it is cited as a document. Citing the case in the text: (Wharton Global Youth Program,
   2026a). Lettering of the three 2026 entries follows their titles, with the Guide's opening "2026–2027" read as words;
   if the team reads numerals first, the Guide becomes 2026a. The order of numerals was not read at the source.
7. **The team's own prior submissions.** The IPS and the Trading Notes Analysis are unpublished works. Format: Author.
   (Year). *Title* [Unpublished ...]. Source. The author is the team as registered with Wharton (rows 27 and 28).
   https://apastyle.apa.org/blog/cite-unpublished-works (Section 10.8)
8. **Retracted articles.** Cite the original article and add the retraction notice in parentheses after the DOI:
   "(Retraction published 2026, *Psychological Science, 37*[9], 665)".
   https://apastyle.apa.org/style-grammar-guidelines/references/examples/journal-article-references
9. **DOIs and URLs.** Write DOIs as https://doi.org/... ; prefer a DOI to a URL when both exist; no full stop after a
   DOI or URL. https://apastyle.apa.org/style-grammar-guidelines/references/dois-urls
10. **The References page.** Start it on a new page; centre the bold heading "References"; alphabetize; 0.5-inch hanging
    indent; double-spaced like the rest of the paper.
    https://apastyle.apa.org/style-grammar-guidelines/paper-format/reference-list
11. **Same author, same year.** Add a, b, c after the year, in the order of the titles (rows 8 to 13, 15 and 16, 27 and
    28, 40 to 44, 82 and 83, 86 and 87, 103 and 104, 115 and 116, 120 to 122). The letters carry into the text, for
    example (Leibowitz, 1986a). Where n.d. works and dated works share an author, this register puts n.d. first; that
    ordering rule was not read at the source.
    https://apastyle.apa.org/style-grammar-guidelines/citations/basic-principles/same-year-author (Section 8.19)
12. **Chapters in edited books** (row 24): Author. (Year). Chapter title. In Editor (Eds.), *Book title* (pp. x–y).
    Publisher. https://apastyle.apa.org/instructional-aids/reference-guide.pdf
