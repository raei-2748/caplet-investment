**Points for the writers (also in the rows):**

1. **"Reinvested coupons fund the rest" (3a's aha) is not in the Trading Notes Analysis as submitted.** It is the
   WInS trade note on the 4.375% Treasury of 15 Nov 2039 (6 Oct 2026), so it needs its own entry (row 58).
2. **Heaton and Lucas published two papers in 2000.** For background risk the paper is *The Economic Journal* one
   (row 4). REFERENCES_APA row 69 is the *Journal of Finance* paper on entrepreneurial risk. If the report cites both,
   they become 2000a and 2000b.
3. **Neyman's 1934 paper is the one for "the term"** (row 43). Card Q74 read the 1937 paper (row 44).
4. **Fama and Bliss (1987) find that one-year forward rates do forecast the one-year rate two to four years ahead.**
   "A poor guide" holds for the near term and for the premium. Do not overstate it.
5. **Velthuis and Gera (GOLDMINE row 15, placed in 3a by the aha table) is not in Branch 2's text as it stands.** The
   paper says closures are "mainly motivated by financial reasons": 14 of 38 museums with data, 36.8%, the most
   common reason.
6. **The 2000-2003 replay checks out on two of its figures.** "About -42%" reproduces on MSCI's spliced ACWI series,
   and costs rose about 19.6% from December 2002 to December 2004 on DGBAS's index. Neither DGBAS issue read today
   printed January 2003, the month that "about 18%" and "+27% in US$" start from (card Q85 row 24 took it from a
   repo copy).
