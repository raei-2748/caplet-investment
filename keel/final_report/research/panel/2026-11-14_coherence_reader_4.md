## Findings

**1. HIGH. The conceit dates the ten payments' ink to January 2028 and calls the January 2027 purchase pencil.**
- Plan: `CONCEIT.md:43` (Jan 2027 row: "Nothing yet. The ten Treasuries are bought ... but bought is not inked: each
  reaches $50,000 only with interest not yet earned"); `:50-51` ("The ten payments and the bottom of the range are inked
  in January 2028"); `:62` ("Finished only from January 2028 ... every rung leans on interest not yet earned"); `:67`
  ("the one line the January 2027 purchase left in pencil", called "the report's most honest evaluation of the IPS");
  `:101` (rank 2 "corrects ... 'inked from 2027'"); `:124-125` and `SKELETON.md:175-176` (3.0: "inked in January
  2028, five years before the first payment"; 3a: "the one line the January 2027 purchase leaves in pencil").
- Frozen: IPS l.13, "In January 2027 we'll use your first deposit to buy 10 Treasuries ... Held to the end, these pay
  what they say"; IPS l.11, "none of your 10 operating payments will depend on a share price, or on anybody else's
  money"; TNA l.7, "a near-ironclad guarantee, and we provide exactly that"; TNA l.15, "each of Laura's payments rests on
  a dedicated instrument (not merely a forecast)".
- Why HIGH: the plan's own guard says "a judge reads 'pencil' as provisional" (`CONCEIT.md:154-155`). Set beside l.13
  and TNA l.7, "nothing inked in January 2027" says the IPS's central step was provisional for a year, and "five years
  before" quietly moves the IPS's January 2027 purchase (six years before) to 2028. It is also the banned idea "the ten
  as bought would have fallen short" (`INTENT.md:52-54`, `WORDS.md:22`) said in image form. And it is wrong by the
  conceit's own definition: ink is "a dollar figure already owned in Treasuries that will pay it" (`CONCEIT.md:25`), and
  in January 2027 each payment's own Treasury already pays $42,180 to $47,338 of it at no interest (`tables.md:42-51`),
  which is what WN l.18 says: "its fixed coupons and principal cover most of it".
- Smallest fix: each payment is mostly inked in January 2027 by its own Treasury (WN l.18's "most of it") and finished in
  January 2028 by the two parts the IPS and the WInS notes left to the 2028 deposit (l.13's completion; the interest on
  cash waiting, WN l.24). Drop the "Nothing yet" row, "bought is not inked" and "five years before"; 3.0 dates the ten as
  "finished by January 2028"; 3a's image, if kept, is the last line the IPS itself left to the 2028 deposit, never a
  line "the January 2027 purchase left in pencil".

**2. HIGH. 3e area (1)'s headline says share returns do not move the top.**
- Plan: `SKELETON.md:47`, "the headline number that the stated top stays $115,000 at every return from 4% to 10% a year
  on the central path ... only her flexibility moves"; `SKELETON.md:100-101`, thesis 6, "the share forecast barely
  matters".
- Frozen: pitch, IPS l.3, "a world stock ETF will determine the ceiling"; IPS l.15, "its returns will help determine
  your maximum facility contribution ... markets will be able to increase or decrease the top"; TNA l.21 (Trading Note 3),
  "a rise before 2031 lifts the top of the range co-sponsors hear"; TNA l.23, "VT answers a different question – how
  generous can the facility contribution be?"
- Why HIGH: read side by side, "the top stays $115,000 at every return" negates the pitch's last clause and Trading Note
  3's note. It is also a rounding edge: the unrounded top runs $116,580 to $119,851 and misses $120,000 by $149 at 10%
  (NUMBERS 4), while Table 2 shows realised markets moving the top from $95,000 to $140,000 (`tables.md:66-74`).
- Smallest fix: say it as the assumed average return, which moves the top by less than one $5,000 rounding step, in the
  same breath as the realised spread in Table 2 (what shares actually do before 2031 sets the top, as the pitch says);
  never "only her flexibility moves" as the headline.
