# Taiwan funder reader on 3d (Wed 11 Nov 2026)

## 1. Who I am and what I read
Senior program officer at a 財團法人 funded each year by its listed parent; I support arts, residencies and cultural spaces, sit on Ministry of Culture panels and have co-funded two capital projects. I read the case, Wharton's report rules, the submitted IPS, rows 3.0, 3c and 3d, the co-sponsor section of INTENT, NUMBERS section 4 (range) and 8d, and the verdicts of cards Q11d and Q11c. I checked no rule at a source myself; the Taiwan points below rest on the cards and my own practice, and I mark them so.

## 2. What the plan asks of me
- Laura owns ten years of operating money in Treasuries, so I am asked only about the building.
- She counts about $95,000 now, up to about $115,000, as her own, owned before 2031.
- I pledge capital, conditional on the facility total being reached by a close date, and yearly programme money from 2033.
- She never asks for her ten payments and never offers me a bonus.

## 3. The figure for my board paper
US$95,000 (about NT$3.1m at 32.768), the stated bottom only. It is owned since January 2028 and only a US default lowers it. The top of $115,000 I would write as "intended, owned after the 2031 move", not counted. A board paper needs one number it can defend if the donor is a stranger; a range gives my chair a reason to ask why. Also, the report's own table shows the bottom can be stated lower ($80,000 to $85,000) if yields are low in January 2028, so I would not count anything until that date has passed.

## 4. Would I pledge?
Decision: yes to a meeting, yes in principle to both forms, signed only on documents.
- Yearly programme money from 2033: the form I can really do. Board intent in the year plan, a fixed sum over fixed years (the card's NT$800,000 a year for three years is the pattern I know). I would size it at NT$0.5m to NT$1.0m a year, about 10 to 20% of a small residency's operating budget (a guess on my side; the plan gives no budget).
- Capital: conditional, by close date, on the facility total. I could take NT$1m to NT$3m, no more than 20 to 30% of a facility budget that I would expect near NT$15m to NT$30m. Capital from a corporate foundation is hard: our endowment is small and the parent decides each year.
- Before I sign I need: the residency's legal body (a 財團法人 or 社團法人 registered in Taiwan, not Laura personally); proof the site is owned or held at least four years; a facility budget with a total and a close date; an operating plan with years; a custodian statement of Laura's holdings; the list of other funders; who signs. A 2031 meeting lands in my 2032 plan, filed January 2032.

## 5. Right and wrong about Taiwanese funders
Right:
- Programme money year by year, parent-funded, budgets filed each January (Q11d claim 2; not re-checked by me; it matches my practice).
- Conditional capital by close date reads as normal to me.
- One dated NT$ figure beside the US$ gift is what I need; US$ is acceptable.
- The residency owns the site and carries overruns; I never top up an overrun.
- Laura's owned figure first, other funders named, no exclusivity.
Wrong or thin:
- The plan treats her money as Laura's alone. A Ministry of Culture capital matching grant counts only money from a recognised Taiwan body, and a foreign individual's pledge may not count as the match (the card says this was not read: a gap).
- Our 10% single-grantee cap (Art. 21) means a big gift to one residency can be refused outright. The plan lists it but 3d should not hide it.
- Public recognition is missing: naming, a donor wall, a thank-you event matter to my board and parent company more than the figure.

## 6. Scores (1-5)
- Clarity 4: ten years owned, then the building, in that order is easy to follow; the range with its rule is heavy for a board.
- Credibility 4: owned-money structure is convincing; the central range moves with January 2028 yields.
- What it asks 3: reasonable shape, but no total and no date yet.
- Difference from other founders 4: no other founder says her operations are already owned.
- Fit with Taiwanese practice 3: annual money is right; the legal body, matching rules and recognition are missing.

## 7. Three fixes, most important first
1. State the facility total and the close date (even as a placeholder rule: total set by the residency's budget, close date 31 Dec 2032), because I cannot condition a pledge without both. Needs: a budget figure in NT$ and a date.
2. Name the receiving body and site: a Taiwan-registered foundation, owner and a lease or title of at least four years. Needs: the registration, site document.
3. Give the programme ask a size in NT$ per year and a term (e.g. three years from 2033) plus the recognition offered. Needs: an operating budget and the 2033 to 2042 plan.
