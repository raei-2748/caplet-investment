### M1. (d) Table 3 row 4: "any gap met from the extra Treasuries" claims more than the zero-interest numbers carry

**What the plan says.** `exhibits/tables.md` l.101, row 4 ("Early end, merger or end moved past a payment; fee
rise"): "*Changes: *the fund; any gap met from the extra Treasuries, or for the range its 0.5% margin. *Never: *a sale
of shares." `tables.md` l.9 adds that the table "ties the 0.5% to the range only (M3)".

**What NUMBERS says.**
- Under the payments' own standard, cash waiting earning nothing, the re-sized extra Treasuries have "$1,177 spare"
  with the ETF fee, and "$0 with the fee and 0.5%" (NUMBERS l.182; Figure 1's note item 2: "$1,177 to spare, the 0.5%
  margin for fund costs").
- NUMBERS l.99 counts "a fund swap under Table 3 row 4" inside the 0.5% margin, beside about 0.27% of other costs.
- NUMBERS l.343: certainty "does not hold against a fund that swapped its holdings for new par bonds, or a friction
  above 0.5%".

**Why a judge would see it.** "Any gap" is unbounded. A merger, or a move into the short-term ETF years before a
payment, gives up coupons that the zero-interest test counts. That can cost more than the $1,177 the note beside the
figure prints.

Tonight the payments carry the same 0.5% as the range. So "or for the range its 0.5% margin" (and l.9's "range only")
now reads as if the payments had no margin.

3e's own landing note (SKELETON l.265, Q109-033) says a payment "can also be left short" by fund costs beyond the
margin. A writer taking row 4 and that note would say opposite things.

**Smallest fix.**
- Row 4: "*Changes: *the fund; a gap met from the 0.5% margin the payments and the range each carry (at today's rates,
  from the extra Treasuries)". Or retire "any" and keep "a gap met from the extra Treasuries at today's rates". Both
  fit the one page.
- Drop "range only" from l.9.
