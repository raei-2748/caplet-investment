## 1. The six points

**(a) The zero-interest top-up. HIGH.** Frozen: IPS l.13 "this would cost roughly $300,000", "we'll simply complete it with the 2028 deposit"; WN l.15 (IBTR) "her first $300,000 is enough to buy Treasuries for all ten payments. If yields fall more than about 0.2 percentage points by January, the 2028 deposit would top up the ladder". Plan: WORDS names all $38,282 "the top-up" and sources it to IPS l.13, yet $35,894 of it (94%) is unconditional and sized for a no-interest world the IPS never mentions. SKELETON 3a opens "High certainty defined", a second definition beside IPS l.11, which CANON A4 makes the definition. 3a's evidence clause ("the ten as bought ... would have fallen short in about half of the starting months") reads as the team failing its own TNA l.7 "near-ironclad guarantee". INTENT failure 7's guard is mostly words to avoid; the numbers still show $300,000 becoming about $340,000. Two of three panel 2 judges raised it.

**(b) "Matched payment by payment" against TNA l.7 "through duration-matching". LOW.** Said positively, with the duration figures cut (density row 2.5), it describes the same ladder. Residual: the long 2.5 row still says "the duration figures, if kept ... the gap is the reinvestment risk, closed by the top-up", which joins (a) and (b) into a flawed TNA fixed later; strike it.

**(c) The range by written rule against IPS l.15 "the bottom will remain constant" and "whatever figure you choose is money you already own". MEDIUM.** The rule itself is applied. **(d) The two reasons defined against IPS l.17. LOW.** Five business days and 31 March 2028 narrow the IPS's two words and add no third reason. Watch 3e's "a missed deposit removes the top-up": it ties the IPS's reason to a sum sized after the freeze.

**(e) The floor at least just over half against "Most of the 2028 deposit". MEDIUM.** Centrally 54.6% ($81,889), so "most" holds; in 16.9% of simulated futures only the $75,001 rule holds it, a majority by one dollar. Table 1's book gives the floor 73.3% of the 2028 money. **(f) "Barring a US default" with net asset value payouts. LOW.** The IPS's own phrase; TNA l.15 already concedes "a net asset value, as opposed to a promise of par". But INTENT item 3's "reaches the top unless the US defaults" drops the second exception SKELETON 3c carries (fund costs above 0.5%), against WORDS' ban on "the only exception".

## 3. Score and the one change

**Coherence 3 of 5.** Instruments, order of jobs and the two reasons hold. The sizing of the 2028 deposit does not, visibly, in 3a and in Table 1.

**The one change.** Stop sourcing the $35,894 to IPS l.13. Keep only the $2,388 the ten still lack as the IPS's "complete it". Present the rest as the operating reserve's sizing, which Wharton asks Section 3 to recommend and IPS l.17 leaves open, justified by IPS l.11: at the IPS's sizing, a reinvestment shortfall in about half of historical starts could be met only from the shares (NUMBERS 2, design A, finding 4), which "none of your 10 operating payments will depend on a share price" rules out. Then drop "High certainty defined", and say once in Section 2 that the book was built before the reserve was sized (VT 8.84% of the book, 6.63% of the plan). Cost: one more WORDS name.
