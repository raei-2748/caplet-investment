**1. Board-paper figure.** US$95,000, about NT$3.12 million at 32.8, filed as **committed**, not secured. In our board's usage, secured means the money is in an account in the project entity's name or sits under a signed donation agreement. Here it is "held for her" at a US broker: it is shown, and earmarked, but it is still hers. I would also add a line saying it is about NT$2.8 million if the NT$ is 10% stronger by December 2032, because our building quotes are in NT$. The $20,000 gap to $115,000 goes in as **expected**: it is in Treasuries, but the founder only "intends" to give it.

**2. Meeting?** Yes. The deciding thing: the ten operating payments are already bought. Most artist villages I review fail on operating money after the opening, not on the building. A founder who has paid for the running years first is rare.

**3. How the range reads.** As a statement of when each part was secured, not a margin for error. "held for her in US Treasuries since January 2028" dates the bottom, and "once half her shares move before the 2031 meetings" dates the top. The cost sentence ("unless the funds' other running costs are far higher than any such fund has shown") reads as a technical footnote, not as doubt. I did not understand "before shares had any say" on first reading.

**4. Promise and hope.** Promise: "Co-sponsors count the bottom, $95,000". Hope: "she intends to give it".

**5. First question.** "What is the total building budget in NT$, and what share of it is your NT$3.1-3.8 million?" To my board, a contribution only means something next to the total cost, and nothing here says what the total is.
