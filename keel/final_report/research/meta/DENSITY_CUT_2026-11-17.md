## 1. How figures are counted

- **Counted:** every number written as digits in a part's prose notes. That covers dollar amounts, percentages, rates,
  ratios, frequencies ("1 in 20", "9 of 17.9 years"), counts offered as evidence ("fifteen houses", "585 starting
  months") and dates used as evidence or mechanics ("31 March 2028", "1974 and 2022", "since 1981").
- **Ranges:** a range counts as one figure ("$95,000 to $115,000", "4% to 10%").
- **Not counted:**
  - the case's givens: $300,000, $150,000, $450,000, $50,000, the ten payments, and the years 2027, 2028, 2031, 2033
    and 2042;
  - the plan's own step names: January 2027, January 2028, "before the 2031 meetings";
  - citations and provenance: J.P. Morgan's 2027 estimate, IPS l.15, 26 U.S.C. 130(d)(2), card numbers;
  - verbal comparisons with no digits: "more often than not", "rarely before", "upper third", "less than one rounding
    step", "more than her whole flexibility", "next to nothing". "Upper third" is close to a figure. It stays because it
    is the approved form of the houses comparison (CLAUDE.md; card Q6b).
- **Before:** the figures that SKELETON sent into a part's prose. That means the density row's "paragraph carries"
  cell, plus every Book 17 rank row, added bullet, "Raised and answered" item, second-pass item, aha row and conceit
  turn that named the part. For 3c it also includes AHA_REGISTER row 1 (Neyman), which took 3c's open slot on Mon 16
  Nov evening. For 3e it also includes the reservoir row's card Q92 note, which asked for "one clause in area (3)".
- **Excluded from "before":** figures that a row marked "never printed", "Table 2 only" or "store only", which are
  listed in section 4. Also excluded are the reservoir rows' own figures, which were never poured.

## 5. Where card Q105 was used, and where this cut departs from it

**Used.** Card Q105 shaped the cut in these places:
- The answer and headline come in each part's first sentence (rows 36, 43).
- 3a states one form of the price (rows 10, 46).
- In 3c, the mechanics come after the sentence saying who owns each end, never inside it (rows 22-23, after Mayer and
  Jackson 2005). The rounding and the cut go to Table 2's note.
- No "might" in 3c (row 35).
- In 3e, the margins are said once together (row 44), and each area points to its table by name (row 36).
- Deleting off-topic figures works better than moving them to the end (Peters et al. 2007: best choices rose from 40% to
  62%; putting them last gave 48%, not significant). So moved figures go to exhibits and the store, never to a part's
  last sentence. The one exception is 3c's $120 rate, which answers the question the sentence before it raises.
