# Q10 research cards: inflation, Taiwan costs and the Taiwan dollar

**Purpose.** Inputs for the Final Report's real-value figures: what Laura's ten fixed $50,000 payments (2033-2042) and
the 2033 facility contribution (central range about $110,000 to $135,000, NUMBERS.md section 4) buy in 2026 US dollars
and in Taiwan, and how sure anyone can be. The case asks for "the effect of inflation on portfolio projections and
facility costs" (case l.146-147) and says each payment "is a fixed $50,000 and is not adjusted for inflation" (case l.90).
Research notes for the store, not report prose. The plan is frozen: a clash is a risk to note, never a change.

**Read.** Every web source on Mon 9 Nov 2026 (Sydney), which is Sun 8 Nov in the US. Numbers for the register are in
`../../numbers/inflation_inputs.json` (same date).

**How to read.** "Real" figures are in end-2026 prices (1 Jan 2027, the day the first deposit goes in); a payment made
at the start of year Y is deflated over Y minus 2027 years. "Keel arithmetic" = my sum on the series named, not a
source's claim. FRED series were read as CSV (fredgraph.csv) through Firecrawl; curl to these hosts is refused here.
US 10- and 16-year windows use monthly CPI-U (FRED CPIAUCSL, Jan 1947 to Sep 2026); October 2025 was never published
(no BLS release), so it is filled with the geometric mean of September and November 2025. Taiwan series are DGBAS's
own time-series files (2021 = 100). Tags: INFLATION_TWD, CERTAINTY, COSPONSOR, RESIDENCY, LADDER.
