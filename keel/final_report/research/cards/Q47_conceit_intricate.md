### Parallels that would mislead (never use them)

- The IPS, the Trading Notes or the WInS notes as pencil, a draft, a proof, a thumbnail or a plate; "the report inks the
  IPS". They are Canon, and every one of these words reads as provisional (V2.4 guard 3). The Jan 2027 purchase is
  never pencil.
- Colour for VT (half of VT becomes Treasuries in 2031), including through the key-plate image (row 2).
- Bleed for her flexibility; the about $8,500 above the top joins her flexibility, it is not trimmed away.
- "Inking inside the line" (not a craft practice; CONCEIT s4).
- "Ink is permanent" or "cannot be removed": say "never taken off cleanly" (A13). Ink holds because each Treasury is
  held to its date, not because it cannot be sold.
- A US default as a smudge or a mis-cut: it is the paper (row 20).
- Inflation or a stronger Taiwan dollar "fading the ink": the dollars stay; what they buy changes.
- "Redraw the page" for any plan change: the two reasons are named in advance, never a redraw.
- The iBonds funds as iron gall (row 17).
- "More ink is always better": the all-to-floor option pays $140,761 and is stated as one figure, $130,000 (NUMBERS 4,
  C8). Pencil is kept on purpose, for her flexibility.
- Liu Kuo-sung's tearing as a mistake.
- Quoting the loose English rendering of the Wang Xizhi text, or claiming that calligraphers never correct (A17).
- Anything about how Laura herself draws (A22); "K stands for key" (A6); "blueline" as a verified term (A10).
- Puns on "issue", "draw" or "drawdown" (V2.4 guard 6).
