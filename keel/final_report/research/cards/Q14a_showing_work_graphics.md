## (b) What it means for each exhibit

- **Table 1, the holdings.** Stays a table (rows 3, 7). No pie or treemap of the weights (row 3), no sparkline or
  price history (row 12). Rows in maturity order, the order of the question (row 13); the subtotal set apart (Q28-027).
  The definition of "owned" sits in the sentence beside the table, never in it (row 23; CONCEIT guard 4).
- **Figure 1, the reserve.** One row per payment in date order (rows 6, 13), no line joining them. Draw only what
  differs between rows: each holding's cash date and the payment date as positions on one aligned date axis, to
  scale, with no axis break (rows 1, 2, 24, 29). Print the dollars as numbers in adjacent columns (own holding,
  backstop, total, left over): the totals are all $50,000, so bars of totals are redundant ink (row 8), and a split
  bar is a length judgment people do badly (row 2, Q28-010). Print "$0" in all ten rows (row 14). Direct labels and no
  legend (rows 22, 23). What is held now and what the 2028 deposit buys look different, labelled in plain words (row 23).
  At most four visual groups, muted grid only for looking up, one line of words naming the question (rows 8, 11, 23).
- **Table 2, five worlds.** Stays a table: about 20 numbers, five worlds by four outputs (row 7), and the failed range chart is what row 20
  predicts. Today's forward rates come first as the comparator (row 11). Every chance is a count out of 100 simulated
  futures, the same denominator in every cell and in the note (rows 15, 19, 20, 22), with "simulated" in the heading
  (row 19). If 3c's confidence is ever pictured, use 20 dots on one dollar axis (row 21), never a band or fan. The stated
  range's ends may be hard lines, because Laura owns them when she names them; the spread's ends may not (row 20).
- **Table 3, monitoring.** A look-up table of words and numbers (rows 4, 7). Each row prints its normal value and its
  action level (rows 5, 11). Rows in the order each signal can first be seen, 2027 to 2033 (row 13). The two reasons to
  change are set apart as the only rows that act (row 23).

  wrong intuition first, then the count over all ten payments: ten promises each kept 99 times in 100 are all kept in
  about 90 of 100 futures (rows 26, 27). Cite Tversky and Kahneman there: a founding paper of behavioural finance, it names the
  intuition the aha corrects.
