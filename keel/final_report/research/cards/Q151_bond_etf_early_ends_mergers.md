- **Claim 1: KEEP, and widen.** No iBonds fund of any kind (45 of 45) ended more than 16 days early, merged or had its end moved; two ended about two weeks early (IBTA, IBCC), both earlier, never later. The sponsor's own closures page shows a muni fund (MUAB) ending a year early; its filings show the page is a year off. Across about 82 ended defined-maturity ETFs at three sponsors, the only ends months early were two emerging-market debt BulletShares of $26m and $34m, closed in a 26-fund cull "to invest in high demand product areas". IBTM held $634m on 12 Nov 2026.
  - **Judge's note:** the closures page of the very fund family Laura uses lists a term fund ending a year early; its own supplement shows it ended in the year in its name; the page is a year off.
- **Claim 2: CHANGE the words, keep "no probability".** A board's call still has no modelled odds, but the reference class can now be stated in words: about 82 ended, 0 merged, 0 extended, 2 small non-Treasury funds ended months early. "Not modelled" can read "not modelled; in about 82 defined-maturity ETFs that have ended, none merged or ran past its date".
- **Claim 3: KEEP.** Every early or moved end found paid holders cash at net asset value within 1 to 7 days of the last trade (iShares 43 of 45 on the page, the two outliers page errors; Invesco 1 to 4 days; First Trust's closed-end FIV at its termination date), after 14 to 86 days' notice. None offered anything for the interest the holdings would still have paid. So a swap's cost is one purchase (spread about 0.05%), inside the margin; the lost interest is the holder's alone, as NUMBERS 3 says.
- **Claim 4: KEEP for the scheduled end, CHANGE for the three events.** All 13 iShares ending notices read (2012-2026) came 48 to 66 days before the last trade, and the six Treasury notices fell 28 Sep to 17 Oct, so "Notice by 31 October" fits. But the scheduled end needs no notice at all ("without further notice"), and the events in row 4's trigger come on the board's calendar: iShares announces its other closures in June (13 Jun 2025, 12 Jun 2026) with last trades in August (9 of 14 closure batches since 2014). Invesco's scheduled-end notices came 14 days ahead; BondBloxx's early closure 16 days.
  - **Judge's note:** a watch set only for September-October would miss the month in which this sponsor actually announces its early closures.
- **Claim 5: KILL.** Every end move found among ETFs went earlier: the BulletShares family from "on or about December 31" to "December 15" (BSJK ended 17 Dec 2020, 14 days before its first stated date), IBTA, IBCC, BSAE and BSBE by about two weeks, First Trust's FIV by 48 days. Ends moved later only in closed-end term funds, each time by a shareholder vote and with a tender for up to 100% of shares at net asset value, so holders who wanted the date got the money anyway.
  - **Judge's note:** the event 3e lists as the risk (an end moved past a payment) has no precedent in about 82 term ETFs; the event with precedent (two weeks earlier) costs almost nothing.
- **For GOLDMINE 27 (2.2):** iShares closed all eleven of its target-date ETFs on 14 Oct 2014, years to decades before their dates, while it has run every iBonds term fund to within 16 days of its date. Its prospectus says an iBonds fund "should not be confused with a target date fund".
