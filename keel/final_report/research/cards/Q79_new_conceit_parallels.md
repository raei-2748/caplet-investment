## Verdicts in one screen

- **C1 "Ink" can be explained as signing: KILL.** Taiwan's own Civil Code separates the two: a land transfer 「應以書面為之」
  (must be in writing) yet 「非經登記，不生效力」 (has no effect until registered) (art. 758). NSW does the same (Real
  Property Act s 41). The fields that do fix a figure by signing (golf's scorecard, the navy's deck log) fix a record,
  not an ownership. So the definition in 2.0 keeps the word "owned" (card Q42), and Taiwan's law shows why.
  Judge's note: everyday English hears "inked" as "signed"; the residency's own country writes into law that a signed
  deed passes nothing until the register holds it.
- **C2 A figure tied to a held object is fixed for good: CHANGE.** The kilogram was "exactly equal to the mass" of one
  cylinder in "a triple-locked vault", yet its copies "drifted" and the unit was re-based in 2018 (NIST). Zoology says
  "Once fixed, name-bearing types are stable" (ICZN 61.1.3) only as a rule of naming. Laura's figure holds because each
  Treasury is held to the date it pays; an iBonds fund pays its net asset value (TNA l.15), which is why the rule holds
  0.5% back and rounds down. Say "owned and held to its date", never "fixed" for a fund's payout (TNA l.13).
- **C3 The 2031 range is a forecast with error bars: KILL.** Geology prints its ages the way the report prints "$95,000 to
  $115,000 on today's figures": "Numerical ages are subject to ongoing revision and do not define units ...; only GSSPs
  do" (ICS chart, 2024). The range is defined by a written rule tied to owned Treasuries; the dollar figures are today's
  reading of that rule, and each end becomes one figure on its date.
  Judge's note: a reader who takes the range for a forecast has it backwards; it is a definition with a reading attached.
- **C4 Announce first, check later, is normal, so stating early is harmless: KILL for Laura.** Records announced first
  get struck late: El Azizia's 58 °C stood about 90 years (1922 to 2012, Keel arithmetic) before the WMO found it
  "invalid"; climbers stood on Manaslu's fore-summit from 1956 to 2021. A navigator's dead-reckoning error "increases
  over time" since the last fix (Bowditch); the plan's own version: a top stated 1, 3, 6 or 12 months before the move is
  not yet owned in 2.9%, 6.2%, 8.7% or 10.7% of simulated futures (NUMBERS l.378).
  Judge's note: the danger in stating early is not a bad forecast on the day; it is an error circle that grows every
  month since the last fix.
- **C5 Understating is as bad as overstating: KILL.** Golf's Rule 3.3b(3): a returned hole score higher than the real one
  stands; a lower one and "you are disqualified" (R&A). A racehorse that fails "to carry at least his correct weight
  over the entire distance" is disqualified (AGCO 8.03). The range rule has the same one-way shape: each end is rounded
  down, about $8,500 above the top joins her flexibility, and stating more than she owns would cost the "credibility"
  the case names.
