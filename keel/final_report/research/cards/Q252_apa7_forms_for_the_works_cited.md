## Verdicts in one screen

- **Claim 1: CHANGE.** The content is sound (S1-S5), but the form is not yet APA 7.
  - **The fix list:** 32 entry-level fixes touching 61 entries, one list-level fix (merge the S5 additions into one A-Z list) and one global fix.
  - **Letters and order, the commonest fault.** Six same-author sets carry letters against APA's title order: Heaton & Lucas, California State Lottery, Caplet, SIPC, MSCI and BlackRock. Five authors put n.d. works after dated works: BlackRock, the Fed's Board, the Treasury, Shiller and DGBAS. Five letters have no partner: Kresge 2011a, RBA 2022a, Treasury 1992a, 文化部 2025c and Wharton 2026a. Vanguard's pair starts at 2026b, with no 2026a.
  - **APA's rule.** Same author and same date take "a lowercase letter after the year ... assigned ... alphabetically by title (Section 9.47)", and "Place citations with no date first".
  - **The global fault.** All 76 journal entries put the comma after the journal name inside the italics ("*Econometrica, 74*(2)"). APA: "Do not italicize the comma between the journal title and volume number" (Creating an APA Style reference list guide, 2022).
  - **Judge's note:** a reader checks the References page in two seconds by its order. A list that runs "J.P. Morgan, Jorion" or "S&P, Samuelson" looks unchecked, even though every source in it was opened at source.
- **Claim 2: KILL.** APA follows the Bluebook for legal references (Manual chapter 11, per APA's guide to what's new).
  - The rule, as a library guide quotes the Manual: "the title or name of a court case is not italicized in the reference list entry and it is italicized in the in-text citation". Give "all citations" when a case has parallel ones.
  - All three cases in the draft are italic. Allegheny College lacks its parallel cite, 159 N.E. 173 (read on OpenJurist, vLex and HallApproved).
  - **Judge's note:** a court case is the one title APA sets in roman type in the list. A finance judge who studied law will see it.
- **Claim 3: CHANGE.** APA's default is to transliterate a non-Roman title and give the English in brackets.
  - APA (Adams, 2021b): "If it is not practical or desirable to transliterate", the original script may stay.
  - Wharton requires 12-pt Times New Roman, which has no Chinese characters, so seven entries would print in a second font. Keel's call is Hanyu Pinyin, as in APA's own Kaohsiung example.
  - The epigraph is the exception (row 43): no reference entry, so its script stays on the title page.
- **Claim 4: KILL.** "Include a retrieval date only if the work is unarchived and designed to change over time. Most references do not include retrieval dates" (Elements of reference list entries, 2025).
  - Keep the date on fund pages, live data series, the closures list and the campaign tally.
  - Drop it on fixed PDFs: the Grant County pledge form, and the iBonds case study if it is dated.
- **Claim 5: KILL.** "The source of an epigraph does not usually appear in the reference list unless the work is a scholarly book or journal" (Works included in a reference list, 2022).
  - 意在筆前 needs no entry.
  - A credit line under it is enough. If used, it says the attribution is traditional (card Q241 row 027: 傳, "traditionally").
  - **Judge's note:** none; it only saves an entry that would look odd.
- **What changes for writers on 27 Nov (in-text forms):**
  - Use the year and letter only, e.g., (Heaton & Lucas, 2000a) for the *Journal of Finance* paper.
  - Use both years for reprints: Samuelson (1963/1994), Perold and Sharpe (1988/1995).
  - Italicize case names in the text.
