## Best five for the report

1. **Row 21 (the panel's median moves, the wording holds), for 3e area (1) and Table 2's note.** Two houses found since the fifteen sit above J.P. Morgan, and the median rises from about 6.5 to about 6.9. "Above the middle of published forecasts" survives every count, but the number in brackets survives only with its panel named.

3. **Row 14 (a "likely range" in Taiwan misses one time in three), for 3c and 3d.** Taiwan's climate service uses likelihood words as names for percentile bands. Laura's range after the move is not a band at all, so it must never be called one in any language.
4. **Rows 2 and 4 (Florida's rule at the Board's own minutes, and loosened since), for 3a.** The 2006 line between promised and spare money is primary-sourced. Twenty years on, the cap is gone, but shares still sit only in the surplus.
5. **Row 12 (信心度 is also a 95% test), for 3c.** In Taiwan "confidence level" carries a statistician's 90% or 95%. That is a reason, in the co-sponsors' own language, for 3c's rule: no "95%" or "confidence interval" label, and "certain" with the stated range as its subject.
