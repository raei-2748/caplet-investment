## Best ten in my files for Caplet's FINAL plan
1. **Row 4, make each promise safe on or before the day it becomes one.** One rule produces all three dates in the FINAL (ladder 2027, floor 2028, the half moved before 2031). The aha: the timeline is not three separate choices, it is one principle, and it is a fact about the promise rather than a guess about markets.
2. **Row 3, the covered call nobody is paid for.** Once Laura names a capped figure, shares left behind it give the falls to the facility and the rises to Laura. The aha: the FINAL's 2031 move is not caution; it removes an option she would otherwise be writing for free.
3. **Row 13, the wrong-way corner.** The one thing that hurts a hold-to-date ladder (yields falling and staying down) comes with a recession, the same recession the FINAL says shrinks a writer's income. The aha: the career thesis and the reinvestment risk named in TNA note 1 are one risk, and the FR can show how the plan treats it.
4. **Row 81, correlation makes the chain certain.** Ten independent 99% bets miss one about one time in ten; one issuer makes them a single bet. The aha: for the payments, diversifying would hurt, which is why the FINAL names exactly one exception (a US default). Simple arithmetic a judge can check.
5. **Row 80, four ways fixed promises have failed** (Alabama PACT, Equitable Life, Bobby Bonilla, Japan's life insurers). The aha: "high degree of certainty" becomes a checklist of real failures; the FINAL shuts three doors and the fourth (reinvestment) is the one TNA note 1 already names.
6. **Row 2, "sum certain" vs fixed percentage.** US trust law already separates Laura's two kinds of money (annuity trust vs unitrust), and the IRS test assumes a rate where the ladder buys one. The aha: a government's own standard for fixed-dollar promises, met by buying instead of assuming. VERIFIED.
7. **Row 39, the ability to hold, not the intention** (UK banks 2009; Treasuries falling in March 2020). The aha: "what if Treasuries fall?" is answered with a test the plan passes (nothing can force a sale before each date), which is what the FINAL's "held to the end, these pay what they say" rests on.
8. **Row 15, escrowed debt is judged on the escrow.** Cities that set Treasuries aside are treated as having paid; co-sponsors can judge Laura's bottom on what is already set aside. The aha: an existing market convention for exactly the FINAL's floor promise (with the honest limit: no legal escrow, and IBTM pays NAV).
9. **Row 6, the seed-money experiments.** A larger announced lead gift drew nearly six times the giving. The aha: the owned floor is not only safety, it is fundraising leverage; this is client knowledge backed by field evidence.
10. **Row 83, risk only the interest.** The share slice is about the floor's expected interest, and Norway's rule and Merton's formula land near the same ~21%. The aha: the FINAL's "most ... the rest" split of the 2028 deposit is set by a rule, not by taste.
