## 2. SHOULD LAND HIGH

1. **H8-113**, part 3b (Book 17 rank 2, "why the floor is bought in January 2028, not in 2031"). It may go instead in
   3c's clause "so the figures are never a promise before then".
   - **Proposed SKELETON note:** "Thesis 3's real case: Harvard's endowment fell about 30% in 2009; it slowed, then
     paused Allston, citing that loss and borrowing limits (W-05 true line)."
   - **Evidence:**
     - Store H8-113, which also carries H3-082's sources.
     - Card Q2c row 12 (VERIFIED): the endowment went from $36.9bn to $26.0bn, a 29.5% fall in value; the FY2009 return
       was -27.3%.
     - Card Q2c row 17: Allston slowed (Shore & Rothenberg 2012, REFERENCES_APA row 339).
     - Harvard University (2009), FY2009 financial report (REFERENCES_APA row 296).
     - W-05 N1. The quotes are VERIFIED, and so are the dates: slowed in Feb 2009, paused indefinitely in Dec 2009. W-05
       gives the true line: "When Harvard's endowment fell about 30% in 2009, it slowed and then paused its Allston
       science building, citing the endowment loss and the limits on borrowing that followed."
     - Harvard Magazine (Feb 2009, Dec 2009) and Faust's letter (18 Feb 2009) are REF.
   - **Why HIGH:** the plan has no real case of the failure that thesis 3 guards against. A grep of SKELETON,
     BOLD_THESES and EVALUATE finds no Harvard, Allston or endowment line. The richest university endowment slowing and
     then pausing a named building when its value fell about 30% is something a judge would remember. It turns
     "name a figure only once the money behind it is a bond" from a rule of thumb into a lesson with a date. Laura's
     bottom is owned from January 2028, and the top before the 2031 meetings.
   - **Limits:**
     - Never say that the building money sat in the endowment, or that the fall alone caused the pause. W-05 marked
       that "so" OVERSTATED; Harvard's stated reasons include its borrowing capacity and credit rating.
     - Leave out Yale and Princeton. W-05 found Yale's quote applied too widely, and Princeton's $695m is a news report
       only.
     - One clause and one figure. The 30% is a sourced outside fact (card Q2c), not a model figure, so it needs no
       NUMBERS section.
