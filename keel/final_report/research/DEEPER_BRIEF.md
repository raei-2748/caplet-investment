# One level deeper: the brief for a follow-up card (from Thu 19 Nov 2026, evening)

Every run in `../OVERDRIVE_PLAN.md` takes some of the previous run's strongest ideas one level deeper, so the best
finds get followed rather than left at their first page. This is the brief for those workers. Read
`BIG_IDEAS_BRIEF.md` first (the client, the frozen plan and the test every idea must pass) and
`ROUND2_WORKER_BRIEF.md` (card format and word rules), then this page.

## What "one level deeper" means

You are given one idea that a researcher found and a rater scored highly, and the card it came from (the parent card).
Read the parent card whole first, so you never repeat its rows. Then do four things:

1. **Read the primary source whole**, not the passage the parent card quoted: the whole letter, act, chapter, statute,
   debate, judgment or paper, in the best edition you can reach. Say which edition and what you read.
2. **Test the parallel's structure.** Which relation in the source does work in Laura's plan (a cause, a sequence, a
   rule and its exception), and is it really there once the whole source is read? If the fuller reading weakens or
   overturns the parent card's idea, say so plainly: that is a result, not a failure.
3. **Find where it breaks**, more exactly than the parent card did, and what the break teaches about the plan.
4. **Find its best companion**: the one other true fact or source, from any field, that makes the idea sharper,
   predicts something checkable about the plan, or shows the same relation from the opposite side.

## What to write

One card, `keel/final_report/research/cards/<id>_<short_name>.md`, in the BIG_IDEAS_BRIEF format, with these changes:
- Under the title bullets, add **Parent:** the parent card and row ids, and **What the deeper reading changed:** one
  plain sentence (strengthened, sharpened, corrected or overturned, and how).
- Two to four ideas, each in three plain sentences, as BIG_IDEAS_BRIEF says.
- 15-30 store rows in the ten-column table, never padded, none repeating the parent card.
- "Best three for the report", and a last line, **Where it lands:** the one part of the report it serves and the
  decision it explains, in one sentence.

Write every idea sentence so it makes sense on one reading: people doing things in the order they happen, every
"because" a real cause, every term set up before it is used, no names the reader was never given. No defending
clauses: if a point needs one, the point is weaker than it looks; say that instead.

## Rules

Real sources read at source (URL or edition, date read; quotations of at most 25 words); anything not read at source
is UNVERIFIED. No invented people, events or quotations. Check any figure about the plan against
`keel/final_report/NUMBERS.md` and any claim about what Caplet submitted against `keel/final_report/CANON.md`. Never
contact anyone or post anything. No AI-use notes. If Parallel or Firecrawl credits run out, use WebSearch and WebFetch;
list anything you could not reach in a Gaps line. Work in `keel/final_report/research/cards/` only; do not run git.
Report back in under 100 words: what the deeper reading changed, and the card's path.
