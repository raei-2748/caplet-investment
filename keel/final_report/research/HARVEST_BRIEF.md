## Caplet's FINAL plan in parts (the tags)
- **CAREER**: Laura's career is a large holding of shares she can't sell, cyclical (advances, speaking fees, licences
  shrink in recessions, when shares fall and co-sponsors cut budgets); so the portfolio counterbalances it.
- **CERTAINTY**: barring a US default, no payment depends on a share price or anyone else's money; payment money goes
  straight into Treasuries; only unpromised money goes into shares. Includes defining "high degree of certainty".
- **LADDER**: Jan 2027, the first deposit buys 10 Treasuries (T-bonds or iShares iBonds Term Treasury ETFs), each
  maturing just before it is needed; held to the end they pay what they say; about $300,000 at current yields.
- **COMPLETE2028**: if yields fall and the ladder costs more than $300,000, the 2028 deposit completes it.
- **FLOOR**: most of the 2028 deposit buys a Treasury fund maturing late 2032; its payout is the bare minimum Laura can
  promise co-sponsors.
- **CEILING**: the rest goes into VT (world shares); its returns set the maximum facility contribution; markets move
  the top of the band, never the bottom.
- **HALFKEPT**: half the value of the shares is promised to no one: flexibility for overruns, a stronger Taiwan dollar,
  a co-sponsor falling short.
- **MOVE2031**: before the 2031 co-sponsor meetings, the other half of the shares moves into Treasuries, so whatever
  figure Laura chooses is money she already owns.
- **RESERVE2033**: from 2033 the Treasuries are the operating reserve, paying out one January at a time.
- **TWOREASONS**: only two reasons to change the plan: a missed deposit or a Treasury default; a market fall is not one.
- **COSPONSOR**: the 2031 range, its confidence, and the fundraising text (FR job; the case asks for it).
- **INFLATION_TWD**: inflation, Taiwan building costs, the Taiwan dollar.
- **REINVEST**: the ladder's interest is reinvested before it is needed (TNA note 1 names this risk); what if rates fall.
- **PROCESS**: how the team decided, learned, implemented in WInS (Articulation criterion).
- **OTHER**: anything else worth keeping.
