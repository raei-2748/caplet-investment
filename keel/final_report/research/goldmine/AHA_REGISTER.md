# The 'aha' register: the spine (Keel's decision, Sun 15 Nov 2026, night)

Caplet, Book 19: "The 'aha' moment in the judge ... is imperative and indeed central to our team's strategy. And there
should be multiple." An aha here is a place where a Wharton judge thinks "I had that backwards", backed by a NUMBERS
figure or a verified source, doing a job Wharton asks that part to do (Book 18: a purpose for everything), and told
once.

Built from three sweeps of the whole repo read tonight: `AHA_REGISTER_candidates.md` (30 ranked ahas from the research),
`SWEEP_A_instructions.md` section 3 (from the books and the ideas they produced) and `SWEEP_B_ips_library.md` section C
(from Keel's IPS-era library), plus the conceit's own ahas (`../../CONCEIT.md` V2.2, card Q47). Every figure below is
from `../../NUMBERS.md` with its section, or marked Keel arithmetic; it is re-checked against NUMBERS when written on 27
Nov. Treasuries may be called guaranteed, locked, set in stone or risk-free throughout (Book 19).

The report is 9-12 pages; ten ahas give the judge about one a page, at most two in a part. Each carries one turn of the
conceit where one fits, which is how the conceit and the ahas reinforce each other.
