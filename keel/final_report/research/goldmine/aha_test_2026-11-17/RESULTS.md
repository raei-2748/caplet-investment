## Fixes the readers asked for, before any item lands

- **Row 10 (version B).**
  - "No payment short, by design" holds from January 2028, once the 2028 deposit's first dollars buy the top-up and
    the extra Treasuries (A, C). Date it: "from January 2028".
  - Two readers asked what "none" cost Laura against 1 in 31 (expected building money or her flexibility). Give the
    price in one clause from NUMBERS (8s or section 4) if the register carries it; if not, it is an open item for the
    numbers owner, never a figure made up for the sentence.
- **Taiwan's founders (item 6).**
  - Say how the thirteen were found ("cases found, not a census", card Q100 row 41).
  - Say what "the founder paid first" means: before any public or co-sponsor money (A, C).
  - Say what the others did: they survive, on next year's income as far as the record shows (card Q100 row 41: "survivors exist"; two of the four stops had other causes too).
  - "None had a year of running costs set aside" rests on registered funds, which are legal minimums (card Q100 row 35): say "no registered fund found held a year of running costs".
  - IT Park's trade was commercial photography, which digital cameras ended over years; date the stop, not the cause.
- **Comfort letter (item 4).**
  - "No fall in markets can change either end" becomes "no fall in markets can change what either end pays when its
    Treasuries mature" (A, C: a fund's final payout is not fixed to the cent; never call it fixed).
  - It needs row 6's answer to "why a range at all" nearby (all three asked). Where 3d uses it, the bottom is said
    once as the part held since January 2028 (Mon 16 Nov's settled wording), and the word "policy" stays on Malaysia Mining's side (coherence reader 7, M12).
  - Owning is not committing (C): Laura could still spend her Treasuries elsewhere. Say what is stated is owned and
    named for the facility, never that a crash "cannot change" her choice.
  - The plan's own "ever-reliable Treasuries" reads as an advert to two readers; the report evaluates and never
    repeats the IPS's words (Book 17).
- **The 1982 bond (item 3).**
  - The fee reads as a contradiction (A, C: "if waiting cash earns nothing, which fund is charging?"). Say it in one
    clause: waiting cash is assumed to earn nothing, and the bill fund that would hold it still charges its 0.15% fee.
  - "The strictest rate practice used" needs its holder or goes: say "the strictest we found" (card Q104 claim 2).
  - Label 9.29% as a half-yearly bond-equivalent rate (about 9.5% compounded yearly, C). Name the bank: the Federal
    Reserve Bank of New York. Keep one example (A): the 1982 bond.
  - Card Q104's row on the competition's ban needs its source beside it: Wharton's Week 4 email bans TIPS and STRIPS
    for both contributions (CLAUDE.md "Current status", 22 Oct).
