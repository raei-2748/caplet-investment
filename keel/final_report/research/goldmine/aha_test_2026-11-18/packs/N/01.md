into Treasuries before the 2031 meetings (the move), and the team's model assumes J.P. Morgan's 7.5% a year, above the
middle of published forecasts (median about 6.5%). Yet the forecast barely touches the figure she states. She states
each end in whole $5,000 steps, rounded down, and run on each of fifteen investment houses' published long-term
forecasts for world shares, from about -2.5% a year (GMO) to 9.0% (BlackRock), thirteen give the same $115,000 top; the
two lowest give $110,000. What moves the figure is speaking early. A top named 1, 3, 6 or 12 months before the move
would turn out larger than the move could buy in 2.9%, 6.2%, 8.6% or 10.6% of simulated futures. So she names the top
only after the move. US federal budget law has a parallel: under the Antideficiency Act, as the Government
Accountability Office reads it, the wrong is not only a payment missed, since "The very act of obligating the United
States to make a payment when the necessary funds are not already in the account is also a violation".
