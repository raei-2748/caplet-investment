## Fixes the readers asked for (carried into `../../SYNTHESES.md` and to the SKELETON worker)

- **L1 (two readers, X and N):** "Her condition" was read as making Laura's own pledge all-or-nothing. Say whose: the
  condition is on the co-sponsors' pledges ("if the pledges aren't all made by December 2032, none of the co-sponsors
  has to pay"), and her own $115,000 comes first either way (SKELETON 3d, voice round 9).
- **L2 (X):** "nearly sixfold" came from seed money at two-thirds of the goal against one-tenth; $115,000's share of the
  building's total is not known, so the effect size does not carry. Cite the direction only.
- **L3 (X):** "the most original use of the structure" depends on her figure being large enough to count as seed; say
  what it does (she can go first unconditionally), never that she is the campaign's seed.
- **A1 (X, Y):** "every co-sponsor's target is exact" overclaims: the building's total can still overrun. Say "her side
  of the sum is exact".
- **A2 (N, Y):** A cites fundraising's rule against counting a pledge whose conditions may fail, while 3d's own ask makes
  the co-sponsors' pledges conditional on one total by December 2032. Keep that rule for why Laura states nothing before
  she owns it (3c), never in 3d beside the ask.
- **M1 (X):** the AACE standard says contingency "usually excludes" escalation and currency: a convention, never "by
  definition".
- **M2 (X, Y):** her flexibility stays in shares, which can fall in the same years costs and the Taiwan dollar rise. That
  is 3e's honest limit already (NUMBERS 8q); 3b says it once beside the clause, never as a promise it can meet.
- **K1 (X):** "What Illinois never listed: a US default" is rhetorical, since Illinois's matched Treasuries carry the
  same risk. Drop the contrast; keep Illinois's three named risks against the plan's dates.
- **D1 (X):** "she should stop leaning on her sponsor as soon as its money is in" is close to circular; the real covenant
  exposure is 2027, before the deposit arrives (that is 2.3's order of purchase and 3e's first note).
- **E1 (X):** share growth is "the largest expected gain", never "the likeliest gain".
- **E2 (X):** say what the 0.5% is a share of: each end's zero-interest value, before rounding down (NUMBERS section 4).
- **G1 (X, Y):** buying extra ordinary Treasuries was not "the only way left": choosing the lowest-coupon Treasury at each
  maturity (issues from the low-rate years) also cuts the coupon cash that has to wait. Say the plan's way, and name the
  low-coupon route as the one not taken, without a figure (none is in NUMBERS).
- **H1 (X, Y, N):** "shares given to it count at what they fetch when sold" is wrong as worded: gifts of shares are
  booked at their value on the day received. Card Q43-039 supports only that many offices sell them on receipt so the
  project spends a fixed sum.
- **H2 (X):** in a bought deal the issuer gets the fixed sum and the bank keeps the price risk; Laura moving her own
