**Item A.** When Laura tells co-sponsors in 2031 that she will give $115,000 towards the residency's building, each of
them hears something else: what is left to raise, the building's total less her figure. That remainder is their target.
In a laboratory study of groups that had to reach a threshold together to avoid a loss (Barrett and Dannenberg, 2012),
groups that knew the threshold exactly avoided the loss in 18 of 20 cases; when the threshold was uncertain, the loss
came in 16 of 20, while uncertainty about the size of the loss did no such harm. Fundraising's own counting rules
already refuse to count a pledge whose conditions may fail. So a figure Laura stated while her shares could still fall
would be a target moving under every co-sponsor. She states her range only once both ends are Treasuries she owns, and
names the top as her figure, so every co-sponsor's target is exact: the residency's total less $115,000.
