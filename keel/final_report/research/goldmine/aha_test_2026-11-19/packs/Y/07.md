**Item G.** Laura's ten payments are paid by ten Treasury holdings, one ending before each January, and the interest
those holdings pay before 2033 has to wait, unspent, for years. The plan sizes the ten so that every payment is made
even if all that waiting money earns nothing, and in January 2028 it buys about $36,800 of extra Treasuries for that
alone; without them, the ten as bought in January 2027 would be about $54,600 short on that test. It looks like an
amateur's caution. It is the oldest test in the trade: Martin Leibowitz described a dedicated bond portfolio in 1986 as
'totally passive management' with 'no reinvestment'. The trade's usual ways of passing it are closed to Laura.
Zero-coupon Treasuries, which pay nothing until their end, are banned in this competition. Immunisation, the other
classic method, needs trading whenever rates move, and the plan's policy allows no trade for a market move. With zeros
banned and trading ruled out, buying extra ordinary Treasuries is the only way left to pass the old test.
