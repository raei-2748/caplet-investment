# Aha register: candidates (research worker, Sun 15 Nov 2026, night)

What this is: a ranked list of the moments where a Wharton judge should stop and think "I had that backwards". The
standard is the owner's: several ahas, intricate, with parallels from fields far from investing, each with a startling
new angle. This is notes, not prose (no Final Report prose before Fri 27 Nov).

How it was built:
- Every figure is from `keel/final_report/NUMBERS.md`, quoted with its section. Nothing below quotes a void row.
  Where a figure is Keel arithmetic on two NUMBERS figures it says so (for example $37,625 = $325,563 - $287,938, 8b).
- Every outside fact is from a card row or store row that is marked VERIFIED or keep, with its id.
- "Prominence in SKELETON now" was checked by grepping `SKELETON.md` for the figure, source and id (Sun 15 Nov night):
  - **headline**: a ranked judgement or a part's spine carries it;
  - **buried in a row**: it is there, but only as a clause, a reservoir row or a superseded row;
  - **absent**: neither the figure nor the source appears.
- Today's team rules apply. Treasuries may be called guaranteed, locked, set in stone or risk-free, and "barring a US
  default" is not added. So no row is marked down for sounding sure. The risks recorded are a hostile judge's facts and
  repeats of the frozen IPS. The IPS is quoted by line from `FINAL_IPS_as_submitted.txt`, and the report evaluates it,
  never repeats it.
- Banned words do not appear here as proposed wording. Kept out: "optimal", "a natural hedge", "endowment" for the
  reserve, "full funding", "prudent", "two-thirds scale" and "backstop" (the NUMBERS term for the extra Treasuries).

Ranking: how far the aha turns a judge's usual view around, times how hard its evidence is, times how well it does a
job Wharton asks a part to do. The ranks are 1 to 30.
