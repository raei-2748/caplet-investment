# Scoring sheet for the blind re-rank, Mon 16 Nov

Notes for the Final Report store, not report prose. For the three blind readers. Evidence: card
`../cards/Q27_reflection_rubric.md` (Tue 10 Nov 2026).

**The job.** Pick the three or four real events that will carry the Team Reflection (about 600 words). Wharton: "Conclude
your report by explaining how your team's research, collaboration, disagreements, decision-making, and learning
influenced its final recommendation. Focus on the overall competition experience and do not repeat the analysis
presented in your Trading Notes Analysis." Criterion: "The team explains how research, collaboration, disagreement,
decision-making, uncertainty, and learning shaped its investment thinking and final recommendation."

**Before you score**
1. Read each event only from its corrected fact sheet. If a figure was VOID on the number register, the sheet gives the
   corrected figure (and title). Score the event as corrected; no credit for a void number or title.
2. A figure marked "that day's model" with a date is fine. A figure with no source counts as absent. A gain or loss in
   the trading simulator is never evidence that the plan was right.
3. Do not guess who did the work. Score only what the sheet shows.
4. Read all five rows first, then score each criterion on its own. Never give one overall mark: readers agree far less
   on an overall mark than criterion by criterion.

**Five criteria, 1 to 5** (2 and 4 sit between the anchors)

| Criterion (Wharton's words first) | 1 | 3 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| A. Influence. "influenced its final recommendation"; "shaped its investment thinking" | No link to the plan Laura was given; drafting, mood or process only | A link is claimed but loose: a general lesson, or a figure tied to no part of the plan | Names the part of the recommendation it changed, or tested and kept, with the state before and after; register figures at both ends where they exist |
| B. Reasoning. "decision-making"; Guide: "the reasoning, assumptions, and tradeoffs" | Says what happened, in date order | A reason, but no option rejected and no cost | The option rejected, the evidence that decided it, and what was given up |
| C. Disagreement. "collaboration, disagreements" | No other view, or a disagreement claimed with nothing to show it | An objection or outside verdict, but the other side is thin, or settled by "we discussed it" | Two real positions; the losing one put so its holder would accept it; how it was settled (a test, a vote, a source); each side tied to a role |
| D. Uncertainty and learning. "uncertainty, and learning" | No learning, or a slogan; or a result credited to skill that the sheet calls timing | Learning stated but generic, or more certainty than the evidence gives | What was believed, what tested it, what is still unknown; for a success, what was luck and what was decision |
| E. New and checkable. "do not repeat the analysis presented in your Trading Notes Analysis"; "authentic" | Mainly retells a Trading Note or the IPS, or nothing can be checked | Partly repeats them, or needs inside knowledge to follow | Dated, sourced, clear to an outsider, and adds what the Trading Notes and IPS do not say |

**Caps.** An event dated after the IPS went in (Sat 7 Nov, 09:00 Sydney) scores at most 3 on A: the Guide says a team
"may not redesign your strategy after observing the results". An event that mainly retells a Trading Note scores 1 on E.

**Totals and ties.** Score = mean of the three readers' totals (5 to 25); one reader never decides. A gap under 1.0 is a
tie: break it on A, then E. Where two readers differ by 2 or more on a criterion, each writes one line why. No conferring
before scoring.

**The set (the lead, after scoring).** At least one event scores 4 or 5 on C. At least one is a success or a decision
kept after a real, dated test, told with what was luck. No two tell one story (the ladder's cost through 2026 is one).
None retells the Trading Notes.
