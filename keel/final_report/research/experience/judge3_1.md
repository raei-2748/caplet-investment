# Blind re-rank, pool 3: reader 1

Lens: a Wharton finance professor who marks the Team Reflection criterion by criterion, and gives credit only to
reasoning a judge can check.

Read, in order and nothing else: `RERANK_RUBRIC.md`, the submitted IPS, the submitted Trading Notes Analysis,
`BLIND_POOL_3.md`. No conferring.

How the lens was applied. A figure earns full credit when a judge could recompute it or find it: a public Treasury
curve, a bar written down before the test, a quoted record, the register. It earns less when it rests only on an
unpublished model run on a plan since revised, and nothing when it has no source. No event is dated after the IPS
(Sat 7 Nov, 09:00 Sydney), so the cap on A does not apply; where a sheet's "after" leans on a model run after 7 Nov, it
is credited as what the team understands now, not as what shaped the plan.
