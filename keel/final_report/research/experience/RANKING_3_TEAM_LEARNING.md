## 1. The two tests, and the score

A moment is eligible only if it passes both tests.

- **Own (the team's own work):** 2 = a teammate noticed it, argued it and decided it; 1 = the team decided it, but the
  decisive analysis was Claude's (a model run, a price check, a memo); 0 = Keel's or Claude's event (a draft, a click,
  a recommendation), or an event about directing Keel.
- **Change (learning):** 2 = a belief before, a trigger, a different belief or behaviour after, with the reason;
  1 = a new rule or habit, with no stated change of belief; 0 = a decision or a fact, with nothing changed.
- **Record (whose words):** 2 = in the team's own record (its timeline in the Final Report doc's Experience tab, its
  Notes and Discussions doc, last year's report, the submitted Trading Notes and IPS); 1 = only in Keel's or Claude's
  files.

Eligible: Own at least 1 and Change at least 1. Rank: the sum, then Own. A moment scored Own 1 is marked so Caplet can
judge it; a reason found after the event is told as what the team understands now, never as what it thought then.
