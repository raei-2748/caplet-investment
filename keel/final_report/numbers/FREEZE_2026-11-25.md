## The withholding-tax question (NUMBERS section 9, card Q231 row 45)

**Question.** Is J.P. Morgan's 2027 LTCMA 7.5% for AC World equities in US dollars stated before or after foreign
withholding tax on dividends?

**Answer: after.** J.P. Morgan forecasts a net index.

- **J.P. Morgan, LTCMA Methodology Handbook, 2027 edition** ("As of 2027", data as of 30 Sep 2026; dated 15 Oct 2026),
  the FAQ "What indices are you forecasting?", Exhibit 4, "Indices by asset class" (p.39): "All Country World equity
  MSCI ACWI Net Total Return USD Index". By contrast the same exhibit gives "U.S. large cap S&P 500 Total Return Index",
  a gross index. Read through a third-party mirror, because am.jpmorgan.com refused the fetch:
  https://www.institutional-money.com/content/im/pdf/JPMAM_LTCMA_Methodology-handbook-report.pdf
- **The 2026 edition, on J.P. Morgan's own domain**, carries the same row:
  https://am.jpmorgan.com/content/dam/jpm-am-aem/global/en/insights/portfolio-insights/ltcma/noindex/ltcma-methodology-handbook.pdf
- Neither edition mentions withholding tax; the only tax words are "no fees or taxes" under exhibits.
- **MSCI, Index Calculation Methodology** (https://www.msci.com/index/methodology/latest/IndexCalc; November 2026 PDF,
  https://www.msci.com/eqb/methodology/meth_docs/MSCI_Index_Calculation_Methodology_Nov2026.pdf), on the Net Daily
  Total Return: "the regular cash dividend is reinvested after deduction of withholding tax by applying the maximum rate
  of the company's country of incorporation applicable to institutional investors ... International indexes: the maximum
  rate applicable to non-resident institutional investors who do not benefit from double taxation treaties."
- **Read** Tue 24 Nov 2026 UTC (Wed 25 Nov Sydney).

**What it means.** The 7.5% is already after dividend withholding tax, at MSCI's non-treaty rates. So the models leave
out no foreign dividend tax. If anything, the index deducts more than VT bears, since VT is a US fund that receives US
dividends without withholding and gets treaty rates abroad (Keel's reading, not sized). No figure moves; the top stays
$115,000.

**The register sentence to add (section 9, closing Q231 row 45):**

> J.P. Morgan's 7.5% for AC World Equity forecasts the MSCI ACWI Net Total Return USD Index (LTCMA Methodology
> Handbook, 2027 edition, Exhibit 4; read 25 Nov 2026), so it is after withholding tax on dividends at MSCI's
> non-treaty rates; the models leave out no foreign dividend tax, and every figure stands.
