(`SKELETON.md`) and the minimum requirements; columns are five tests drawn from the criteria and the two readers:

- **E, evidence:** the part rests on sources read at the source and figures from `NUMBERS.md`.
- **P, precision:** a fixed-income manager would accept every claim (WHY_WHARTON test 3).
- **C, clarity:** a first reader with a stack gets the point on one reading (tests 1, 2; EXPECTATIONS 3.1).
- **K, coherence:** consistent with the frozen IPS, Trading Notes, reflections and WInS book, and repeats none of them.
- **D, distinctiveness:** something a judge would remember, earned by thinking, not decoration (EXPECTATIONS 1.1, 2.5).

1 = missing or wrong; 2 = thin; 3 = adequate, like a good team's; 4 = strong; 5 = best in the competition. Before 27 Nov
the scores judge the plan and its evidence, since there is no prose. A score rises only when evidence lands. Each run
changes only the cells its work touched, with the reason in the log below.
