# Skeleton: the Final Report's parts, budgets and evidence (version 2, Tue 10 Nov 2026; first version Mon 9 Nov night)

Planning notes, not report text: each part lists its job, the claims it must establish (as notes), the figures it may
use and where the evidence is. Section names and limits are Wharton's (`WHARTON_NORTH_STAR.md` section 0). The argument
is `INTENT.md`. Scored by `SCORECARD.md`; changed by any run that has a reason, logged in `ARGUMENT_LOG.md`.

**Page arithmetic (version 2).** Wharton's limits are per section, and exhibits count inside them (tables and graphics
may be single-spaced; no appendix is offered). A double-spaced page holds about 280-300 words (to be measured in
Wharton's exact format by the template worker, card Q23). Title page 1; Section 2 at most 3 pages with Exhibit 1, so
about 700 words; Section 3 at most 8 pages with Exhibits 2 and 3 (about a page between them), so about 2,000 words.
Total prose about 2,700 words. Every budget below adds up to that, and a part that wants more must take it from another
part by name.

**One rule for every paragraph (stack reader, panel 1).** Each paragraph holds something that is in neither the IPS nor
the Trading Notes: a measure, a test, a decision or a number. The IPS is cited, never restated (Wharton: "without
repeating the content of your IPS").
