Method notes only, not findings (findings go to the store, figures to `NUMBERS.md`). Every run reads this page after
`DAILY_RUN.md` and adds what it learned about how to work: what raised a scorecard cell, what did not, and what the next
run should do instead. A lesson that stops helping is struck through with the date, never deleted. Newest at the top.
