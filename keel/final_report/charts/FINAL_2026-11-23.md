## The exhibits in the report

### Table 1. The Final WInS Portfolio at Cost on the Original $300,000 (Section 2)
- **Question.** What does the frozen portfolio hold, at cost, and which payment does each holding pay?
- **Data.** NUMBERS 8g (holdings), 8m (units against targets), 8 (e) (the reconciliation) and 4 (the plan in January 2028).
- **Form.** A table, because Wharton requires one: ticker, name, type, amount and % of every holding, totalling $300,000 and 100%. The reader's task is exact lookup, which sits outside Cleveland and McGill's ranking: no graphical encoding beats a printed number when the judge must check a sum.
- **Wharton format.**
  - Times New Roman 12 pt in the template; Liberation Serif, with the same metrics, in this build.
  - The columns add up to 6.5 in.
  - Body single-spaced; number, title and note double-spaced (APA 7).
  - Rules only at the top, under the heads, above subtotals and at the bottom. No colour.
  - Source line: "Data from the team's WInS trade record, 6-7 Oct 2026; team calculations." It has no works-cited entry yet (decision 6).
- **Page cost.** 22.9 lines with a 110-word note (15.9 without), one page. Section 2's prose ceiling falls to 578 words, against the tree's 580.
- **The one sentence.** "Nine-tenths of the $300,000 is in Treasuries that each pay one year's $50,000, and the one share fund was bought last with what was left."

### Figure 1. If Cash Waiting Earns Nothing, Each $50,000 Payment Is Still Met by Its Own Treasury and Extra Treasuries From the 2028 Deposit (3a)
- **Question.** Where does each year's $50,000 come from if no cash earns anything while it waits?
- **Data.** NUMBERS 8h (results_reserve fee block), 8a, 2 and 8 (e).
- **Form.** A table-figure.
  - Horizontal bars on one time axis, from January 2027 to the day each payment's cash arrives. The bar end is a position on a common scale, Cleveland and McGill's first-ranked task; the dotted tail is a length on that same scale.

  - Two greys tell a fund from a bond. A legend and the labels (tickers against coupons) carry the same distinction, so no colour carries meaning alone.
- **Wharton format.**
  - Times New Roman named in the SVG; 10-10.5 pt at the printed size; 6.5 x 3.19 in.
  - Source line: "Data from U.S. Department of the Treasury (n.d.); team calculations."
- **Page cost.** 19.3 lines with a 109-word note (12.3 without).
- **The one sentence.** "Even if no waiting cash earned a cent, every year's $50,000 is paid by its own Treasury plus a slice of the extra Treasuries."
