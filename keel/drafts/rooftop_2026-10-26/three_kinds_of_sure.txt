Sure comes in three strengths. The 2038-2042 Treasuries are sure by contract: the government owes that principal and those coupons, and only what we reinvest ever meets interest rates. The 2033-2037 iBonds and the 2031 floor are sure by design – each fund closes in its year and pays what its Treasuries are worth, close to plan but never to the dollar. The stock fund is a forecast. It only moves the top.
