=== PLACE 5 (the paragraph under 'Know thy rival') ===
--- Version 5p ---
We know this rival because we built it first: three-quarters stocks, until our own model sank it. Laura takes thoughtful risks; the strongest alternative takes one for her – keeping our ladder, putting the 2028 deposit mostly in stocks, buying its bottom in January 2031 with whatever they fetch that morning. Asked in our model to name our typical bottom, $145,000, it leaves Laura about $15,000 more on average by 2042, gift and leftovers counted.
