# The three north stars (Ray, 23 Oct 2026)

Every Keel member, every room run, every brief and every draft for Caplet carries these. Read this page at the start
of any run that touches the IPS, the Final Report, an episode or a draft. It sits above the room cards and the board,
but never above the hard rules (`pm/SELF_DIRECTION.md`, "Limits that do not move").

## Ray's words, verbatim

> I think there are three things which we must absolutely keep in mind above all else for this competition. The
> first is that we must have a genuinely distinctive team voice. It must carry throughout. And it must sound unique.
> Cerebral yet witty, passionate yet thoughtful, insightful yet confident: we must sound quite unlike any other
> team. Because that is the only way we will be able to set ourselves apart from other teams in a meaningful way in
> the eyes of the judges. I want our work to be the kind of thing they can honestly ENJOY reading. The second thing
> is that it would be nice to have some kind of single unifying extended metaphor or motif to carry throughout our
> IPS and Final Report. I'm often inclined to believe that it would be nice if it were a truly elaborate and vast
> and expansive metaphor for which almost every corner of our strategy would be able to be cleverly tied back in a
> powerful and creative and impactful and effective way. At the same time, keep in mind that this isn't a
> literature competition; it's an investment competition. So that isn't the primary goal. And we would much prefer
> that you demonstrate an extraordinary intellect and knowledge of investing and strategic thinking and so on than
> mastery of the English language. But a combination of BOTH the former and the latter certainly couldn't do any
> harm. And the third and most important thing is that we must be, quite frankly, truly extraordinary.
> Exceptional, stunning, whatever. We cannot make it to the semi-finals of this competition by being just another
> great competent team with a decent IPS and a good Final Report. We need to be something else entirely. We need to
> have a uniquely breathtaking quality. That will likely involve an unexpected level and layer of creativity. You
> know? Something which really does just set us apart from everyone else. Something which no one else would do or
> think to do. (Not even the other teams who may also be using AI.) Something to which a judge would think, 'aha'.
> Insights which make us seem like geniuses. You know? That sort of thing. Anyway, these are just some thoughts for
> you to keep in mind and ruminate upon. I don't imagine that you would have to do anything to act on these at this
> particular moment. But you must always have these in your back pocket and consider how to apply them and if you
> can do so then we will be well on our way to some level of success.

## The three, in one line each
1. **Voice.** Cerebral yet witty, passionate yet thoughtful, insightful yet confident; unlike any other team; work a
   judge enjoys reading. (How: `VOICE.md`, Ahaan's writing as the base; confident claims are never softened.)
2. **One motif.** A single, vast extended metaphor that nearly every corner of the strategy ties back to, in the IPS
   and the Final Report. It serves the investing; the investing never serves it.
3. **Extraordinary (the most important).** Not another competent team. An unexpected layer of creativity no other
   team, AI-using or not, would think of; insights that make a judge say "aha".

## How Keel applies them (Keel's working reading, 24 Oct; Caplet can change it)
- **Ask of every output: which star does this raise?** A memo, slip, test or episode that raises none of the three
  and no judging criterion is not the best use of the run.
- **The investing outranks the prose.** Ray's order is intellect first, language second. A metaphor earns a line
  only where it explains a real mechanism of Laura's plan part for part (the filter in `office/IDEATION.md`); an
  image that maps nothing is decoration, and last year's lesson was over-packaging (`../CLAUDE.md`, Team).
- **The motif must hold the locked lines.** The TNA is submitted and the IPS locks Sat 7 Nov 09:00 Sydney; the
  Final Report may not revise the strategy. A motif is only a candidate if it fits the words already locked
  (`library/ips_road_2026-10/00_SPINE.md`, section 1), so it is chosen before the IPS is written, not after.
- **"Aha" is a true insight, not a flourish.** It must survive Kai's "who says?" and Felix's "where does it say
  that?"; every fact sourced and dated, every number MODEL-labelled. A genius line that is false is the fastest way
  out of the competition.
- **Voice is shown, never claimed.** No sentence calls the team witty, bold or unique; the sentences are.
- **Fiction still stays fiction.** Keel's events never enter a Caplet deliverable; Caplet's students write the final
  words in their own voice and log the AI use.
