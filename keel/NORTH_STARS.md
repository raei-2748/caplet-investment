## The first three, in one line each (star 4 below; all four equal)
1. **Voice.** Cerebral yet witty, passionate yet thoughtful, insightful yet confident; unlike any other team; work a
   judge enjoys reading. (How: `VOICE.md`, Ahaan's writing as the base; confident claims are never softened.)
2. **One motif.** A single, vast extended metaphor that nearly every corner of the strategy ties back to, in the IPS
   and the Final Report. It serves the investing; the investing never serves it.
3. **Extraordinary.** Not another competent team. An unexpected layer of creativity no other
   team, AI-using or not, would think of; insights that make a judge say "aha".

## How Keel applies them (Keel's working reading, 24 Oct; Caplet can change it)
- **Ask of every output: which star does this raise?** A memo, slip, test or episode that raises none of the four
  and no judging criterion is not the best use of the run.
- **The investing outranks the prose.** Ray's order is intellect first, language second. A metaphor earns a line
  only where it explains a real mechanism of Laura's plan part for part (the filter in `office/IDEATION.md`); an
  image that maps nothing is decoration, and last year's lesson was over-packaging (`../CLAUDE.md`, Team).
- **The motif must hold the locked lines.** The TNA is submitted and the IPS locks Sat 7 Nov 09:00 Sydney; the
  Final Report may not revise the strategy. A motif is only a candidate if it fits the words already locked
  (`library/ips_road_2026-10/00_SPINE.md`, section 1), so it is chosen before the IPS is written, not after.
- **"Aha" is a true insight, not a flourish.** It must survive Kai's "who says?" and Felix's "where does it say
  that?"; every fact sourced and dated, every number MODEL-labelled. A genius line that is false is the fastest way
  out of the competition.
- **Voice is shown, never claimed.** No sentence calls the team witty, bold or unique; the sentences are.
- **Fiction still stays fiction.** Keel's events never enter a Caplet deliverable; Caplet's students write the final
  words in their own voice and log the AI use.

## Ray's refinements, 24 Oct 2026 (his words, shortened; the full message is in the project chat)
1. **The name.** "'Root and branch', whilst being our preliminary name for our strategy, needn't be our final one."
   Other AIs nearly always reached for roots first (from Laura's *Messy Roots*), so a roots name risks reading as AI
   groupthink. The name "must be deeply unique to our team and our strategy", tied to the motif, "whilst still being
   directly and clearly applicable to our investment strategy". Everyone optimises for the client: "a good strategy
   is to optimise for OURSELVES first and foremost." **Keel reads this as: nothing roots-, tree- or branch-themed in
   any suggestion from now on** (Long Room, 24 Oct evening, `meetings/2026-10-24_after-root-and-branch.md`).
2. **The motif is subtle.** "A tool that helps powerfully and distinctively convey our ideas, not a crutch that
   wastes word count." Used "where it's genuinely impactful", building the narrative arc; never in every sentence,
   never decorative or bolted on. AI uses extended metaphors "far more – and far more eloquently – than humans do",
   so heavy metaphor raises the AI suspicion it is meant to beat. Find it on "the road less taken": unexpected
   connections across fields.
3. **Sound deeply human.** Lean and lucid, but "real, messy human writing". When cutting, don't just rearrange words
   to be shorter ("exactly what AI has a frustrating tendency to do"); think about how humans cut. A banned-word list
   was not enough: the humanised TNA still read as AI and detectors scored it "100% AI-generated". Ray shared three
   of his own essays as voice inspiration (in the chat only; not copied into the repo).

So star 2 now reads: **one motif, used sparingly, that only Caplet could have, and a name that comes from it.**

## Ray's notes of 25 Oct 2026 (Caplet XR and de-obsessions; his words shortened, full message in the project chat)
1. **Caplet XR** is exceptionally clever: hyper-specific to the team, it justifies the team name, and its extensions
   (releases on schedule, "do not crush") work; find more dimensions. A second meaning of XR that sounds like a real
   investing product tier, used in the intro or conclusion, for flair. Not mandatory: a better name is welcome.
2. **Stop obsessing over overclaim words.** Humans overclaim a little; AI always hedges to the mildest word. Want flair
   and voice like Ahaan's essays (but don't actively overclaim).
3. **Judges are not mini-Lauras.** They are competition judges seeking the best investing knowledge and strategy; don't
   evaluate drafts by whether Laura would like them.
4. **No time on grammar, punctuation or spelling.** En dashes are correct in Australia. Focus on substance.
