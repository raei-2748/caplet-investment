## Why it matters
That is a bond ladder, built by a drug company. Caplet XR is the same capsule with ten bead populations: each
payment's Treasury is a bead, its maturity is the coating, and the dates are fixed by what we buy, not by markets.

Felix's three breaks are the useful part, because two of them are investing lessons:
1. **Wax is sloppy, Treasuries aren't.** The wax was later replaced by "more stable and reproducible" polymers. A
   Treasury pays an exact sum on an exact date. Ours is the reproducible coating.
2. **Thick coats cost less.** Same $50,000 dose, but about $37k buys the 2033 bead and about $23k the 2042 one
   (MODEL, 25 Sep 2026 par curve, zero-coupon basis, rough). Discounting in one image.
3. **Our beads leak.** Coupons arrive before a bond "dissolves". The leak is the plan's one real risk (reinvesting
   coupons at lower yields, ~$21k shortfall, MODEL, 15 Oct), and Lab R01's dedicated ladder is just *deciding where
   the leak goes*: later beads' coupons pay earlier doses.
