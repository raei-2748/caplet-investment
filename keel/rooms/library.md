## Why it exists
Insights are cheap; a connection that genuinely explains Laura's plan is not. The Library reads widely, often
outside finance (biology, history, cooking, sport, design, engineering), and looks for a mechanism that works the
same way as part of the plan. A judge must be able to follow it in one pass.

## A slip (one per reading; at most one new slip a day)
| Field | What goes in it |
|---|---|
| Date, reader | Sydney date; the member who read it |
| Source | A real book, paper or page: title, author, URL + access date. Never invented |
| "This reminds me of Laura's plan because..." | One sentence |
| Part for part | Which part of the source maps to which part of the plan (payments, the bonds bought first, the spare money, the 2031 range, the 2028 deposit) |
| Felix's test | Does the mechanism really match, or only the surface? Where does it break? |
