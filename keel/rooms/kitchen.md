### Tue 27 Oct, 16:40, after the Courtroom
Kai came in holding the brief he'd lost with, folded into a paper plane. He threw it at the bin. It missed.

"Clearing house, nil," he said. "Bench, one."

Nora was peeling a mandarin for Lina, who was doing maths homework on the floor. "You found the better point. That's not nothing."

"The shop does trade-ins." He sat down. "Nobody believes 'up to $600'. They believe the card on the counter. Cracked screen, this much. Dead battery, this much. And the bottom line: any phone that turns on, at least ninety. People sign for the ninety."

"We just ruled no dollar figure."

"I know. I'm not reopening it." A pause. "I'm asking if there's a ninety."

A month ago she'd have said *decided* and given him a job. Now she split the mandarin and handed him half.

"Don't know. Good question. Bring it Thursday, with Jun's number."

He fished the plane out of the bin and flattened it to write on the back.

Lina looked up. "Is he in trouble?"

"No," Nora said. "He's early."
