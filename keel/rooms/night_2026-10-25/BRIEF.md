## Ray's standing rules (read `../../NORTH_STARS.md` in full if you write prose)
1. **Three north stars:** a distinctive voice (cerebral yet witty, passionate yet thoughtful, insightful yet
   confident) a judge enjoys reading; one subtle motif; above all, be extraordinary: an "aha" no other team, even an
   AI-using one, would reach. Intellect about investing outranks literary skill.
2. **24 Oct refinements:** nothing roots-, tree- or branch-themed. The name must be ours, tied to the motif, and
   clearly fit the strategy. Optimise for ourselves first. The motif is subtle: a tool, never a crutch, never in
   every sentence. Heavy eloquent metaphor reads as AI. Writing must sound human and lean; cut whole repeated ideas,
   don't squeeze sentences shorter (that is an AI habit). The humanised TNA still scored "100% AI" on detectors.
3. **Ray's essay voice** (`../../VOICE.md`, "Ray's own essay voice"): a long sentence then a blunt short one;
   concede, then turn on "But"; beat an objection with a distinction; define once, plainly; one hedge at most;
   spaced en dashes for asides; British spelling. Ahaan's base (same file) for the IPS form.
4. **Scalpel rule:** every idea in the IPS ticks a judging criterion (Investment Strategy, Client Knowledge,
   Portfolio Analysis, Creativity & Presentation; Articulation is the Final Report's). Insights are cheap.
5. **Ideation ladder:** Haiku 5.5 wide, Sonnet 5.5 deeper only if nothing kept, Opus 5.5 far only if still
   nothing. Record which rung produced each kept idea. An analogy must map the real mechanism.
6. **No softening.** Confident true claims stay as strong as written; flag only hard issues (limits, rules, facts,
   numbers, format).
7. **Binned, never re-propose:** roots, trees, branches, seeds, gardens; keel and sail, ballast and sail; ladders or
   rungs as a name; floor and fund; secure first, grow later; comic panels, ink, storyboards; NSW water licences;
   Firm Yield, General Security, Dry-Year Rules, Opening Allocation; "defeasance" as an IPS word. Also no name with
   a certainty word (secure, sure, safe, guaranteed, certain), nothing that sounds like a cap or collar.
8. **Hard limits:** TIPS and STRIPS are banned in the IPS; no overclaim words (guaranteed, risk-free, promised,
   cannot miss, never below, immunization, CPPI); no charts, citations or footnotes in the IPS; every number dated
   and labelled MODEL or HISTORY; facts from the official case (`competition/official/2026_27/`) or a cited source.
   Never edit the IPS Report Google Doc. Never use WInS or SMApply. Keel's events are fiction and never enter a
   Caplet deliverable as Caplet's experience. Simulated people (Laura, co-sponsors, judges) are tests, never quotes.
9. **Drafts** are labelled `AI DRAFT (Claude, 25 Oct 2026)`; Caplet rewrites in its own words and logs AI use.
