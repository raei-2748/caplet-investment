## What it shows
1. **All three of Monday's names stay unclaimed.** In 144 names over two rounds, no model reached a pill, a
   pre-registration or a press day. That is the one thing the test can say with some confidence.
2. **But the manuscript variant is not ours.** Sonnet's fifth team said, unprompted, that Laura promises co-sponsors
   "only the pages already inked plus half of whatever is still in pencil". That is Amara's "the fund is the
   manuscript; we never quote a page that isn't printed" (Long Room vote 2b) almost exactly, and Opus spent a whole
   draw on ink-before-colour. Laura's trade is the *first* place a machine looks once it reads "graphic novelist".
   So the print run survives only as **press day** (the day the price is set), never as ink, pages or drafts.
3. **Root and Branch and Keel and Sail came back again**, unprompted, in a second round. Ray's call on roots was
   right twice over, and Keel's own name must never be the team's image in a Caplet suggestion.
4. **Rules are a cold theme, but only as labels** ("Gift by Rule"). The *reason* a rule written in advance matters
   (pre-registration) appeared nowhere. Pre-Registered's edge is the reason, not the word "rule".
