## 4. The practitioners' two questions, answered
- **Ward: "what if you're worried between doors?"** Alarms are for what you watch; Laura's cheques are owned, not
  watched. A ward scores a proxy for a patient's state; the ladder *is* the cheques. The one thing still watched (the
  coupon gap) is settled at a door. Her own half, sellable any day, is where worry may act.
- **Pension trustee: "why no funding-level trigger?"** A de-risking trigger protects a live liability funded by risky
  assets. Here each liability is matched the day it exists (cheques 2027, the gift 2031), so before naming there is no
  liability for a trigger to protect, and after it none left exposed. A trigger before 2031 would be market timing on a
  number nobody has heard. **"You only need an alarm for what you don't own."** (Reasoned from the logic of liability
  matching; no pension document quoted. The Pensions Regulator's page refused this environment's proxy, 29 Oct.)
