doses. Households that traded most earned 11.4% a year against the market's 17.9% (Barber & Odean, *J Finance* 55(2),
2000; abstract via ideas.repec.org, 25 Oct 2026). Caplet XR is two doses (2027, 2028), then a hold rule.
