## Part B: three investing insights

### B1. In finance terms the plan is a *defeasance*, and that gives "high degree of certainty" a standard to meet
- **What it is (CHECK):** in a CMBS loan defeasance, the borrower replaces the property collateral with a portfolio of
  US Treasuries whose payments match the remaining loan payments. From 1983 to 1996, US accounting
  (FASB Statement 76) even let companies treat debt as paid off once matching risk-free securities were placed in an
  irrevocable trust. Statement 125 later withdrew that, because the debt still legally exists.
- **Why it's an aha:** "high degree of certainty" then has a real benchmark. In January 2027 the plan defeases
  Laura's commitment six years before the first payment. It also names the one exact place where it falls short of a
  textbook defeasance: those trusts needed cash flows that coincide with the payments, while ours arrive early as
  coupons and must be reinvested. That gap is the whole ~$21k residual. The locked line ("near-ironclad... we provide
  exactly that") gets a precise definition plus one named leak, which beats a vague promise.
- **Draft line (24 words):** "We define high certainty as defeasance: in January 2027 each payment is matched by
  Treasuries; the only leak is reinvesting coupons that arrive early."

### B2. The 2031 range is not a forecast. The rules make it true, so the confidence figure has to say something else
- Gift = min(floor + half the fund, top), with top = floor + half the fund's 2031 value. If the fund rises after 2031,
  the cap binds. If it falls, the gift falls, but never below the floor, because the backups never spend the floor.
  So the gift lands inside the range by construction (~99% in the number sheet; the 1% is the IBTM NAV wobble). This
  corrects Rung 1A #34, which said the gift "can land outside it": it can't go above the top, and only NAV wobble
  can take it below the bottom.
- **The aha for a statistics graduate:** the range is the gift's *support* (the full set of values it can take), not
  a confidence interval. "99% inside" is true but tells co-sponsors nothing. The honest confidence figure is about
  *where inside* it lands: about 3 in 4 to reach the top (range_2031 README, model). Quote that instead. That is
  how you avoid overpromising while still saying something.
- **Draft line (23 words):** "Our rules keep the gift inside the range, so the confidence we state is the real
  question: about three in four to reach the top."

### B3. Laura's half insures two risks that rarely come in the same decade. Her own income is the third risk, and it moves with the first
