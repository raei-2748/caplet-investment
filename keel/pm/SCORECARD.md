# Scorecard (one row a week; newest at the top)

> Written by the Courtroom on its last run before Sunday 17:00 (the weekly plan writes it if the Courtroom hasn't).
> The two lowest scores are next week's targets for every room (`SELF_IMPROVEMENT.md` section 1).

**Rubric:** 1-7 each, scored on the newest draft as a judge would read it, with one line of evidence per score.
Mock-judge scores count only on a draft that two students and the outside reader have scored first (CHARTER
section 6); otherwise Keel's own panel scores it and the row says "Keel panel".
- **Wharton criteria:** IS = Investment Strategy · CK = Client Knowledge · PA = Portfolio Analysis · AR = Articulation of Competition Experience · CP = Creativity & Presentation.
- **North stars (`../NORTH_STARS.md`):** N1 = a distinctive team voice · N2 = a motif that serves the investing · N3 = truly extraordinary.
