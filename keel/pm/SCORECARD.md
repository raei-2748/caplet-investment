# Scorecard (one row a week; newest at the top)

> Written by the Courtroom on its last run before Sunday 17:00 (the weekly plan writes it if the Courtroom hasn't).
> The two lowest scores are next week's targets for every room (`SELF_IMPROVEMENT.md` section 1).

**Rubric:** 1-7 each, scored on the newest draft as a judge would read it, with one line of evidence per score.
Mock-judge scores count only on a draft that two students and the outside reader have scored first (CHARTER
section 6); otherwise Keel's own panel scores it and the row says "Keel panel".
- **Wharton criteria (BIBLE Book 8, word for word; score each as a whole, and the evidence line names the weakest part by code from `../library/ips_road_2026-10/03_CRITERION_MAP.md`):**
  - **IS = Investment Strategy:** "Presents a clear and creative investment thesis; demonstrates disciplined planning across Laura's changing time horizons and cash-flow needs; uses appropriate diversification; and maintains consistency with the team's IPS."
  - **CK = Client Knowledge and Objectives:** "Tailors the strategy to Laura's circumstances, priorities, risk considerations, and residency goals; demonstrates a thoughtful understanding of the client; and presents recommendations that can earn her confidence."
  - **PA = Portfolio Analysis:** "Demonstrates understanding and effective use of investment concepts and tools; integrates quantitative and qualitative analysis; and uses reasonable assumptions and projections to evaluate funding reliability, the facility contribution, and financial flexibility under varying market outcomes."
  - **AR = Articulation of Competition Experience:** "Demonstrates teamwork, communication, and learning; clearly explains the team's research and decision-making process; and reflects meaningfully on the team's growth and response to challenges."
  - **CP = Creativity and Presentation:** "Presents a compelling, well-organized narrative in an authentic team voice; uses data effectively to support conclusions; demonstrates original thinking and meaningful reflection; and communicates Laura's potential facility contribution and investment uncertainty clearly and credibly to prospective co-sponsors."
  - On an IPS draft, PA-c, AR-a, AR-c and CP-d belong to the Final Report (Book 7); score what the IPS can carry and say so.
- **North stars (`../NORTH_STARS.md`, all four equal):** N1 = a distinctive team voice · N2 = a motif that serves the investing · N3 = truly extraordinary · N4 = extraordinary investing knowledge, applied.
- Score as a human competition judge would (Ray, 25 Oct: not as Laura would).
