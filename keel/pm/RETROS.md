# Retrospectives (one after each deliverable)

30 minutes, the whole team, the day after a deadline. Keel (Tess) fills "Facts" beforehand from the board, LEDGER
and git history; the team fills the rest in its own words. The output is **one process change**, which becomes a
board card. These are real team events, so they may later support the Final Report's "competition experience"
section; Keel never writes the team's opinions here.

## Template
- **Facts** (Keel): planned vs actual dates for each milestone; cards done late and why; office memos used.
- **Keep** (team): what worked.
- **Stop** (team): what cost time or quality.
- **Start** (team): what to try next time.
- **The one change** (team): card ID created on BOARD.md.
