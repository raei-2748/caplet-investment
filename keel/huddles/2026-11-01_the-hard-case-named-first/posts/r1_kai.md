  **The move:** Promise a use, not an outcome (a bid in this range has a chance), say whose opinion it is, then state the limit in the next sentence as plainly as the promise.

  **The move:** This is the sceptic's side, not a seller's move: he sets the report's mild words beside the real damage and says exactly where it was.
