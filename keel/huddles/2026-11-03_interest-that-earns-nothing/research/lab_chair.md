## E1. Public escrows are sized "without reinvestment"
- **Source:** Massachusetts Water Resources Authority and U.S. Bank Trust Company, N.A., *2025-2 Defeasance Escrow
  Agreement*, dated 22 Dec 2025, https://www.mwra.com/media/file/2025-2-defeasance-escrow-agreement, read 3 Nov 2026.
- **Quote (s3):** the escrowed obligations "mature and bear interest payable in such amounts and at such times as will
  be sufficient, without reinvestment," [to pay the defeased bonds]. **s6:** "so that sufficient moneys will be
  available to pay when due the principal of and interest on the Defeased Bonds". **s5:** receipts not needed at once
  "shall be reinvested in Defeasance Obligations at a yield not to exceed the yield on the Defeased Bonds".
- **What it means for us:** when a public borrower wants its old bonds treated as already paid, the money set aside in
  Treasuries is sized so it pays every coupon and principal on time **even if nothing it receives is reinvested**.
  That is Kai's lock (idea bank #147), as a real-world rule, not our invention. Reinvesting is allowed; counting on it
  is not.
- **Context:** NABL (National Association of Bond Lawyers), *Verification Agent* (last modified 29 Mar 2023),
  https://www.nabl.org/bond-basics/verification-agent/, read 3 Nov 2026: the verification agent is "responsible for
  independently confirming that the investments purchased for the Escrow Fund will be sufficient" "to fund the Debt
  Service payments on the Refunded Bonds, up to and including the date the Refunded Bonds are redeemed." The page says
  nothing about the reinvestment assumption; the escrow agreement does.
- **Breaks:** one agreement, one issuer. "Without reinvestment" is common drafting in US refunding escrows, but Keel
  has read only this one today: say "a public escrow we read", not "every escrow", until more are read.
- **Where it lands:** Final Report (the reason, cited). Not the IPS: no citations, and "defeasance" is binned as an IPS
  word (lab book). The IPS takes the rule, uncited.
