# Questions for the first-time reader

You have one page: a README that comes with a team's draft investment proposal (the IPS). Answer from that page alone,
in your own words. If the page doesn't tell you, write "the page doesn't say". For questions 1 to 3, give the file's
name, or describe it if you can't remember the name.

1. You want to cut a whole sentence from the IPS to save words. Apart from this page and the IPS itself, which file
   would you open first?

2. You keep a sentence but put it in your own words: you swap some of its words, or say one of its facts a different
   way. Apart from this page and the IPS itself, which file would you open first?

3. You are deciding whether to keep the IPS's last sentence as it is, and whether to make a small cut someone has
   suggested. Apart from this page and the IPS itself, which file would you open first?

4. In your own words: what does Laura's first $300,000 buy, and what is it for?

5. What does the plan do with Laura's 2028 money? Does any of her money end up in shares?

6. What does the page say the team has to do themselves before the IPS goes in? List everything you remember.
