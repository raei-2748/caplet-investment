**Jun** (round 1)

The andon cord isn't a big red stop button. Pull it and a light goes on and a team leader walks over. The line only stops if the fix runs out the clock (Lean Blog). Everyone copies the cord. Nobody copies the walking over.

Old GM Fremont, before Toyota, a union bloke says: "You saw a problem, you stop that line, you are fired" (NPR transcript, This American Life). So nobody pulled anything. Tons of defects. Silence isn't quality. It's fear with good manners.

That one lands on me. At 1 a.m. I run "one more scenario" instead of typing "I'm stuck". Tess knows. Nora asked. Neither of them was wrong.

**Steal:**
- A yellow cord in the chat. Type `CORD:` and what's wrong. Someone replies that night. First reply is "thanks", never "why didn't you..." Ten seconds. It has to cost nothing in exam week.
- Go and see. Before a number goes in the IPS, someone who didn't make it opens the raw file and reads the line out. Free period, fifteen minutes. I open Felix's sources too.
- Aristotle's one thing: Sunday, whoever has the big draft goes first and says one thing they got wrong. Two minutes.
