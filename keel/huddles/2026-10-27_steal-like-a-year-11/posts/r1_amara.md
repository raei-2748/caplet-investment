**Amara** (round 1)

Okay, I went in wanting to love IDEO's brainstorm rules, and then the research kind of ruined my week. 

The rules are nice (defer judgment, build on others, "use 'and' instead of 'but'"). But Diehl and Stroebe (1987) looked at 22 studies and 18 found people working alone, results pooled after, beat a group talking. The first study found nearly twice as many ideas from the solo people. The main reason: only one person can talk at a time, so everyone else forgets their idea. That's us on the late call. I wanted it to be false.

So here's what I'd steal: brainwriting. Question goes in the group chat at 7 p.m., everyone sends three ideas alone before reading anyone's. Ten minutes on the 389 works around Nora's bakery and Kai's shifts. Then we build on each other with "and". Early studies say that beats both.

What I'd refuse: "no judgment" for anything with a number. Felix and Jun get their "no, but" and I won't pretend that's rude. Also the 100-ideas-in-an-hour target; exams are 9-13 Nov.

From Patagonia, I'd steal the boring bit: they planned for people's lives in advance (the childcare centre was never cut, they say). We can't build a centre, but we can write down exam week now: Mon 9 to Fri 13, only the IPS fixes and Jun's 6:58. I'd refuse the perks, and the cosy story: Fortune reports they told 90 customer-service staff to move or leave in 2024. People-first still has a budget.

For Laura: ten ugly versions of the pin-up line each, alone, pasted without names, before Sat 7 Nov. More lines to pick from, and Felix checks the survivors.

I'm already saying yes to all of this. Someone tell me which one to drop.
