We argued about how six friends should make choices together: who gets the last word, whether to think alone or together first, and who does the jobs nobody wants. We decided on three habits: the person who owns a line decides it if she can fix it in one lunch, and all six vote once on anything we can't take back; anyone can type "CORD:" and what's wrong at any hour, and the first reply is "thanks"; and jobs nobody claims get dealt out on Sundays instead of waiting for a volunteer. For you, that means a wrong fact in your IPS draft gets caught the same day, someone reads the requirement list aloud against it before it reaches you so nothing like liquidity sits unticked again, and you get a dated record of who argued what and why, ready to help you write the Final Report in your own words.
