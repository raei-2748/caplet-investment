## The question
**Which habits from real companies, offices and startups should Keel steal, and what do they look like for six Year 11s
with school, jobs, sport and families?**

Why it matters (Ray, 27 Oct): Keel exists so Caplet's voice and experience come out more nuanced, which serves the
Final Report most of all. Ray's complaint: Keel's culture reads like a firm of investment professionals ("Decision,
owner, date", pre-mortems, risk registers), and the six don't really talk to each other: ideas come from one-shot
generation, not from bouncing around until something valuable compounds. Fix both.

## How to post (every round)
- **Write as yourself, in your own voice** (`VOICE.md` base + your variation; `CAST.md` "Says"/"Never says"). You are a
  Sydney teenager talking to friends, not a consultant writing a deck. Short paragraphs, real opinions, jokes allowed.
  No corporate words you wouldn't say at lunch ("leverage", "synergy", "stakeholders", "cadence", "alignment").
- **Real research only.** Name what you read with the URL. Quote exactly or paraphrase plainly. If you couldn't verify
  it, say UNVERIFIED. Include the criticism too: what went wrong when the company did it.
- **Translate, don't transplant.** For anything you want to steal, say exactly what it looks like for us: when (lunch,
  the bus, Sunday night), where, who, how long, and what it costs someone with a shift or a recital that week. Say
  what you refuse to steal and why.
- **Tie it to the work.** One line: how would this habit make Keel's IPS help to Caplet better before Sat 7 Nov, or
  give Caplet a more honest Final Report (Articulation of the competition experience)?
- Rounds 2-4: answer people by name. Steal from someone, attack someone, build on someone ("yes, and"). Change your
  mind if you've been convinced, and say what did it.

## Rounds
1. **Read and pitch** (all six, in parallel): research note in `research/<name>.md`; post (≤ 300 words) in `posts/r1_<name>.md`.
2. **Reply** (all six read every round-1 post): ≤ 220 words each, `posts/r2_<name>.md`.
3. **Settle the fights** (Nora names the 2-3 real disagreements; the members on each side go and check): new
   research notes + posts `posts/r3_<name>.md`.
4. **Vote and close**: each member votes on the shortlist; Nora reads out what passed; Tess writes the essence.
   Results go into `CULTURE.md`, `CAST.md`, `STORY_BIBLE.md` and the runbooks the same day.
