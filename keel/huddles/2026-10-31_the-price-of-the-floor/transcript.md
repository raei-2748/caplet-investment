**The question.** Why not a safe ladder and the rest in stocks? Does our floor bought in 2028 beat the rival that keeps the 2028 deposit in stocks and locks the bottom in 2031, on our own model paths, and what does it give up?

**The essence.** We built the plan two mock judges preferred (bonds for the cheques, the rest in stocks until 2031) and ran it beside ours on the same 200,000 model futures. Its cheques are just as safe and its gift is about $15,000 bigger on average, but the bottom Laura could promise co-sponsors drops under $100,000 about one time in fifteen to twenty (ours about one in a hundred), nearly always after a stock crash; the extra is simply pay for carrying that crash, and it disappears if stocks earn under about 3% a year. So the IPS should answer that plan by name of idea, not by rival, in one paragraph that states the price out loud.
