## The founding (August 2026), the story so far
Nora built Keel in the first two weeks of August, one person at a time, each with a pitch written for them:
- **Tess** (first, 3 Aug): "You told me I steamroll quiet people. Come and make sure I don't."
- **Amara** (4 Aug): "Be the person who remembers there's a human being at the end of the spreadsheet."
- **Felix** (6 Aug): "You found the mistake in the debating rules. This competition has a rulebook too."
- **Kai** (10 Aug): "You beat my team with one question. I want that question on my side."
- **Jun** (11 Aug, through Kai): "Finance is just maths with consequences." (Kai's line; Nora let him use it.)

The name came from Amara on 14 Aug: a keel keeps a boat upright under sail. The client case arrived later in the
month; by mid-September the team had a first plan ("lock early"), an argument about how much growth Laura needed,
and its first big mistake (Amara's interview quote, see her file). On Sun 27 Sep, the night before trading opened,
the six spent a three-day stretch in the Long Room writing `CULTURE.md`. The prequel (`episodes/2026-09-27_ep00.md`)
tells that night.

## The arcs
### 1. The sail (Kai and Jun; the whole team)
How much of Laura's money should be at risk, and what does "bold" mean in a plan built on safety?
- **Beats so far:** Kai wants 60-80% of the growth money in stocks (Ep 3); Jun's first bad-year table (Ep 9); the
  VT trade and the 2.1% line Tess cut (Ep 25); Kai's five attacks, ending "more equity is not the leap" (Ep 26).
- **Coming:** the sail vote (Sun 25 Oct), the final number (Sun 1 Nov). Kai learns that the bold idea is the honesty
  rule, and that his job is to make it true for the bonds Keel owns.
- **Where it stands (26 Oct):** the buffer was a lower quote with a sticker; Keel votes floor last + a quoted margin 6-0; the slack question goes to Laura's half (Ep 30).

### 2. Two captains (Nora and Kai)
Nora decides fast; Kai thinks she closes questions before they are ready. Both are partly right.
- **Beats so far:** the Year 9 debate (backstory); Kai wins the IEF/TLT argument after Nora had closed it (Ep 3);
  Nora overrules Kai on trading in the first week (Ep 6) and is proved right; Kai reopens the IBTR question after
  the vote (Ep 10) and is proved right; the "argue hard, decide once" truce (Ep 11).
- **Coming:** the IPS freeze, where a question Nora wants closed (the 2031 range wording) is one Kai is right to
  keep open. Nora learns to hold a question open on purpose; Kai learns to let a logged decision stand.
- **Where it stands (26 Oct):** Kai reopened Sunday's vote by the truce's rule: evidence, at a meeting, numbers without a speech (Ep 30).

### 3. The folder (Amara and Tess)
Tess cuts Amara's best lines because they are not yet true. Amara wants them back.
- **Beats so far:** the interview quote (backstory); the first cut lines (Ep 7); the inflation paragraph that
  replaced TIPS (Ep 25); the 2031 range line, cut by Amara herself (Ep 26).
- **Coming:** in December, the co-sponsor text. A line from the folder comes back with Jun's model behind it.
- **Where it stands (23 Oct):** eleven lines in the folder; Amara has asked for two of them back "when they are true".

### 4. The overstay (Jun, Tess, the Long Room)
Jun has been working alone in the Long Room after the others sleep, which breaks the house rule.
- **Beats so far:** Jun alone in the room after a price check (Ep 4) and on sizing night (Ep 9); his numbers arrive
  "too fast" and Amara sees him at 4 a.m. (Ep 12); Tess finds the file times in the log and says nothing (Ep 16);
  Tess tells Jun privately (Ep 19); Nora finds him at 1 a.m. and asks "something you need, or something you're
  afraid of?" (Ep 24).
- **Coming:** Jun answers Nora's question in front of the team (it is about Hana, and about choosing his own path);
  the house rule changes so that nobody needs to work alone (a night shift of two).
- **Where it stands (25 Oct):** Jun answered Nora: "afraid", of a wrong number in public; asked Kai to run Monday's numbers with him (Ep 29).

### 5. Two clocks (Felix)
Felix is right and late. The Final Report instructions arrive at 01:00 Sydney on Tue 10 Nov with little time.
- **Beats so far:** the ticker that wasn't (Ep 2); the daylight-saving switch (Ep 7); the example is not a rule

  (Ep 26); three of twelve ranked in a minute, and a reading called a reading (Ep 28).
- **Coming:** the Final Report instructions night: he has to say "these three things matter, the rest can wait"
  within an hour.
- **Where it stands (24 Oct):** ranked three in about a minute when Nora asked (Ep 28); the instructions night is still ahead.

### 6. Say it the day you see it (Tess)

- **Beats so far:** the poster and the group report (backstory); the log she sat on (Ep 16) and the overclaim she sat

- **Coming:** saying something to Nora, about Nora, on the day she sees it (the IPS freeze will give her the chance).
- **Where it stands (23 Oct):** keeping it in meetings and, once, with a friend; not yet with the leader.

### 7. Outside (all six)
The Long Room gives them more time than their lives have room for. Families and friends notice.
- **Beats so far:** Lina asks Nora why she seems "older after Keel nights" (Ep 13); Felix misses band (Ep 15); Amara
  forgets her tutoring kids (Ep 17) and is early the next week (Ep 23); Kai's Bà asks what is wrong with his eyes

- **Coming:** someone has to choose between Keel and something outside it before the IPS; the team learns to protect
  each other's outside lives as a rule, not a favour.
- **Where it stands (24 Oct):** Tess said it in the Kitchen; Jun said "yeah" first; home at seven (Ep 27).

### 8. The Window (Keel and Caplet)
Keel helps six students at another school it has never met, without deciding anything for them.
- **Beats so far:** the Window first carries Caplet's question (Sun 18 Oct, Ep 21) and Keel writes its rules for
  helping; Kai makes the strongest case against Caplet's IPS draft without deciding anything (Ep 22); Jun answers a
  question with sources and no recommendation (Ep 24).
- **Coming:** a Caplet choice that Keel thinks is wrong. Keel must say so clearly, give the evidence, and then let
  Caplet decide.
- **Where it stands (23 Oct):** five office answers sent; Keel is learning what "proposes, never decides" costs.
