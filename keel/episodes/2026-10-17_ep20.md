# FICTION. Episode 20: The door

**Saturday 17 October 2026, Sydney · Point of view: Nora · Long Room: 4 Arden days (the note; the reserve) · 7 days to the TNA**

> **Summary.** Tess tells the whole team about the "guarantees" note and the two days she sat on it, and proposes a

> note that can't be edited is an added note that corrects it, placed under the button rule. Jun rejects a glide
> path for the 2033-42 reserve: the ladder already is one. Nora notices she let Tess lead a meeting and liked it.
> **Arcs:** 6, 2, 4. **Values:** 4 (added), 5. **Ideas:** I-15 rejected.

---

The bakery at six. A wedding order: four hundred pastries, all of them wanted by eight. I didn't think about Keel
once, which is the first Saturday that's happened since August, and I think it's because Tess had messaged at 5:40:

**Tess:** I need the first ten minutes today. I have something I should have said on Thursday.

---

### The Long Room, Arden day one

Tess stood up. Tess never stands up.

**Tess:** On Thursday, filing the screenshots, I read the Nov-2040 note properly. It says the bond "guarantees
Laura's 2041 payment". We all heard it on the 7th and nobody blinked, including me. It's not quite true: a Treasury
is paid in full unless the U.S. government defaults. Jun never says "guaranteed". It's on the wall. And notes can't
be edited.

**Kai:** It's not a TNA pick.

**Tess:** No. But our whole plan rests on saying "certainty" carefully, and our own note says "guarantees". I saw it
on Thursday at 14:10 room time. I said nothing for two days. Not because it didn't matter. Because it was Amara's
sentence and she was having a bad week, and I'd rather be silent than clumsy. That's not a good reason. It's my
oldest bad habit.

Amara, who already knew, said, "She told me last night. I'd rather have known on Thursday."

Nobody said anything for a moment. Then I did the thing I've been learning to do, which is not speak first.

**Me:** Tess. What do you want to happen?

**Tess:** Two things. A fix for the note. And a value.

---

### The fix

**Felix:** Notes can be added, not changed. WInS lets us add a note to a trade from order history. So we add a
second note to the Nov-2040 trade that corrects the first. Plainly.

**Amara:** Can I write it? It's my sentence.

She wrote it in four minutes:

> *"Correction to our note above: this Treasury pays Laura's 2041 payment in full unless the U.S. government
> defaults. 'Guarantees' was the wrong word."*

**Tess:** Strict count fine. 147 characters.

**Kai:** Adding a note is still touching WInS. Button rule. I'll place it Monday night, read aloud, Felix watching.

**Me:** Good. Decision, owner, date: Amara wrote it; Kai places it Monday night; Felix watches. And all eleven notes get
read again, once, by someone who didn't write them, looking only for overclaim words. Felix.

---

### The value

Then Tess took a marker from her pocket, which means she'd planned it, and walked to the door.

**Tess:** We have four values. I want to add one between "argue hard" and Jun's. "Say it the day you see it." A
problem raised late costs more than a problem raised clumsily. I'm proposing it because I'm the one who most needs it.

**Kai:** Seconded. Loudly.

**Jun:** Seconded.

He said it fast. He looked at Tess and then at me, and I knew there was something else in it that I didn't know yet.
I let it go. That's not like me. I think I'm learning.

Six to zero. Tess wrote it on the door in her own handwriting, smaller and neater than mine. It became number four.
Jun's became five. She added, underneath, "Added 17 Oct, after Tess sat on a problem for two days." I asked if she
wanted that part. She said, "The journal doesn't lie. The door shouldn't either."

---

### Arden days three and four: the reserve

The rest of the stretch was Jun's. The case says the reserve is set aside at the start of 2033, and the team has to
say how its asset composition "should change" as payments are made.

**Felix:** Target-date funds have a glide path: less in stocks, more in bonds, as the date gets closer. Is that what
they mean?

**Jun:** For the reserve? No. The reserve is the ladder. In 2033 it holds the nine payments still to come. Every year
one rung matures and pays one payment. Nothing is sold. The composition changes by itself, because the shortest rung
disappears each year. It already *is* a glide path. It just glides by maturing, not by selling.

**Kai:** So we reject the glide path because we already have one.

**Jun:** We reject *selling* to glide. We describe ours in one sentence.

**Tess:** "One rung is paid each year; nothing is sold."

**Me:** That's the sentence a judge remembers. Tess, keep that.

She looked at me oddly. Then she smiled. It's my line for praising other people. I'd never said it to her.

Jun's number had come in at the end: **$294,770** for Friday's close, about **18bp**. Kai said it was like watching a
tide come in. Amara said, "It only matters if we're still standing there in January." Felix said, "Laura is."
