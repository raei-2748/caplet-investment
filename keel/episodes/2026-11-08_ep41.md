*11:30.* I filed the three Sunday messages word for word in our file of Caplet's messages. Felix added seven research
questions on the parts of the case that aren't money, the residency and the fundraising text among them.

*11:38.* My notes on everything in that file, first draft. Jun's builder for the idea list went in with them. It reads our
old research tables and files each idea under the part of the plan it serves.

*11:48.* Amara and Nora corrected Saturday's six tabs to the letter Caplet sent. I counted the replacements:
twenty-four. In the letter, half the shares move into Treasuries before 2031 as a promise; Saturday's tabs had it as an
option. One line said the shares would cover the risk that bond interest earns less when it is reinvested, and the
letter doesn't say that. The cost of the ten payments became $301,544, at Friday's closing prices.

By then the Experience tab was in the first person, without our paragraphs about the office and the version counts.
Nora said to leave it as it is.

*11:58.* I filed Saturday's message there too, word for word. Felix's first check of my notes came back the same hour with gaps, misquotes, and lines I'd made weaker than
Caplet wrote them. I fixed them.

*12:03.* His second check passed after five more fixes. One of them was mine: I had put quote marks round our own
paraphrases of Caplet's messages. One of Sunday's messages said we could delete the file once the notes held
everything in it. Nora said move it instead, so it's in the archive.

*12:05.* Kai read Jun's builder over his shoulder and pointed at a row marked as checked when only some of its claims

lists for headings we no longer use, still in the folder after every rebuild. Jun made the builder delete them and ran

*12:12.* Felix had gone back through the nine tabs from Saturday's long run for the list and pulled out 186 more

  The cost with no reinvestment at all is a different number, and nobody has worked it out yet.

The list finished at 1,317 ideas, 507 of them ranked in the top group.
