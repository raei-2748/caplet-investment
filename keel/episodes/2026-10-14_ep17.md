Mrs Lim from the library called my mum at 4:20 on Tuesday. Priya and Leo had been waiting at the reading table
since 3:30, with their books open at the page we'd stopped on last week, and I wasn't there.

I was in the Long Room. Four room days on the reflections. Outside it was eleven minutes, and those eleven minutes
were the ones I was supposed to be at the library.

I've tutored them every Tuesday since February. I've never forgotten. When I got home, Mum didn't shout. She just
said, "They thought you weren't coming back." That was worse.

I went to the library this morning before school and left them each a book and a note. I wrote the note eleven
times. Tess would have cut it to one line. I didn't let her see it.

**Jun:** So the projections start from $300k in January 2027 and $150k in January 2028, whatever WInS does.

### Room day two: the 2028 money

Kai had a new idea, and for once it wasn't from a paper. It was from his mum.

**Kai:** In 2028, Laura gets the $150,000. Say most of the growth part goes into VT. If she puts it all in on 2
January and the market drops 20% in February, that's a terrible start. My mum does this with her commission: she
puts it in a bit at a time. Dollar-cost averaging. A twelfth a month for a year.

**Jun:** On average, putting it in at once does better, because markets rise more often than they fall, and money
that's invested earlier has longer to grow.

**Kai:** On average.

**Me:** Can I argue for Kai?

Everyone looked at me. I don't usually argue for Kai.

**Me:** It's not about the average. It's about regret. If Laura puts it all in and it drops, she'll feel she made a
mistake, even if it's fine by 2033. She's raising money for a building. In 2028 she's talking to co-sponsors. If
her growth money has just fallen a fifth, that's what she'll be thinking about in every meeting. Slicing it in
costs a bit on average and buys her a calmer year.

**Kai:** I didn't think of that. I was just thinking about my mum.

**Me:** You were thinking about a person. That's the same thing.

**Felix:** It adds a rule to the IPS. Twelve dates, twelve amounts. Rules cost words.

**Tess:** And words are the budget. Five hundred.

**Jun:** I want numbers before anyone decides: how much it costs on average, and how much it saves in the bad
starts. I can do it this week.

**Nora:** Parked. It goes into the sail vote with Jun's numbers. Kai, Amara: you're arguing it together.

Into the ledger: **I-13, dollar-cost averaging for the 2028 money: parked until the sail vote.**
