---

08:00. The roster deadline passed while I was pulling the first tray of manoushe out of the oven. I checked the
time on the bakery clock and felt nothing, which is what a deadline should feel like when you've done it early.

The house rule says no stretch longer than fourteen room days without a day outside. Since the 27th of September
I've been in the room for more room days than I can count without Tess's log. So today was outside. No Long Room,
no group call. Jun posted at 6:58 anyway (**$289,893**, Friday's close, **35bp**), and I let myself reply with one
word: "Thanks."

---

Netball at two. We lost. I played goal defence like a person who'd been awake for four days in a room where time
doesn't work, which is to say badly and with great confidence. Our coach told me I was "reading the play too
early". I said, "That's my whole problem," and she laughed, and I didn't explain.

---

At home, Lina was doing her spelling words at the kitchen table, at the end where we used to lay out the bills. She
had a list of ten. I tested her. She got nine. She asked if I'd been to "the room" this week.

I've never told her about the Long Room. Nobody tells anyone about the Long Room. I asked what room.

**Lina:** The one you go to. After Keel nights you're older.

**Me:** Older how?

**Lina:** You talk like Mum after night shift. Slow, and like you already know what I'm going to say.

I didn't have an answer. I said I'd been working hard. She said, "Not like homework hard. Like you went away." Then
she asked me to test her on the tenth word again, which was *separate*, and she got it right.

I sat at that end of the table for a while after she went to bed. This is the table where Dad said, "We pay the ones
we promised first." I'm not sure he meant the promises to yourself.

---

### The bus home from netball, and the instruction

I'd brought the TNA instruction to read on the bus. I'd read it before, in August, the way you read a menu. This
time I read it like Felix.

Three Trading Notes, "exactly as it appears in WInS". A reflection of a hundred words or fewer for each. Each trade
"supported, tested, or refined" the strategy. Wharton "will verify" the notes match WInS.

Then the line I'd skimmed: at this stage the team is "not expected to calculate the final size of the operating
reserve, complete long-term portfolio projections, determine Laura's facility contribution, or draft her
communication to co-sponsors." Those come later.

So what? It means the TNA is small and exact. It's not where we prove the whole plan. It's where we prove that our
decisions came from our plan. Three trades, three hundred words, and every word has to be true.

I made a list on my phone, the way I do:

- Pick three trades: one that supported, one that tested, one that refined. (Jun, Amara, Kai should each own one.)
- Copy each note exactly. (Felix.)
- Strict word count. (Tess.)
- Nothing about the reserve, the projections, the facility or co-sponsors. (Everyone.)

Then I looked at the list and saw that I'd written myself into every line except the ones with a name. In September
I'd have taken all four. I deleted my name from the top and wrote "Nora: the board, the vote, the submit." That's
the job. The rest is theirs.

Sunday photo tomorrow. Fourteen days.
