top 50 of our notes, the ideas we'd saved to surprise a judge, and the combined ideas from Thursday. They made 144
attacks, three of them called fatal.

Their marks averaged 3.4 out of 5, and all four gave 3 for creativity and 3 for what we learned along the way. They all
said top 50 but not top 10, and they gave the same three reasons. The range Laura gives co-sponsors reads like one
figure with a second one stuck on. What Caplet actually bought in the competition looks different from what our plan
says to do with the 2028 money, and we never say why. And there are so many borrowed stories and figures that the plan
gets lost.

Kai wrote an answer to all 144, and 22 things changed. From now on Laura gives one figure, $115,000,
and only once the money for it is already bought. Kai also said we'd been talking about the funds as if they were
bonds. One fund's own papers say it doesn't promise any set amount at the end, so we can't call what it pays
guaranteed. Fifty rows of our notes were moved down or reworded. The comfort letter came
out of Laura's words, and so did "her genre fell 22.4%" and the patent for the pencil.

I went through the bond judge's sums first. Then I drew where Laura's money
