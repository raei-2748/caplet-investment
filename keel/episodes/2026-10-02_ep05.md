# FICTION. Episode 5: Ten times

**Friday 2 October 2026, Sydney · Point of view: Tess · Long Room: none · 22 days to the TNA**

> **Summary.** Late on Thursday night Kai typed a practice order for ten times the shares he meant, as a market
> order, and only caught it on the preview screen. Nothing was sent. At lunch he proposes the button rule himself:
> only he places orders, limit orders only, ticket read aloud, Felix watching, screenshots before and after. Felix
> finds that a trade note can be added at preview and may never be editable. Jun knows something about Kai that the
> rest of us don't.
> **Arcs:** 2, 7. **Values:** 5. **Ideas:** none. **Culture:** the button rule begins.

---

*Journal, 12:40.*

Kai came to lunch without his slate in his hand. That has never happened.

He sat down, put both hands flat on the table, and said it before Nora could ask who wanted to go first.

**Kai:** Last night, about quarter to midnight, I was practising in WInS. Just to see the order screen. I typed a
fund order for a test. I meant forty shares. I typed four hundred. And it was a market order, not a limit. I got
all the way to the preview screen before I saw it.

**Nora:** Did it go?

**Kai:** No. I hit back. Nothing was sent. Order history's empty. Felix checked it with me at 7 this morning.

**Felix:** Empty. No open orders, no cancelled ones.

**Kai:** But it would have gone. One more click. It's a practice order in a competition with fake money and I still
felt sick. So. Here's a rule, and it's mine, so nobody has to make it.

He'd written it on a napkin.

> Only one person places orders: me. Limit orders only. I read the ticket out loud before I press it: ticker,
> buy or sell, quantity, limit price, note. Felix watches, on a call if not in the room. Screenshot before, screenshot
> after.

**Amara:** Why you? If you're the one who nearly did it?

**Kai:** Because now I'm the one who'll never do it again.

Nobody laughed. Jun looked at Kai for a second longer than anyone else did. I don't know what that was. I wrote
"Jun knows something" in the margin and then crossed it out because it isn't mine to write. Then I wrote it again
in pencil, because the journal is supposed to be true.

**Nora:** Objections? Two minutes.

**Felix:** One addition. The note. WInS lets you add the trade note in Preview Order, before you place it. The FAQ
says notes can be added or viewed later, "Add/View Notes". It doesn't say whether a note can be *edited*. So I
assume it can't. The note we write at preview is the note Wharton sees.

**Kai:** So I read the note out loud too.

**Felix:** And it's under three hundred characters. The note box stops at 300.

**Me:** Three hundred characters is about fifty words. The note has to say why, how it fits, and what it does for
Laura. In fifty words.

**Amara:** That's a haiku with a bond in it.

**Nora:** Decision: Kai's rule, with Felix's addition. Owner: Kai. From today. Tess, journal.

I wrote it. Then I added the column the journal has that most teams' don't: **What we'd do differently.** I wrote:
*Practise on paper first. The screen is not a place to learn.*

---

*Journal, 16:15. The bus.*

Jun and Kai sat together on the bus, which they don't usually; Kai usually sits with whoever is loudest. I was two
rows behind with Felix. I couldn't hear them. Kai was looking at the window and Jun was talking, which is the wrong
way round for both of them.

Felix said, very quietly, "Should we ask?"

I said no. Some things are a person's own. I said it like a rule, and Felix nodded like it was one.

Jun's number came in while we were on the bus, for Thursday's close: **$289,880**, about **35 basis points** of
room. A little dearer than Wednesday. Kai didn't reply with a cat. I noticed.

---

*Journal, 21:30.*

Nora's Sunday photo will be on Sunday, but she sent a Friday one, which she never does. Just the board. Under the
countdown she'd written: **Button rule. Kai. Since 2 Oct.**

Kai replied with one word: "Good."
