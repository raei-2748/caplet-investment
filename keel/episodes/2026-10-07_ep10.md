# FICTION. Episode 10: Night orders

**Wednesday 7 October 2026, Sydney (the night of Tue 6 to Wed 7) · Point of view: Tess · Long Room: none (a call) · 17 days to the TNA**

> **Summary.** The first orders. At 00:20, ten minutes before New York opens, Kai reopens the 2037 vote with Jun's
> margin sizing: the term fund needs a cushion because its payout isn't fixed; the February 2036 bond doesn't. The
> team re-votes 5-1 for the bond. Nine orders go in under the button rule; one bond fails Jun's 25bp price check and
> waits a day. In the morning Wharton's Week 2 email confirms that Laura's real plan, both deposits, may use only

> **Arcs:** 2 (two captains). **Values:** 3, 2. **Ideas:** I-03, I-04 applied.

*Journal, 00:14.*

Six of us on the call. Nora in her kitchen with the light off so Lina wouldn't wake. Kai at his desk, two screens,
the order page on one and Felix's checklist on the other. Jun had posted the sizing sheet at 23:50: every rung, its
quantity, its margin.

*00:20.* Kai unmuted.

**Kai:** Before I read the first ticket. I want the 2037 vote back. I've got evidence, not volume. Jun's sheet,
rows five and six.

**Nora:** Kai. It's twenty past midnight.

**Kai:** That's why I'm saying it now and not after I've pressed the button. Rule from last Wednesday: reopen with
new evidence, out loud. Jun's sheet is new. It wasn't there on Monday.

Silence. Then Nora: "Go. Three minutes."

**Kai:** On Monday we picked IBTR because it matches the date. Jun sized it tonight. Because its payout isn't fixed,
he had to buy extra, a margin, to be fairly sure it covers $50,000. The Feb-2036 bond doesn't need a margin. Its
coupons and face value are fixed. It costs a little more today. What we buy with the difference is certainty. And
the cash sits ten months, but cash doesn't lose dollars.

**Jun:** That's right. The bond's rung is all fixed cash. IBTR's isn't. On my sheet the difference in cost is small.

**Felix:** And it's a Treasury, which is the word we use with judges.

**Amara:** And if the reader is afraid?

**Kai:** The bond tells them the amount. The fund tells them "about".

**Nora:** Tess. You voted IBTR for "simpler to explain". Still?

I thought about it for longer than three minutes allowed. *What would we lose if we cut it?*

**Me:** The simplicity was "one kind of rung for 2033 to 2037". With the bond it's "funds where WInS has no bond,
bonds everywhere else". That's just as simple. Simpler, maybe. It's the rule we already use.

**Nora:** Vote.

Five to one for the bond. Nora voted for IBTR. She said, "I still prefer the date match. I'm carrying the bond." She
said it plainly. Kai said, "Thank you," which I have never heard him say to her.

---

*00:30.* New York opened. Kai read the first ticket aloud:

**Kai:** IBTM. Buy. Limit. Quantity as on row one. Note: "Pays for Laura's first payment, 1 January 2033..." Felix?

**Felix:** Checked. Matches the sheet. Note matches the counted version. 252 characters.

**Kai:** Screenshot. Pressing. Screenshot.

That was the rhythm for the next two hours. Four term funds, 2033 to 2036. Then the bonds: Kai read each one, Jun
read out the yield he'd worked out from WInS's last price and the curve's yield for that maturity, and the
difference.

**Jun:** Feb-2036: three basis points off. Fine. Feb-2037: two. Fine. May-2038: thirty-one. No.

**Kai:** Thirty-one.

**Jun:** Rule's twenty-five. It might be a stale price. It might be real. Either way we don't buy it tonight.

**Kai:** It's six basis points over a rule of thumb you made up on Thursday.

**Jun:** Yes. And if I'd made it up as thirty-five you'd be right. I didn't.

Kai laughed, the real laugh. "Fine. Tomorrow night." He moved on.

Nine orders by 02:41. The funds filled at their limits within the hour. The bonds would fill at end-of-day prices.

---

*06:12.* Felix, in the group chat, from the bus:

**Felix:** Week 2 email. "Portfolio construction and recommendations for both contributions may include only
approved investment types: eligible stocks, ETFs and Treasury bonds available on WInS." Both contributions. The
$150,000 too.

**Amara:** So Laura's whole real plan has to be built from what's on WInS?

**Felix:** That's what it says. Our book already is. Nothing changes for us. But anyone planning to use something
WInS doesn't list for 2028 has just lost it.

**Nora:** Good. Logged.

*06:58.* Jun, as always: **$288,929** for Tuesday's close (fills not affected; it's a price, not our portfolio),
**39bp** of room.

*07:00.* New York closed. Felix checked order history: nine filled or pending end-of-day. Nothing unexpected. Kai
sent the before-and-after screenshots to the drive, eighteen of them, named by ticket.

Then Nora, privately, to me: "I was right to wait for the notes. He was right about the bond. Is that both of us
winning or both of us losing?" I said it was the door working. She sent back a full stop, which is Nora for "yes".
