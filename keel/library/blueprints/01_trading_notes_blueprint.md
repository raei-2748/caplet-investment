## 1. What is actually required (official)
- Select **3 Trading Notes from trades executed in WInS**; include each **exactly as it appears in WInS**; "Yes, we will
  verify this" (TN p.2).
- For each: a reflection of **100 words or fewer** explaining (TN p.2):
  1. why the team made the decision;
  2. how it aligned with the overall investment strategy;
  3. how it supported Laura's goals, funding needs, or risk considerations.
- Together the three should show "individual investments as part of a cohesive portfolio strategy"; quality of
  reasoning matters, **not** whether the trade made money (TN p.1).
- Not expected: reserve size, long-term projections, facility contribution, co-sponsor text (TN p.1).
- Trades need not still be held at the end; a later sale can still be a good example (TN p.1).
- Must be consistent with the strategy the IPS will state on Nov 6; all three deliverables are judged together (C p.4).
- Evaluation criteria that apply most: Investment Strategy (consistency, diversification), Client Knowledge,
  Articulation of Competition Experience (decision process, learning) (D).

## 3. What to put in the WInS note at the moment of trading (elements, in the team's words)
The note is quoted verbatim later, so write it well at trade time. Each note should contain:
- the action and instrument (buy/sell, ticker, rough portfolio share);
- the role in Laura's plan (which "job": the promise, the growth sleeve, or discipline);
- the reason now (the signal or rule that triggered it);
- the main risk accepted (e.g. bond prices fall if rates rise, which does not matter for held-to-maturity matching).
Keep it short and plain English. Check the WInS character limit first.

## 4. Reflection checklist (per note, <=100 words)
- [ ] Answers all three required questions (why / strategy fit / Laura's needs), in that order or clearly.
- [ ] Names Laura's specific need (fixed $50k payments 2033-2042, facility contribution, credibility in 2031), not
      generic "diversification".
- [ ] Uses at most one number, and it is verified (e.g. "payments cost about $292k in today's Treasury prices").
- [ ] Mentions a trade-off or what was learned (this is what separates strong teams).
- [ ] No jargon without meaning (say "moves like the payments when rates change", not "DV01").
- [ ] Consistent with the other two notes and the planned IPS (same strategy words, same percentages).
- [ ] Count: <=100 words.

## 5. Judge red flags to avoid
- Hot themes that don't fit the mandate (the team's own pre-mortem: ARKK/MSOS-type "storyteller" trades).
- Justifying by returns ("it went up 3%").
- Any number that contradicts the IPS or Final Report.
- Notes written after the fact (they must match WInS exactly).
