# Firm A

## 1. How the ten payments are protected
The portfolio is split into a Treasury bucket (intermediate U.S. Treasuries) and a stock bucket (broad U.S. and international stock funds). A glide path lowers the stock share each year: 65%, 60%, 50%, 40%, 30% and 20% of the portfolio in 2027-2032. From 2030 the Treasury bucket buys Treasuries maturing in 2033-2042 in stages, so the ten-year reserve (about $395k at today's forward rates) is complete on 1 January 2033.

## 2. What "high certainty" means and how it was checked
High certainty means at least a 95% probability that the portfolio covers the reserve on 1 January 2033. Checked by a 200,000-path Monte Carlo on JPM's 2026 long-term assumptions (U.S. stocks 6.7% compound, Treasuries 4.0%) and a one-point uncertainty in 2033 interest rates. Model estimate: funded in 99.3% of paths; 91.4% if the 2028 deposit is $75k.

## 3. How growth is pursued and with what money
All money not yet needed by the Treasury bucket sits in the stock bucket, rebalanced once a year to the glide path. The average stock share over 2027-2032 is 44%. Model estimate: median money left after the reserve is $206k.

## 4. What Laura can tell co-sponsors in 2031 (range and confidence)
The range is the 10th to 90th percentile of the model's 2033 facility contribution, re-run with 2031 values, stated with 80% confidence. Model estimate from today: $75k to $302k, median $175k.

## 5. Flexibility after 2033
The facility receives 85% of the money above the reserve. The other 15% is kept in Treasury bills for project changes.

## 6. What the competition portfolio (WInS) holds
The 2027 allocation: VTI 45%, VXUS 20%, IEF 30%, SHY 4%, cash 1%. Five ETF trades at $25 each. Pending WInS availability check.

## 7. The main risks the firm accepts
- Payments depend on markets until the reserve is bought: model estimate 0.7% miss, 8.6% if the 2028 deposit is $75k, 44% if it does not arrive.
- A one-point rate fall before the staged purchases adds up to about $16k to the reserve's cost (model estimate).
- The 2031 range is wide: its bottom is less than half its median.
