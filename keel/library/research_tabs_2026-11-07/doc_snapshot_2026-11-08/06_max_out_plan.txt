2. Ideas a generic team would not have (all within the IPS)
The reserve is its own low-risk benchmark. Pension plans must now report what their promises would cost at low-default-risk bond yields. Laura's reserve actually holds those bonds, so the two figures coincide. Fits Laura because: a statistician can check the funding ratio herself.
Size the reserve as if no interest is reinvested, the way bond escrows are sized. Because a 2-point fall is the historical median, this is the honest way to size it. Fits Laura because: "no payment depends on a share price" becomes literally true.
A written, already-owned floor helps her pass foundations' readiness tests. Kresge counted written pledges toward the share of a goal raised. The Royal Ontario Museum's unpaid pledges show why money already paid in matters. Fits Laura because: the case names credibility as her risk.
The prepaid decade removes founder dependence. Bamboo Curtain is the Taiwan lesson (state no cause for its closure). Fits Laura because: a residency in Taiwan, founded by her.
Her payments are the stable base that Taiwan grants sit on top of. Ministry grants are reviewed every year and can be cut by the legislature. Fits Laura because: it explains the case rule using her actual market.
A currency table that names its own limit. The money kept back covers an ordinary Taiwan-dollar move (about +11% at the 90th percentile) but not a return to the 2022 rate (+18.5%). Fits Laura because: she has lived there and will notice if this is overclaimed.
A PM-style "if this happens, we do this" rule table, with 2031 as the formal review. Fits Laura because: she worked as a product manager running roadmaps and A/B tests.
3. Contradictions and tensions
7% vs 7.5% vs about 6.5%. R3 verified J.P. Morgan's 7.5%. R4 could not verify it and leaned toward FP Canada. R6 found that the submitted IPS contains no rate at all. Judgment: use 7.5% as the base so it matches the locked Trading Note 3. Call it the top of the dated range, and show Vanguard's about 5.8% as the low case. The effect is a few thousand dollars.
"3 in 4 reach the top" may be out of date. If half of VT moves into Treasuries before 2031 (as the IPS says), the top is mostly fixed in 2031, so the October figure probably reflects the earlier design. Judgment: re-run on the IPS design before quoting.
In-range confidence: "at least about 99%" (R4) vs "99 in 100 that the floor pays at least 98.7% of face value" (R5). These are different quantities. Judgment: compute the in-range probability directly.
Quoting Laura. Client §8 says "avoid quoting her"; R1 recommends one quote. Judgment: one short, cited, public quote is evidence, not putting words in her mouth. Never paraphrase her into a commitment.
"TIPS banned" (three tabs) vs the Week 5 email, which bans only individual TIPS and STRIPS. Judgment: correct the wording. The reason for not using TIPS (payments are fixed in dollars) still stands.
Two versions of the "first plan". One was about three-quarters shares and missed payments; the other locked the ladder in 2027 and could not miss. Judgment: the team must reconcile them before writing E1.
Taiwan building inflation: M4's 3.5% vs 7.18% now. The 7.18% is a snippet only. Judgment: model a 3.5–7% range, applied to the building, not to the payments.
Set in Stone "92%". That figure is producing theatres only. Use 46–92% by building type (Table E). Don't use the different measure on p.13.
The "AFP 60–80%" figure comes from a careers-site page, not AFP guidance. Drop it, and keep Capital Campaign Pro's 65%, or 75–80% for small organisations.
Giving USA figures. Label them as inflation-adjusted, and use the arts figures instead.
"Required yield 5.09%" vs "Treasuries yield 5.15%." The bases differ (5.09% annual equals 5.02% half-yearly), and the 5.15% matches roughly the mid-October curve. Put a date on it or drop it.
Funding ratio. The 99.3% compares the deposit, not the holdings, with the cost. Once the bonds are held, the ratio should sit near 100%.
Westminster praised for "adapting" vs Guide p.5's ban on redesign. Present Caplet's discipline as following the Guide.
