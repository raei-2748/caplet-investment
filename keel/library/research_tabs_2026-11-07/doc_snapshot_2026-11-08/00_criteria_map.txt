Criteria Map: where each judging criterion is served
Wharton judges the Trading Notes, the IPS and the Final Report together on five criteria. This tab says which Research tab feeds each one, what is already strong, and what is still missing. Start here.
Investment Strategy
Feeds: Numbers and Models (sections 3-6), Ideas and Sources (section 1).
Strong: one clear design that never changed in substance from late September to the IPS; rival plans tested on the same markets; a named weak point (reinvesting interest) with a priced answer.
Missing: one numbers sheet on one engine and one dated curve, built on the IPS as submitted. Cite the Trading Notes' 7.5% for shares (J.P. Morgan's 2027 estimate for global equities, 19 Oct 2026) and test a range around it.
Client Knowledge
Feeds: Client (all sections), Co-sponsor Brief (sections 1 and 5).
Strong: the career-as-unsellable-shares idea, backed by research on author incomes and human capital; her goals ranked from the case; every risk tied to a rule.
Missing: a short answer on why the plan suits Laura and not any client with fixed payments. The Client tab's "what the case does not say" list should stay as questions, never as facts.
Portfolio Analysis
Feeds: Numbers and Models (all sections).
Strong: the history test since 1871, the stress tests, the "delete the shares" test, and the WInS book moving with what it owes.
Missing: two or three charts at most, each answering one question. WInS gains are never added to Laura's projections.
Articulation of Competition Experience
Feeds: Keel Experience (sections 1, 4, 5 and 7).
Strong: real, dated decisions with causes and costs: the 27 Sep model, the stale-price catch, the 00:30 market open, the trading rule after the mis-click, Wharton's rule changes, and writing the IPS ourselves.
Missing: each teammate's own sentence on what they did and learned. Only the team can write this. Keel's characters and scenes never appear as Caplet's experience.
Creativity and Presentation
Feeds: Co-sponsor Brief (all sections), Ideas and Sources (section 3).
Strong: a range whose bottom is already owned before Laura says it, the same rule fundraisers use; a plain draft paragraph for her materials.
Missing: the Final Report's own shape. Lessons from the IPS: one real insight, warm and direct sentences, clear structure, no invented names or motifs.
