come from each commit's own offset. A "log date" is the date written in an AI log, with no clock time.

**Column meanings.**
- **Tool / model family**: the product and model as the record names it. Where the record does not name it, the
  cell says UNVERIFIED. An inference is labelled as one.
- **Requested by (as recorded)**: only what a record says, quoted. An AI file saying "at Ray's request" is quoted,
  not confirmed. "not recorded" means no file says. Account names never prove who typed or asked.
- **Kept / rejected**: what a record shows happened to the output. Otherwise "unknown - team to fill".
- **Touched a deliverable?** Nothing has yet been submitted to SMApply except the roster, and no record links AI to
  the roster. So the categories are:
  - **YES-LOCKED**: AI wording or AI actions inside the WInS record, which is permanent. The TNA must quote three of
    these notes "exactly as it appears in WInS".
  - **YES-DRAFT**: AI text sits in a draft of the IPS or the TNA reflections (Google Doc "IPS Report").
  - **INDIRECT**: AI ideas, numbers, models, specs or reviews that shaped what the deliverables describe.
  - **NO**: tooling, admin or research that no deliverable draws on.
- **Strength**: **strong** = a system timestamp (git, GitHub, Drive, WInS order time, file time); **medium** = a
  team document, or a measured text overlap; **weak** = only an AI-written file says it.

## 6. A recommended Works Cited structure for AI use

### 6.1 What Wharton has asked for (exact words)

- **2026-27 AI Policy page** (https://globalyouth.wharton.upenn.edu/ai-policy/, read 20 Oct 2026; `web/ai_policy_20Oct.txt`):
  - :377: "All material referenced or generated by AI should be cited like any other reference material (with due
    consideration for the quality of the reference, which may be poor). If you use AI to assist you in any way
    during the competition, how you use it must be recorded in your Works Cited pages."
  - :347: "AI-generated work should be cited like any other reference material, including how and where students
    used AI-generated information."
- **2026-27 Rules & Roles page**
  (https://globalyouth.wharton.upenn.edu/competitions/investment-competition/rules-roles/, read 20 Oct 2026;
  `web/rules_roles_20Oct.txt:467-475`): "AI-generated work may not be submitted as your own." "Any AI-generated
  material included in your report must be properly cited, just like any other reference source."
- **2023-24 Final Report spec** (the only past spec found that set a format; https://globalyouth.wharton.upenn.edu/investment-competition/deliverables/,
  WordPress page 3768, last modified 30 May 2024, cached 16 Oct 2026 in `oct16/cache/bc62866f...`):
  - "All sources, images, and other media including use of generative AI must be cited using the American
    Psychological Association (APA) citation style. Please create a separate bibliography page attached to the end of
    your report titled 'Works Cited'."
  - "In your citation, please include 1-2 sentences on how you used that AI tool to achieve your goal."
  - "Do NOT include external links to content outside of your report. Our judges are instructed not to open any
    links within your report."
  - The page count was given "(not including works cited page)".
- **2026-27 official files**:
  - IPS instructions :123-125: "formal citations are not permitted" in the IPS, and "Relevant sources and
    supporting evidence must be included in the Final Report."
  - The TNA instructions have no citation slot. The 2026-27 Guide, case and SMApply pages do not mention AI
    (grep, 20 Oct).
  - The Final Report instructions come out on 9 Nov. **They decide the format; this section is a fallback until
    then.**

### 6.2 What follows for the structure (constraints, not wording)

1. **One place.** The Final Report's Works Cited is the only place where AI use can be recorded for all three
   deliverables. The TNA (23 Oct) and the IPS (6 Nov) cannot carry citations. Under the policy, AI-generated wording
   cannot be made acceptable there by citing it later. The register is what lets the Works Cited be complete and
   true.
2. **Per tool, with "how"** (2023-24: 1-2 sentences "on how you used that AI tool"; 2026-27: "how and where").
   Group the 33 rows into about 8-10 entries by tool and purpose, not 33 lines.
3. **"Where" means the deliverable and section.** Use INDIRECT/YES-DRAFT/YES-LOCKED to say which deliverable each
   use informed.
4. **No live links for judges to follow.** Judges have been told not to open links (2023-24; also the 2026-27 IPS
   rules). A reference may still carry a URL if the style needs one, but no entry may depend on a link (for
   example a chat share link or the repo) to be understood.
5. **Name what is not known.** For S1-S4, an entry "tool not identified" is truer than a guess.
6. **Keep it out of the page count if allowed.** In 2023-24 the Works Cited did not count toward the page range.
   Whether that holds in 2026-27 is UNVERIFIED until 9 Nov.
