## 1. Bottom line (ten points)

1. **Every semifinal from 2021-22 to 2025-26 used the same format:** a **recorded 10-minute video pitch** to the client, then **four written answers of 250 words or fewer**. The four questions arrived about 10 days after the video was due, and teams had about 4 days to answer (NS22-NS26). In no year did the official instructions include a live Q&A for semifinalists.
2. **The 2026-27 wording is the same wording that sat beside those recorded videos.** "The Semifinals will be conducted virtually" appeared in 2022-23 and 2024-25 too (WP3774:38, 90; RR:479). The FAQ's "a short presentation (via video conference for the Semifinals …) … to a live panel of judges" (FAQ:109) matches the 2024-25 FAQ, and that year's semifinal was a recorded video (KB §2.4). The "Recording Policy" banning recording and posting during the semifinals also stood in 2022-23 and 2023-24 (WP3774:40, 92). It refers to Wharton's live events, not to the team's own video. **Planning assumption: a recorded video plus four written answers.** Be ready for a live element. Two school write-ups mention one: a 2025-26 semifinalist expected "a virtual interview with the program's judges", and a 2024-25 student recalled "extra questions we had to answer in a Zoom meeting". Both are UNVERIFIED school claims (Part 2c).
3. **"Virtual Semifinals" on Wharton's calendar has meant the live results events in March,** not the judging. In 2024 and 2025 there were "five virtual events" in Wharton's WAVE room. In 2025, students "beam[ed] in … to introduce their teammates and learn which two teams from each event would advance" (News 2024, 2025). For Caplet these fall around **11 pm to 1 am Sydney**.
4. **Timing for Sydney (FORECAST).** Top 50 announced mid-to-late January 2027. The video is due on a Monday 11-18 days later, at **8:00 am ET = 00:00 (midnight) Sydney** the next day (Tuesday). Written questions follow about 10 days later at about **01:00 Sydney on a Friday**, and are due the next **Tuesday 00:00 Sydney**. Results come in early-to-mid March. The finale is **Thu 29 to Fri 30 Apr 2027 ET**; in Sydney that runs from about **Thu 22:00 through Saturday morning**. The window opens in the Knox summer holidays: Term 1 starts **Fri 29 Jan 2027** (Knox term dates, fetched 20 Oct).
5. **"No new material" is a Final Report job, due 4 Dec.** In every year since 2022-23, anything "completely new" meant disqualification (NS23:27-31; NS26:49-52). Whatever the video, the written answers or a judge's question needs must therefore already be in the IPS or Final Report. That includes the case-study holding, both portfolio snapshots, every number to be spoken, the 2031 range, the stress tests, the weaknesses and each student's contribution. Part 4 is the checklist. It is the only part of this playbook that matters before 4 Dec.
6. **The hindsight trap is new this year.** Laura's first deposit is dated January 2027 (case:43). By the semifinal, real January 2027 prices will be known. Presenting "what the ladder really cost on 1 Jan 2027" would be new analysis, and the Guide bans redesigning "after observing the results" (Guide:158-159). Use the Final Report's dated numbers. If asked, answer with the rule the plan already set.
7. **Case study: IBTM is the strongest choice; the team decides.** It does two jobs (the 2033 payment and the facility floor), it is about a third of the WInS book, it is the holding most judges will not have seen before, and it pre-empts the hardest question ("why is a fund whose payout is not fixed your floor?", DRILL K3). VT is the safe fallback. An individual T-bond probably does not qualify under the past wording, "one stock or ETF" (NS24-NS26). The Final Report should carry a plain analysis of **both IBTM and VT**, so either can be used without new material (Part 5).
8. **Slides: plan 11-12, cap 15.** In 2025-26 the limit was "up to 15 slides", with "approximately 9–11 core slides" advised and a suggested timed flow (NS26:17-19, 79-87). The Part 6 slide plan gives each slide one job, its evidence and the Final Report section it must come from.
9. **Every member speaks and states their value.** "Every team member must have a speaking role and briefly explain how they contributed value to the team" (NS26:42-43; also NS24, NS25). Each claim must match a logged, dated product. Wharton bans inventing "teamwork and experiential stories" (RR:557). With roles still blank and the journal stopped on 7 Oct (SYN §4b), this is a gap the team can close only by keeping records from now on (Part 7).
10. **Australia-specific.** There is no travel funding and no visa sponsorship (RR:481). The finale may be joined virtually, and the 2025-26 registration offered "In-person / Video Conference / Hybrid" (WP10236:5). A virtual finale means presenting at about **10 pm to 2 am Sydney on Fri 30 Apr**, in Knox Term 2 week 1 (Term 2 starts Tue 27 Apr 2027).
