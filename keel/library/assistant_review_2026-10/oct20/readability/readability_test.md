## 3. The 10 most confusing sentences (problem and fix only; no rewritten text)

1. **PACK:3** "State of play: at 10:15 today nothing had changed since the baseline ... (DELTA §2-3). The TNA is due Sat 24 Oct 08:00 ..."
   - Why: 85 words in one paragraph. "Baseline" is undefined. The deadline is at the very end and has no time zone.
   - Fix: move the deadline (with ET and Sydney) and the team target to line 1. Split the state of play into 3 short bullets.
2. **PACK:5** The sources line: 16 codes and paths in one line.
   - Why: a student meets 16 new codes before the agenda and cannot open any of the files.
   - Fix: move it to the end as a key. Add one line saying students do not need to open these files.
3. **PACK:22** "Proposal: quorum 4 of 6. A motion passes with more votes for than against, at least 4 cast. Absent members vote in chat by 22:00 ..."
   - Why: it does not say whether chat votes count toward the 4, or what happens on a tie.
   - Fix: number the rule's parts. State whether chat votes count and the tie result.
4. **PACK:42** "Paste check: VT 288, IBTR 294, IBTM 292 characters. Two other notes are 288 and one other is 294 ..."
   - Why: "characters" of what (spaces included?) is not said, and the "other notes" are not named.
   - Fix: say the count includes spaces. Name the look-alike notes, or point to KIT "Common checks".
5. **PACK:31 (and KIT:257 vs KIT:390)** "each contain a 13-word clause copied verbatim ..."
   - Why: the clause is not quoted in PACK. KIT:390 quotes it as "12 words".
   - Fix: quote the clause once. Make the word count the same in both places.
6. **PACK:54** The "Reject all" sentence (about 90 words, 6 steps in one line).
   - Why: steps, warnings and an UNVERIFIED point are mixed together.
   - Fix: turn it into numbered steps. Put the warning ("may reject students' own suggestions") before the step.
7. **PACK:55** "The bigger risk is not pressing a Submit that is the TNA's real delivery." Then 3 cases.
   - Why: the double negative is hard to read. It seems to clash with PACK:98 ("Don't press Submit ... tonight") because it does not say this is for Thursday.
   - Fix: put "on Thursday, at submit time" first. Use a 3-row table (what you see / what you press).
8. **PACK:57** "The fork is public, so every commit in the network can be fetched through it by SHA ..."
   - Why: four GitHub terms, and no plain reason why it matters (client contact details are exposed). The "fork owner" is not named as a role.
   - Fix: give the risk in one plain line first. Gloss fork and commit. Say who the fork owner is by role.
9. **KIT:14** "IBTR room (basis D): $297,162 / $2,838 / 9.8bp on 19 Oct, up from $298,940 / $1,060 / 3.6bp on 16 Oct."
   - Why: three unlabelled numbers. Basis D, room and bp are not yet defined. "Up from" is unclear (the cost went down, the room went up).
   - Fix: define basis D, room and bp before first use. Label the three numbers (cost / room / yield fall that uses it up), or point to the table at KIT:477.
10. **KIT:520** "2 days 6-14% measured at the 21 Oct close; 7-16% that either the 20 Oct or the 21 Oct close is over the line (σ 0.71-1.0, re-derived 20 Oct)."
    - Why: two ranges, a Greek letter and "the line" in one sentence. The useful answer ("about 1 in 10") comes last.
    - Fix: lead with the plain answer. Move the ranges and σ to a note.
