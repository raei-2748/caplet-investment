# E07 · In the manner of Paul Graham: twelve one-liners, then the cull

> Exercise, AI DRAFT (Claude, 27 Oct 2026). Practice, not IPS text, not for submission. Brief: write sentences that
> could stand alone, then kill the ones that are only shaped like wisdom. Graham's own test: "Style is doing things in
> a distinctive way without trying to. Trying to is affectation."

1. A Treasury bond is not safe. It is safe for a date.
2. Laura doesn't have an average. She has ten Januaries.
3. Inflation can make $50,000 buy less. It cannot make $50,000 cost more.
4. Diversification is admitting you don't know which. A ladder is admitting you do know when.
5. Stocks decide how generous Laura can be, never whether she can pay.
6. A range is a promise with a margin. A single number is a promise with a wish.
7. The plan's most dangerous day is the day it looks overcautious.
8. The bottom of the range is money she owns. The top is money she expects.
9. We don't forecast interest rates. We arranged not to need to.
10. Every promise in this plan has a maturity date. Hope doesn't.
11. The cheapest moment to be careful is before anyone is tempted not to be.
12. Owed, not hoped for.

## Critique: the cull
- **Keep (true, specific to Caplet, and a judge would remember them):** 3 (answers the inflation assumption exactly),
  4 (a real distinction between two risk tools, the only aphorism here that teaches something), 1 (Final Report or
  the opening of an argument, not the IPS: it needs the paragraph after it).
- **Keep for the Final Report:** 7 (the behavioural risk to a ladder is selling it in a boom; true and under-said).
- **Kill:** 2 (cute, and the plan isn't judged on averages anyway); 5 and 8 (true but they're the plan restated as
  a slogan); 6 (the "X is a promise with Y" pair is the most AI-shaped sentence in English); 9 (false as worded:
  rates still matter for reinvestment and for the build cost); 10 and 11 (fortune cookie); 12 (a tagline, not a
  sentence).
- **The lesson:** eight of twelve died, and every one that died was a parallel pair ("X is A. Y is B."). Parallel
  pairs are where AI hides when it has no argument. The survivors each contain a fact a reader didn't have. That is
  the test: **an aphorism must carry information, not just symmetry.**
