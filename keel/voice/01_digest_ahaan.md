## 3. Where Keel disagrees, with reasons (the cover note asks for this)
- **"Elaborate and vast" metaphor vs "subtle".** Books 1 and 2 pull in opposite directions inside 500 words. Keel's
  answer: the IPS gets the bare bones (one image, the name, at most one extension); the vastness goes into the Final
  Report's *structure*, as a medicine leaflet whose every heading is an investing rule (I-2). Elaborate, and still
  never in every sentence.
- **"Write like me".** Copying Ahaan's phrases ("seductive in its simplicity", "not without ammunition") would hand
  him back his own tics, and a judge reading 400 IPSs would hear a costume. Keel copies his *engine*: argue, give the
  other side its best line, build long and land short, care about something.
- **Voice as wit.** The professionals in `02_reading_notes.md` are funny about once a page. Their voice comes from
  their opinions. So the guide starts with seven things Caplet believes (section 1), not with jokes.

## 4. How Keel digests a message from now on
The same day a long message arrives: (1) split it into asks, in a table like section 2; (2) turn each ask into a
test that can fail; (3) show one piece of work that passes, or say plainly why not yet. The verbatim copy in
`BIBLE.md` is the archive, not the digest.
