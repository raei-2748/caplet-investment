# Voice study: finding Caplet's voice (Tue 27 Oct 2026)

AI-generated (Claude Code). Not for submission. Ray asked, on 27 Oct, for Keel to **stop writing the IPS for now**
and find a distinctive voice first: read great essays, investor letters and bank research, work out their patterns,
practise, and digest Ahaan's long messages properly. This folder is that work.

**The finding in one paragraph.** Keel's prose isn't flat because of bad words. It is flat because it has amputated
its long sentences (3-4% of sentences in the IPS drafts are 30+ words; Ahaan's essays: 18%), carries five times
Ahaan's density of numbers, and has a quarter of his idea-words: it lists what the plan owns and leaves out what the
plan believes. Every writer worth copying (Buffett, Marks, Levine, Mauboussin, the banks' best, the Hopkins essays,
Ahaan himself) argues with someone, admits something, and builds a long sentence before landing a short one. The
fix is to find the idea first; the sentences follow.
