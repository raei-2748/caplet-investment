# Firm D

## 1. How the ten payments are protected
In January 2027 the first $300,000 buys ten zero-coupon U.S. Treasuries, each maturing on 15 November before one $50,000 payment (2033-2042). On the 2026-09-25 curve they cost $294,387. If money falls short, the latest payments are bought first; the 2028 deposit completes the rest.

## 2. What "high certainty" means and how it was checked
The payments are bought, not forecast: fully funded at market prices, each matched by a bond maturing before it, certain in nominal U.S. dollars barring a U.S. Treasury default. Checked on the official Treasury curve. Model estimate: about 1 in 3 rate paths push the cost above $300,000 by January.

## 3. How growth is pursued and with what money
In January 2028 the $150,000 deposit buys a zero-coupon Treasury repaying $150,000 before 2033 (the building minimum; cost about $118k). The remaining ~$40k sits in one world stock fund until 2033. Stocks are about 9% of her money in 2028-2032.

## 4. What Laura can tell co-sponsors in 2031 (range and confidence)
Bottom: the minimum she owns ($150k). Top: the minimum plus half the stock fund's value on 1 January 2031 (model estimate median $175k). The gift falls below the bottom only on a U.S. default. The top is reached if the fund is no lower in 2033: model estimate 73%; U.S. stocks' two-year return was non-negative in 84% of periods since 1928.

## 5. Flexibility after 2033
The ten bonds become the operating reserve (about $395k in 2033), held to maturity. The gift is the minimum plus half the stock fund, capped at the top; the rest stays with Laura in bills. Model estimate: gift $165k/$174k/$188k, kept $16k/$32k/$66k (5th/50th/95th percentile).

## 6. What the competition portfolio (WInS) holds
The 2 January 2028 plan scaled to $300,000: IEF 23.5%, TLH 42.5% (duration-matched to the payments), the 15-Nov-2032 Treasury note or IBTM 24.3%, VT 8.7%, cash about 1%. Pending WInS availability check.

## 7. The main risks the firm accepts
- Less stock, less upside: model estimate 95th percentile $250k.
- A ladder costing over $300,000 leaves some payments waiting on the 2028 deposit.
- A fixed $50,000 buys less each year in Taiwan.
- No 2028 deposit means no building minimum or stock fund.
