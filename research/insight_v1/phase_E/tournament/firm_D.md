# Firm D

## 1. How the ten payments are protected
In January 2027 the first $300,000 buys ten zero-coupon U.S. Treasuries, one maturing on 15 November before each $50,000 payment (2033-2042); on the 2026-09-25 curve they cost $294,387. If money falls short, the latest payments are bought first and the 2028 deposit completes the rest.

## 2. What "high certainty" means and how it was checked
High certainty means each payment is bought at market prices with a bond maturing before it: certain in nominal U.S. dollars unless the U.S. Treasury defaults. Checked on the official Treasury curve. Model estimate: about 1 in 3 rate paths push the cost above $300,000 by January.

## 3. How growth is pursued and with what money
In January 2028 the $150,000 deposit buys a zero-coupon Treasury repaying $150,000 before 2033 (the minimum facility contribution; cost about $118k). The remaining ~$40k goes into one world stock fund. The average stock share over 2027-2032 is 7.5%. Model estimate: median money left after the reserve is $207k.

## 4. What Laura can tell co-sponsors in 2031 (range and confidence)
Bottom: the minimum she owns ($150k). Top: the minimum plus half the stock fund's value on 1 January 2031 (median $175k, model estimate). The bottom holds barring a U.S. Treasury default; the top is reached if the fund is no lower in 2033 than in 2031 (model estimate 73%).

## 5. Flexibility after 2033
The facility receives the minimum plus half the stock fund, capped at the top; the rest is kept in Treasury bills for project changes. Model estimate (median): $174k to the facility, $32k kept.

## 6. What the competition portfolio (WInS) holds
The January 2028 plan scaled to $300,000: IEF 23.5%, TLH 42.5%, the 15-Nov-2032 Treasury note or IBTM 24.3%, VT 8.7%, cash 1%. Four trades ($25 per ETF, $10 per Treasury note). Pending WInS availability check.

## 7. The main risks the firm accepts
- Less upside: model estimate 95th percentile of money left after the reserve $250k.
- A rate fall before January 2027 plus no 2028 deposit leaves part of the 2033 payment unfunded (model estimate $12k after a half-point fall, $31k after a one-point fall).
- No 2028 deposit means no minimum contribution and no stock fund.
