# Firm B

## 1. How the ten payments are protected
The whole portfolio grows until 2033, then buys the reserve. Stocks are about 75% of the portfolio in 2027-2030, 60% in 2031 and 40% in 2032; the rest is intermediate U.S. Treasuries. On 1 January 2033 the firm buys a Treasury ladder covering the ten $50,000 payments (about $395k at today's forward rates).

## 2. What "high certainty" means and how it was checked
High certainty means at least a 95% probability that the portfolio exceeds the reserve's cost in 2033. Checked by a 200,000-path Monte Carlo on JPM's 2026 long-term assumptions (U.S. stocks 6.7% compound, Treasuries 4.0%) and a one-point uncertainty in 2033 interest rates. Model estimate: funded in 96.8% of paths; 86.3% if the 2028 deposit is $75k.

## 3. How growth is pursued and with what money
All of Laura's money pursues growth until 2031. The case says she "has been willing to take thoughtful risks", and a plan running to 2042 gives stocks time to earn their higher expected return. The average stock share over 2027-2032 is 67%. Model estimate: median money left after the reserve is $226k; 95th percentile $540k.

## 4. What Laura can tell co-sponsors in 2031 (range and confidence)
The range is the 25th to 75th percentile of the model's 2033 facility contribution, re-run with 2031 values, stated with 50% confidence. Model estimate from today: $118k to $304k, median $203k.

## 5. Flexibility after 2033
The facility receives 90% of the money above the reserve. The other 10% is kept in Treasury bills for project changes. Reserve bonds are held to maturity.

## 6. What the competition portfolio (WInS) holds
The 2027 allocation: VTI 50%, VEA 15%, VWO 10%, IEF 24%, cash 1%. Four ETF trades at $25 each. Pending WInS availability check.

## 7. The main risks the firm accepts
- Payments depend on markets until 2033: model estimate 3.2% miss, 13.7% if the 2028 deposit is $75k, 40.8% if it does not arrive.
- A stock fall in 2031-2032 lands just before the reserve is bought.
- A one-point rate fall by 2033 adds about $16k to the reserve's cost.
- Model estimate: 5th percentile of money above the reserve $20k.
