# Firm C

## 1. How the ten payments are protected
From 2030 the firm buys Treasuries maturing in 2033-2042 in stages, so the ten-year reserve (about $395k at today's forward rates) is complete on 1 January 2033. Stocks are 65% of the portfolio in 2027-2029, 55% in 2030, 40% in 2031 and 25% in 2032.

## 2. What "high certainty" means and how it was checked
High certainty means at least a 95% probability that the portfolio covers the reserve on 1 January 2033. Checked by a 200,000-path Monte Carlo on JPM's 2026 long-term assumptions (U.S. stocks 6.7% compound, Treasuries 4.0%). The model gives the themed funds the same return and volatility as broad stocks. Model estimate: funded in 98.5% of paths; 89.4% if the 2028 deposit is $75k.

## 3. How growth is pursued and with what money
Of the stock money, 70% is a broad world fund and 30% is split across four themes linked to Laura's public work: social media and the creator economy, Taiwan, clean energy, and an ESG-screened U.S. fund. They carry a values narrative alongside the core. The average stock share over 2027-2032 is 53%. Model estimate: median money left after the reserve is $214k.

## 4. What Laura can tell co-sponsors in 2031 (range and confidence)
The range is the 10th to 90th percentile of the model's 2033 facility contribution, re-run with 2031 values, stated with 80% confidence. Model estimate from today: $65k to $335k, median $182k.

## 5. Flexibility after 2033
The facility receives 85% of the money above the reserve. The other 15% is kept in Treasury bills for project changes. Reserve bonds are held to maturity.

## 6. What the competition portfolio (WInS) holds
The 2027 allocation: VT 45%, SOCL 5%, EWT 5%, ICLN 5%, ESGU 5%, IEF 34%, cash 1%. Six ETF trades at $25 each. Pending WInS availability check.

## 7. The main risks the firm accepts
- Payments depend on markets until the reserve is bought: model estimate 1.5% miss, 10.6% if the 2028 deposit is $75k, 42.6% if it does not arrive.
- Theme funds are narrower and more volatile than the model assumes.
- A one-point rate fall by 2033 adds about $16k to the reserve's cost.
