# Firm F

## 1. How the ten payments are protected
A mean-variance optimiser sets the weights of twelve funds each quarter, subject to a CVaR limit: the worst 5% of outcomes above the reserve must average at least $40k. Dynamic risk parity weights the bond funds. Stocks fall from 55% of the portfolio in 2027 to 20% in 2032. The ten-year reserve (about $395k at today's forward rates) is bought in stages in 2031-2032.

## 2. What "high certainty" means and how it was checked
High certainty means at least a 97.5% probability that the portfolio covers the reserve on 1 January 2033. Checked by a regime-switching Monte Carlo with calm and crisis regimes, calibrated to JPM's 2026 long-term assumptions. Model estimate: funded in 97.5% of paths; 99.7% in a single-regime lognormal model (93.1% if the 2028 deposit is $75k).

## 3. How growth is pursued and with what money
Stock funds carry momentum, quality, minimum-volatility and value tilts. The average stock share over 2027-2032 is 38%. Model estimate: median money left after the reserve is $201k; CVaR (worst 5% average) $42k.

## 4. What Laura can tell co-sponsors in 2031 (range and confidence)
The range is the 5th to 95th percentile of the model's 2033 facility contribution, re-run with 2031 values, stated with 90% confidence. Model estimate from today, by percentile: 5th $59k, 25th $121k, median $171k, 75th $225k, 95th $317k.

## 5. Flexibility after 2033
The facility receives 85% of the money above the reserve. The other 15% is kept and managed by the same optimiser. Reserve bonds are held to maturity.

## 6. What the competition portfolio (WInS) holds
VTI 20%, VEA 10%, VWO 5%, MTUM 5%, QUAL 5%, USMV 5%, VLUE 5%, IEF 15%, TLT 10%, SHY 8%, TIP 6%, GLD 5%, cash 1%. Twelve ETF trades at $25 each. Pending WInS availability check.

## 7. The main risks the firm accepts
- Payments depend on markets until the reserve is bought: model estimate 6.9% miss if the 2028 deposit is $75k, 45.8% if it does not arrive.
- Optimiser weights are sensitive to small errors in expected returns.
- Results depend on the regime model's parameters.
- A one-point rate fall by 2033 adds about $16k to the reserve's cost.
