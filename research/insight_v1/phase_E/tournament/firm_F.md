# Firm F

## 1. How the ten payments are protected
An optimiser (a formula that picks weights from expected returns, volatilities and correlations) resets twelve fund weights each quarter, subject to a loss limit: the worst 5% of outcomes above the reserve must average at least $40k. Bond funds are weighted so each adds equal risk. Stocks fall from 55% of the portfolio in 2027 to 20% in 2032. The ten-year reserve (about $395k at today's forward rates) is bought in stages in 2031-2032.

## 2. What "high certainty" means and how it was checked
High certainty means at least a 97.5% probability that the portfolio covers the reserve on 1 January 2033. Checked by a 200,000-path Monte Carlo on JPM's 2026 long-term assumptions (U.S. stocks 6.7% compound, Treasuries 4.0%) and a one-point uncertainty in 2033 interest rates. Model estimate: funded in 99.7% of paths; 93.1% if the 2028 deposit is $75k.

## 3. How growth is pursued and with what money
Stock funds carry momentum, quality, minimum-volatility and value tilts. The average stock share over 2027-2032 is 38%. Model estimate: median money left after the reserve is $201k; worst-5% average $42k.

## 4. What Laura can tell co-sponsors in 2031 (range and confidence)
The range is the 5th to 95th percentile of the model's 2033 facility contribution, re-run with 2031 values, stated with 90% confidence. Model estimate from today: $59k to $317k, median $171k.

## 5. Flexibility after 2033
The facility receives 85% of the money above the reserve. The other 15% is kept and managed by the same optimiser.

## 6. What the competition portfolio (WInS) holds
The 2027 allocation: VTI 20%, VEA 10%, VWO 5%, MTUM 5%, QUAL 5%, USMV 5%, VLUE 5%, IEF 15%, TLT 10%, SHY 8%, TIP 6%, GLD 5%, cash 1%. Twelve ETF trades at $25 each. Pending WInS availability check.

## 7. The main risks the firm accepts
- Payments depend on markets until the reserve is bought: model estimate 0.3% miss, 6.9% if the 2028 deposit is $75k, 45.8% if it does not arrive.
- Optimiser weights are sensitive to small errors in expected returns.
- A one-point rate fall before the staged purchases adds up to about $16k to the reserve's cost (model estimate).
