# Strategy changes: what the final spec (E6) changes against the current strategy

1. The core stays: the first deposit buys all ten payments before any money takes stock-market risk (brief s8 items 1-8).
2. The facility half changes. In January 2028 the second deposit buys a building minimum equal to its own dollar amount. Everything left goes into one world stock fund held to 2033, and nothing is traded in 2031. This replaces the ~60%-equity sleeve and the 80% lock in 2031.
3. For the TN: the WInS book changes from ~65/35 IEF/TLT/equity to (ii)R (operations hedge, dated building minimum, stock fund). The central idea becomes a split of jobs, and there is a gate before any order.
4. For the IPS: 10 blocks on four dated decisions; certainty defined by price with two named gaps; a capped 2031 range with the chance of reaching the top stated; "fits inside $300k" and "bought in January 2027" dropped as headlines.
5. Every change is a proposal. The team has not voted (CLAUDE.md, VRF), and the gate votes come first (E6 5.4a).

**Serves:** TN (Oct 23) first, then IPS (Nov 6). Research only; the team decides and writes every word.

**Labels** (as in `why_us.md`): VP, VRF, SNIP, ASM, MODEL, INT. "Current" means brief s7 plus CLAUDE.md "Strategy status". Figures cited E6 [n] were re-run by W3 on 2026-09-28 and matched.

## 2. The 10 insights behind the changes (question -> answer -> evidence (status) -> deliverable)

1. **When designs tie in the middle, what chooses?** -> The better bad case, with fewer rules. -> REC p5 $182k against $168k, median $207k against $210k (MODEL, E6 [2]); history worst $169k against $120k (VP data, derived); M237, M238. -> IPS (B4, B7); TN note B.
2. **What job is Laura hiring a firm for?** -> Making promises that no market or future contract can take back: the payments first, then a building minimum from her own earnings. -> Case L43-45, L91-92, L114-116 (VRF); M143, M228 (INT). -> IPS pitch and B1.
3. **Is "high certainty" true, and where is it not?** -> It is bought, not forecast, but with two gaps. The January 2027 price can exceed $300k (~1 in 3 paths). The payments are nominal U.S. dollars against Taiwan costs. -> $294,387 ladder, 19bp headroom (VRF inputs, ASM); 30-34% (MODEL); $33,681 real in 2042 (ASM); M004, M053, M084. -> IPS B2-B4; the pitch avoids date claims.
4. **Why so little stock for a founder who "takes the jump"?** -> The caution is forced by arithmetic, not chosen for her temperament. All money nobody relies on is 100% stocks. -> 97.4-98.1% of the first deposit (MODEL); B1b-Q11 (VP, research only); D13c N1 (INT). -> IPS B5; TN reflection C.
5. **How is the 2031 range set without overpromising?** -> By a rule: bottom = what is owned, top = a market value on the day, capped. Excess stays hers, and the chance of reaching the top is reported. -> P(top) 73% (MODEL); 84% of U.S. two-year periods (VP data, derived); case L110-120 (VRF); M039, M040, M107, M191. -> IPS B7.
6. **What makes this more than the textbook lock-early?** -> The Laura-specific rule: each deposit buys one promise when it arrives. The second deposit's own amount sets the building minimum. -> Case L43-45, L110-112 (VRF); M011 (INT); E4 L6. -> Pitch element 2; TN note B.
7. **What should the WInS book show?** -> Her plan on the first day all three jobs exist, scaled, with the scaling said inside every note and in the IPS. -> E6 5.1; D10 1.5; IPS L75 (VRF); M005, M247. -> TN (all notes); IPS B10.
8. **Which words survive a permanent, un-editable note?** -> One vocabulary: never "match", "promised" or "guaranteed"; "moves like / stands in for". -> 74.7% of TLH matures after 2042 (VRF); case L110, L116 (VRF); M062. -> TN notes; IPS.
9. **How do the three notes show supported, tested and refined honestly?** -> A is tested on a pre-registered window with three numbers. B is refined only if logged. C tells the true growth-first story. -> Growth-first 3.2% / 40.8% miss (MODEL); E2 F8-F9 (INT); TN guide L12, L29-30 (VRF); M002. -> TN reflections.
10. **How dense can a 500-word IPS be?** -> About 12 ideas in 10 blocks, one number, and a paraphrase test. -> E2 counted ~55 ideas in E1 (INT); "over-complex for the insight" (CLAUDE.md, VRF); M036, M235, M234. -> IPS.

## What this teaches

- A good plan is usually improved by removing things, not adding them. Here the payments-first core survived every audit. The parts built around it (a mixed sleeve, a later lock, a model percentile) were replaced by one dated purchase and one fund.
- Rank changes by what is due first and by what a reader can see. A perfect 2033 rule is worth less than notes that do not contradict the IPS.
