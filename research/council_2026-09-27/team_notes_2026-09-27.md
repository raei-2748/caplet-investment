## INVESTMENT PHILOSOPHY
The case is functionally a defined-benefit pension-funding problem from 2033: 10 x $50,000 fixed nominal payments (2033-2042) = fixed nominal annuity liability that must be funded with a high degree of certainty. Therefore use Liability-Driven Investing (LDI).

### LDI toolkit
- DEDICATION (cash-flow matching): buy Treasuries maturing when each payment is due (2033...2042), eliminating market risk.
- IMMUNISATION (duration matching): heading only, not yet written.

## ASSET ALLOCATION / Framework
### 2 clocks
1. Growth clock (2027-2031): no withdrawals; "full license" for growth-tilted, equity-heavy portfolio.
2. Liability clock (2031-2033+): from 2031 (Year 5, co-sponsor conversations start) de-risk toward 2033.
### 3 buckets (inspired by Ashvin Chhabra's Wealth Allocation Framework)
1. Personal risk bucket = operating reserve (capital preservation only).
2. Market risk bucket = facility contribution (reasonable risk).
3. Aspirational risk bucket = flexibility reserve (stays invested for growth; dry powder).
### Portfolio design
- Growth sleeve (2027-2031): diversified global equities with some factor tilt; full risk budget.
- Glide path "Residency Runway" (2031-2033): equity steps down from ~75% in 2027 to 20-25% by eve of 2033.
- Operating reserve "Payment Ladder" (from 2033): cash-flow-matched Treasury ladder, one maturity before each of 10 payment dates; defeasance at individual level; "high degree of certainty" = government credit, duration-matched.
- Tiering (latest version): near payments 2033-2036 locked into actual T-bills/notes/bonds in 2033; 2037-2042 sit in a short-duration bond sleeve and are rolled into the ladder at least 3 years before each payment, to avoid locking today's curve.
