## 3. What the Taiwan sources say about a founder's own money

Main source: 文化部補助藝術村營運扶植計畫作業要點 (MOC directions for supporting artist-village operations), text as
amended 26 Jul 2019. A search snippet says it was amended again on 23 Nov 2023; I could not open that version
(UNVERIFIED). Points cited by number; local copy `src/hccc_7839.txt`.

1. **Own money is a separate, visible line.** The form's columns are "申請經費 自籌經費 總經費" (requested / own /
   total) (form, src:153). VERIFIED.
2. **How reviewers judge.** "重要性及必要性、具體可行性、未來發展特色及潛力、經費編列合理性及計畫之效益性"
   (importance and necessity, concrete feasibility, potential, reasonable budgeting, benefit) (point 9(2), src:76-77).
3. **No minimum own share for private applicants.** A ratio exists only when the applicant is a city or county:
   MOC pays at most 50-90% by fiscal tier (point 6(2), src:36-43), and the local government must budget its share
   before money is paid (point 10(3), src:94-98). So "matching" is built into central-to-local grants, not into
   grants to private groups. This neither supports nor refutes the team's "don't offer to match" note (notes:216-218).
4. **Proof asked for.** Operating status incl. an operating-funds estimate, financial planning, site and building
   use rights, "其他相關單位資源証明" (proof of other parties' resources), registration (point 8, src:51-66). No pledge
   letter or bank proof for the applicant's own money is listed. Whether the online system asks for one: UNVERIFIED.
5. **Currency: NT$.** Stipend "新臺幣五百元" a day, cap "新臺幣三百萬元" per application, "新臺幣五百萬元" per
   applicant per year (points 5(1), 6(1), 13(5)). Context only: NT$3m is about US$94k at 31.82 (FRED, 18 Sep 2026;
   fact_register.md:208). This is not a funding estimate and should not go in a deliverable as one.
6. **One year at a time.** "以單一年度執行完畢為原則"; multi-year (at most 2 years) only after three consecutive
   funded years (point 4, src:19-22). Application window: one month, announced separately (point 7, src:45).
7. **The funder's own money is uncertain.** Amounts await the Legislative Yuan budget; MOC may cut or stop them
   (point 9(5), src:82-83).
8. **What it pays for.** Annual operating plans (stays, exchange, staff training, administration) and equipment;
   not construction (point 5, src:27-32).
9. **Who can apply.** "依法設立或登記之法人、團體或組織" (registered organisations) (point 3). Legal set-up is out of
   scope (case:169); I note it only because it means a founder's money would show up as the organisation's own funds.

Second source: Executive Yuan, 中央政府各機關對民間團體及個人補(捐)助預算執行應注意事項, 6 Dec 2016 text
(`src/ey_tc.txt`). Later amendments (2021, 2024) exist per search snippets; their text UNVERIFIED.

10. **Multi-funder disclosure.** A project asking two or more agencies must list the whole budget and each agency's
    amount; "如有隱匿不實或造假情事，應撤銷該補（捐）助案件，並收回已撥付款項" (hiding or faking: grant cancelled,
    paid money recovered) (point 4(1), src:31-33). Leftover money returns pro rata (4(5)). Grants are published
    quarterly (7(2)).

Context: Taiwan has an artist-village network run for MOC by the Taiwan Art Space Alliance since 2017, whose members
include city-run villages and founder-started studios (On the Move guide, src/otm_taiwan.txt:141-164). One 2021
example (Hsinchu Railway Art Village) lists MOC's heritage bureau as guide, the city as organiser and a private arts
foundation as commissioned operator (`src/h7688.txt:19-22`). That is one example, not a pattern.
