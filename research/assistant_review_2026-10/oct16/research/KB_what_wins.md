## 0. Bottom line (ten points)

1. **The Top 50 is decided on paper, and the IPS is the hinge.** Wharton's rules name the IPS and Final Report for the
   cut [W2]. The IPS has a hard format gate (`competition/official/2026_27/2026_WGY_Investment_Policy-FINAL.txt:82-83`)
   and freezes the strategy (IPS:72-74). Past semifinal rules disqualified "completely new material" [D1][D2][D3].
2. **The odds are long and getting longer.** Top 50 = 3.8% of final reports in 2021-22, 2.1% in 2025-26 (49 of 2,339)
   [N1][N14][W1]. Only about 36-44% of registered teams finish [N6][N11][N14].
3. **Simple beats clever, year after year.** Judges in 2022, 2024 and 2025 and the 2023 champions all said it: "less is
   more", "depth and accuracy", "simple, elegant ideas" [N3][N7][N10][N12]. Wharton's own feedback: tools such as
   Porter's Five Forces "are NOT investment strategies"; a strategy is "a set of rules and guidelines; a plan of attack"
   [D4].
4. **Goal first, portfolio second.** The most common trait of finalists in outside coverage is working backwards from
   the client's goal [O2][O1]. Judges praised "the ability to connect the client's objectives to an investment
   strategy" [N9].
5. **Reasoning that survives questions decides the top places.** Finales are 10 minutes of pitch plus 5 minutes of
   questions (stated for 2023 and 2025) [N6][N13]. A 2025 finale judge said what set managers apart was "clear
   thoughtfulness and reasoning behind all the decisions" [N13].
6. **This year's rubric is new where it matters to Caplet.** The five criterion names are unchanged since at least
   2022-23, but Portfolio Analysis now scores "reasonable assumptions and projections ... under varying market
   outcomes", and Creativity now includes communicating "investment uncertainty clearly and credibly to prospective
   co-sponsors" (`competition/official/2026_27/smapply_public_2026-10-14/deliverables.txt:57,59`; old text [D5]).
7. **The readers include bond experts.** An Aberdeen/abrdn person sat on every finale panel 2022-2026. In 2024-2026
   that person was a fixed-income or credit specialist [N10][N13][N16]. Aberdeen/abrdn asset managers are named as Top 50
   readers in 2021-22, 2022-23, 2023-24 and 2025-26 (2024-25 says only "professional asset managers"), alongside
   "internal reviewers" (to 2024-25) and "Wharton School student evaluators" (2025-26) [N1][N4][N8][N11][N14].
8. **No winning strategy is public.** Wharton says: "We do not release final reports from previous winners" [D8]. No
   article since 2021-22 describes a champion's actual strategy (R1 4.7; R3 P12). Any team-file claim that "the winner
   did X" needs a source.
9. **Non-U.S. teams make the Top 50 often but rarely win.** About 40-51% of each Top 50 is outside the U.S., but all five
   champions from 2021-22 to 2025-26 were U.S. teams [W4]. No Australian team has made a Top 50 since Brisbane State
   High in 2021-22 [N1][N4][N8][N11][N14].
10. **The 16 Oct commit does not change these conclusions, but it adds two risks** (section 7.1): a ladder-cost figure
    on a basis that does not match Caplet's actual holdings, and an AI-suggestion rule that strains the "authentic team
    voice" criterion and makes the team's own AI disclosure template untrue if any AI wording is accepted.

### 2.3 Past rubric, verbatim, and what changed

The old text comes from Wharton's own site database (WordPress page 3778, "Judging and Evaluation", blocks for 2022-23

| Criterion | 2022-23 and 2023-24 (same text; client name changed) | What 2026-27 adds or drops |
|---|---|---|

### 2.4 Semifinal judging (past)

- 2024-25 and 2025-26: judged on the five criteria plus "Written Responses" [D1][D2].
- 2025-26 format: "You have 10 minutes for your team presentation. We will not watch presentations beyond 10 minutes."
  "Teams may include up to 15 slides." "Every team member must have a speaking role and briefly explain how they
  contributed value to the team." "Be a pitch to Connor Barwin, your potential client." Must include "a snapshot of your
  team's recommended investment portfolio" and "a case study of one stock or ETF in your portfolio". Four written
  answers, "250 words or fewer", no graphics. "Do NOT introduce completely new material, or your team will be
  disqualified." Teams "may reflect on strategic weaknesses, limitations, or missed opportunities without changing the
  original strategy." [D1] (re-opened)
- 2022-23 tips: "Slow down!", "Why use 25 words to explain something when three will do?", "Practice your presentation
  with someone that has no prior investing knowledge." [D3] (re-opened).
- 2026-27: "a short presentation (via video conference for the Semifinals ...) ... to a live panel of judges. All team
  members must participate." [W3]. The 2024-25 FAQ said "a 10-minute presentation ... to a live panel of judges" [D7]
  while the real 2024-25 semifinal was a recorded video [D2]. So the only proven wording change is "10-minute" becoming
  "short". The 2027 format is UNVERIFIED.

  creating a portfolio, going backwards. Because before doing the competition I thought that doing investing was just a

answered it is not public (R3). INFERENCE: a liability-matching answer is an expected type of answer, so the edge must
