## 0. The short version

1. **Found and verified: two past Final Report specifications and two past rubrics, word for word.** Wharton's
   website database still holds the 2022-23 (client Peter) and 2023-24 (client Hilary) Deliverables and Judging pages
   [OFFICIAL-STORED]. They give page ranges, required sections, word limits, citation rules and the five criteria.
   None of the earlier assistant reviews I checked (30 Sep, 15 Oct) quote these.
2. **The five criteria have the same names since at least 2022-23.** Only the descriptions changed. The 2026-27
   descriptions are rewritten around Laura. They add "maintains consistency with the team's IPS", "reasonable
   assumptions and projections ... under varying market outcomes" and clear, credible communication to co-sponsors.
   They drop the sector minimum and "visually pleasing presentation of data; many creative elements".
3. **Wharton has never published weights or a points scale.** Every official source lists criteria only. The
   "Final Report AI Judge" in last year's team doc (four "official pillars" with 25/20/20/10/5/10/10 weights) is
   AI-made and contradicts every official source. Do not use it.
4. **The 2026-27 IPS is the direct successor of the 2025-26 Midterm Report.** 2025-26 midterm: title page plus one page,
   maximum 500 words, elevator pitch, "how your strategy is unique", no graphics, no links, TNR 12 double-spaced, and
   "YOU ARE NOT PERMITTED TO CHANGE IT FOR THE FINAL REPORT" [OFFICIAL-COPY]. The 2026-27 IPS reuses the same idea and
   nearly the same sentence: "Your IPS is the foundation of your Final Report and will be evaluated" (official/
   2026_WGY_Investment_Policy-FINAL.txt:72). New this year: trading also stops at the IPS.
5. **The Trading Notes Analysis used to be a section inside the Final Report.** 2022-23: 3 notes on one page. 2023-24: 2
   notes (one stock, one ETF), 150 words each. 2025-26: a "Trading Notes Analysis section of your Final Report"
   [OFFICIAL-COPY]; team docs show 3 notes (ETF, stock, team choice) of about 100 words. In 2026-27 it is a separate,
   early, graded deliverable, and Wharton says "Yes, we will verify this."
6. **The strategy lock is old and strict.** Since 2022-23 the semifinal rules say: "DO NOT introduce completely new
   material or you will be disqualified ... Your team is not permitted to present a partially or entirely new
   strategy." Reflecting on weaknesses is allowed. This year the lock starts at the IPS (6 Nov). Anything the team
   wants to compute in December (reserve rule, 2031 range method, bad-market rule) must already be in the IPS as a
   principle.
7. **Final Report forecast (labelled, section 4).** Very likely: TNR 12, double-spaced, 1-inch margins, PDF of 5 MB or
   less; no external links; a separate APA-style Works Cited that records AI use; a page range where out-of-range
   reports "will not be reviewed" (past ranges: 9-12 pages in 2022-23; 10-13 plus Works Cited in 2023-24); a
   portfolio-at-a-glance section; report-detail sections on strategy, decision-making, growth, challenges and
   takeaways; probably a 150-word advisor reflection. Plus everything the case demands (reserve size and mix, a
   certainty definition, facility contribution, the 2031 range with confidence, part of the fundraising text).
8. **Time is shorter this year.** In 2025-26 the Final Report requirements were posted on 27 Oct for a 12 Dec deadline
   (46 days). This year they come out on Mon 9 Nov for Fri 4 Dec (25 days, with the US clock change on 1 Nov).
9. **Who reads.** 2025-26 Top 50: "Wharton School student evaluators" plus "a professional review panel of asset
   managers from Aberdeen Investments". 49 of 2,339 final reports went through (2.1%). No Australian team in the Top 50
   lists for 2023 to 2026; the last was Brisbane State High in 2022.
10. **Semifinals (if reached).** 2021-22 to 2025-26: a 10-minute recorded video (2025-26: at most 15 slides), every
    member speaks and says how they added value, a case study of "one stock or ETF in your portfolio", then four
    written answers of 250 words or fewer, no graphics. Scored on the five criteria plus "Written Responses". The
    2026-27 FAQ says "a short presentation ... to a live panel of judges"; the 2024-25 FAQ used the same "live panel"
    wording (with "10-minute") while the semifinal was in fact a recorded video, so the FAQ alone does not show a format
    change. The 2026-27 semifinal format is UNVERIFIED until Wharton publishes it.
11. **"Unique but not stupid", in Wharton's own words.** For years Wharton has asked what makes a strategy unique, and
    in the same breath warned: "buy low and sell high" and Porter's Five Forces "are NOT investment strategies"; "Your
    strategy is a set of rules and guidelines; a plan of attack"; "The client profile and his financial goals are also
    sources of creative inspiration"; "less is more"; "hiding behind fancy terms and formulas" is a trap.
12. **Page fit, re-checked with font metrics.** A full 50-word pitch plus 500-word IPS needs about 43 of the 46
    double-spaced lines on IPS pages 2-3. Putting a blank line between five paragraphs pushes it to about 48 lines, so
    it spills onto a fourth page and breaks the "2-page maximum" gate. Use first-line indents, not blank lines, and check
    the exported PDF.

### 1.2 Trading Notes Analysis (due Fri 23 Oct, 5:00 pm ET) [OFFICIAL]

Source: `official/2026_WGY_Trading_Notes_Analysis-FINAL.txt`.
- Purpose: "demonstrate how your team's investment decisions reflected the strategy it developed for the client"
  (lines 4-6).
- "select three investment decisions that best illustrate your team's strategic thinking" (line 11).
- "The focus should be on the quality of your reasoning and the intended role of each decision, not simply whether an
  investment gained or lost value during the competition." (lines 15-16)
- Not expected yet: reserve size, projections, facility contribution, co-sponsor text (lines 20-23).
- Notes need not be for holdings still owned; each reflection must "explain how each decision supported, tested, or
  refined your overall investment strategy" (lines 25-31).
- Example note names a role, a benefit and a risk ("although interest-rate changes may affect its value") (lines 36-39).
- Instructions (lines 42-54): three notes from executed WInS trades; "Include each Trading Note exactly as it appears in
  WInS"; each reflection "100 words or fewer" explaining why, alignment with strategy, and support for the client's
  goals, funding needs or risk considerations; "The Trading Notes must correspond to trades executed in WInS. Yes, we
  will verify this."; submitted "via the SurveyMonkey Apply form".
- What a note itself should hold (Guide:84-86): "the reasoning behind the decision, including its alignment with your
  strategy, the supporting research or analysis, and its expected role in growth, liquidity, risk management, or future
  funding." Also: "Do not treat Trading Notes as an afterthought." (Guide:91)

### 1.3 Investment Policy Statement (due Fri 6 Nov, 5:00 pm ET; trading stops) [OFFICIAL]

Source: `official/2026_WGY_Investment_Policy-FINAL.txt`.
- Two parts: "Investment Strategy Elevator Pitch / Maximum 50 words" and "Investment Policy Statement / Maximum 500
  words" (lines 31-41).
- Content: tailored to goals, horizons and needs; risk and return, liquidity, construction and diversification,
  planned changes; approach to the operating commitment, the reserve's composition over time, a responsible facility
  contribution, flexibility (lines 45-50). "Focus on the strategy and decision-making framework ... rather than
  describing individual investments or presenting detailed financial calculations." (lines 51-52)
- Five "make it clear" points; "You do not need to address these points separately." (lines 53-67)
- Not expected: final reserve numbers, detailed projections, final facility range, co-sponsor draft (lines 68-70).
- "Your IPS is the foundation of your Final Report and will be evaluated." "Your team may not revise its investment
  strategy after the submission deadline." (lines 72-74)
- Gate: "Submissions that do not meet the requirements below will not be considered for semifinal selection." (lines 82-83)
- Page 1 title page: official team name; finalized names, "Format: First Name, Last Initial"; WInS username (lines 84-100).
- Pages 2-3: "2-page maximum" for the pitch and IPS (lines 103-109).
- Times New Roman, 12-point, double-spaced, 1-inch margins, PDF, 5 MB maximum (lines 110-122).
- "Graphics, charts, images, attachments, external links, footnotes, and formal citations are not permitted." Sources
  "must be included in the Final Report." (lines 123-125)

### 1.5 WInS and portfolio rules [OFFICIAL]

- $300,000 virtual cash; the $150,000 is not added (`smapply_public_2026-10-14/trading.txt:22, 26`).
- Trading 28 Sep to 6 Nov, then frozen (trading.txt:24).
- Up to 200 trades; no more than twice a security's current daily volume (trading.txt:40-41).
- Allowed: stocks priced at $5 or more; any ETF on WInS; Treasury bonds on WInS from the US, UK, Germany, France, Italy
  and the Netherlands (trading.txt:43-46). Banned: margin, short selling, stock-secured debt, crypto, derivatives,
  "Anything other than the approved investments listed above" (faqs.txt:46-52).
- "There is no required sector allocation or minimum number of sectors." Diversification may be "across asset classes,
  sectors, industries, market capitalizations, geographic regions, risk characteristics, and funding purposes. The goal
  is to build an intentional mix ... not simply to own a large number of securities." (trading.txt:48-49)
- "Additionally, teams must meet the required trading activity and portfolio management guidelines throughout the
  competition." (faqs.txt:76; rules page `R2_pages/wg_rules_roles.txt:454`). No public 2026-27 page defines these
  guidelines beyond the limits above. UNVERIFIED whether a first-trade deadline exists this year (past years had one:
  14 Oct 2022, 13 Oct 2023, 18 Oct 2024, 10 Oct 2025). Caplet traded on 6-8 Oct, so a similar deadline would be met.
- Commissions $25 per stock or ETF trade, $10 per Treasury trade; errors: "run with it" (faqs.txt:61, 66).

### 1.6 AI and ethics [OFFICIAL]

- Advisors "may not make decisions on behalf of students" (line 414). Paid advisors and non-Wharton courses that claim
  to teach the competition are banned (rules page, Advisors section).

### 3.1 How Wharton defines "unique" (verified), and the guardrails

- Uniqueness has been asked for directly in most years: 2015-16 ("What sets you and your strategy apart from other
  teams?"), 2022-23 and 2023-24 final reports (75 words on "what makes your investment strategy unique compared to other
  teams"), 2022-23 and 2023-24 midterms, 2025-26 midterm ("Must explain how your strategy is unique").
- Wharton's definition of a strategy: "a set of rules and guidelines; a plan of attack" with "triggers" and "a
  strategic thread" (2023-24 feedback).
- Where creativity should come from: "The client profile and his financial goals are also sources of creative
  inspiration" (2025-26 midterm). The 2024 semifinal judge praised teams that linked the strategy to the client's own
  interests ("a lot of your creativity resonated").
- Guardrails: tools are not strategies; jargon does not impress; no long, meandering explanations; one explainable idea
  beats many; depth and accuracy beat volume (sources in 2.3).
- Laura's own profile is about storytelling ("Laura Gao believes stories have the power to change how people see the
  world", Client_Profile.txt:12). [INFERENCE] A plain, visual explanation in the Final Report (graphics are allowed
  there, not in the IPS) is a client-grounded way to be distinctive, provided every visual carries real data and passes
  the "uses data effectively" test. Gimmicks with no numbers would fail it.
- Also verified and usable: Wharton tells teams to "operate by" the CFA Institute Asset Manager Code (rules page
  line 562). Its disclosure standard reads: "Ensure that disclosures are truthful, accurate, complete, and understandable"
  (`R2_pages/cfa_asset_manager_code.txt:277-278`), and performance must not be misrepresented (line 268-269). This lines up
  with the case's warning about overpromising to co-sponsors and with keeping WInS P&L out of projections.

### 3.2 Page-fit check for the IPS (re-verified)

Method: `oct16/research/R2_tools/fit_est.py`. Times New Roman metrics (Liberation Serif is metric-compatible), 12 pt,
Word "Double" spacing (27.6 pt per line), 1-inch margins on US Letter, so 23 lines per page and 46 lines for pages 2-3.
Filler words were taken from the official Guide (average 5.8 letters per word), split into five paragraphs.

| Layout | 50 + 450 words | 50 + 500 words | 50 + 540 words |
|---|---|---|---|
| First-line indents, no gap between paragraphs | 40 lines | 43 lines | 46 lines |
| Blank double-spaced line between paragraphs | 45 lines | 48 lines (spills to page 4) | 51 lines |

Limits: Google Docs and Word measure "double" spacing slightly differently, and A4 paper gives fewer words per line but
more lines. The only reliable test is the exported PDF. The 15 Oct brief gave "about 41 of 46 lines (not re-verified)";
this estimate (43) is consistent with it.

Context: the 2025-26 midterm said "Maximum 500 words" and "1 page only" with double spacing [OFFICIAL-COPY]. By the same
metrics one double-spaced page holds roughly 280 words, so the page, not the word cap, was the binding limit last year.
This year Wharton gave two pages for 550 words, which fits only if the layout is tight.
